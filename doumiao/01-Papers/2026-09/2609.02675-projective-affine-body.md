## 摘要

Multibody systems have widespread applications in diverse fields such as robotics, entertainment, and animation. Their capability to model and simulate intricate interactions among interconnected bodies makes them invaluable in various domains. However, complexity arises with factors like non-smooth contact handling, nonlinearity in flexible joints, and parallelization challenges.

We introduce a **stable and highly parallel GPU algorithm within affine body dynamics** for solving constrained multibody dynamics with nonlinear constraints.

## 核心创新点

### 解决方案
1. **变分形式重构**: 将约束多体动力学重新表述为变分形式
2. **Peridynamic Bonds**: 将系统视为通过 peridynamic bonds 连接的仿射体集合
3. **统一模型**: 提供仿射体动力学、约束和接触的统一模型
4. **半隐式求解**: 使用半隐式逐次代换法求解非线性优化
5. **无全局 Hessian**: 无需组装全局 Hessian，适合 GPU 高效实现

### 技术优势
- ✅ 高度并行 GPU 算法

- ✅ 实时模拟能力
