## 摘要

Simulating large-scale free-surface water by coupling a localized 3D fluid solver to a cheaper 2D surface model has long faced a mismatch in wave dynamics: efficient 2D wave models used in graphics are typically either linear or non-dispersive. These models are fast, simple, and accurate for calm, small-amplitude seas, but coupling them with strongly nonlinear 3D solvers produces visible reflections and artifacts at the 2D--3D interface. 

We address this problem by introducing a **nonlinear and dispersive 2D wave model based on the canonical Zakharov formulation**. Its Hamiltonian structure, in which the surface elevation and surface potential form a canonical pair (η, ψ) governed by the wave energy, enables a canonically consistent two-way coupling scheme, allowing information to pass smoothly across the 2D--3D interface.

## 核心创新点

### 问题背景
1. 大规模自由水面模拟需要耦合 3D 流体求解器和 2D 表面模型
2. 传统 2D 波模型（线性/非色散）与非线性 3D 求解器不兼容
3. 2D-3D 界面产生可见反射和伪影

### 解决方案：Zakharov 公式
- **哈密顿结构**:水面高度 η 和表面势能 ψ 形成规范对
- **canonical pair**: (η, ψ) 受波能量控制
- **双向耦合**: 信息在 2D-3D 界面平滑传递

### 性能指标
| 指标 | 数值 |
|------|------|
| 波高误差降低 | **1.7-5×** (相比 SWE, BEM, Airy) |
| 相比 BEM 加速 | **>10³×** |
| 完整系统加速 | **>4×** (相比纯 GPU NB-FLIP) |

## 技术方法

### 1. 非线性色散 2D 波模型
```
基于 Zakharov formulation:
H = ∫(ω|η|² + |∇ψ|²) dA  (哈密顿量)
∂η/∂t = δH/δψ
∂ψ/∂t = -δH/δη
```

### 2. 2D-3D 耦合策略
- Canonical pair (η, ψ) 确保界面一致性
- 信息双向平滑传递
- 消除界面反射伪影

### 3. 3D Navier-Stokes 集成
- 与 3D NS 求解器耦合
- Kelvin 尾流测试验证
- 色散匹配实验
