### 1. Spatiotemporal FLIP

**标题**: Spatiotemporal FLIP for Fast Free-Surface and Two-Phase Simulation With Very Large Time Steps

**来源**: SIGGRAPH 2026 Technical Papers  
**奖项**: Honorable Mention

**摘要**: Extends the widely used Fluid-Implicit Particle (FLIP) method to enable very large time steps for free-surface and two-phase simulation.

**技术方向**:
- FLIP 方法扩展
- 大时间步长
- 自由表面
- 两相流
