## 技术方案

### 核心思想

Large language models (LLMs) can improve task performance during learning, yet these gains do not
necessarily reflect learners' ability to thereafter apply the knowledge and skills independently. We
present AI Cosmos, an agentic infrastructure for situated learning that organizes learning around
two complementary principles: scenario-based participation and cumulative assets of practice. AI
Cosmos organizes learners and role-specialized agents within learning scenarios that integrate task-
relevant knowledge and, when needed, executable workbenches. An asset-memory loop preserves the
processes and outcomes of practice as reusable assets with explicit provenance and dependencies. We
evaluate scenario-based interaction through two preregistered randomized controlled studies
examining the acquisition of legal reasoning and programming competence, holding the same underlying
LLM across conditions. AI Cosmos achieves greater learning gains than a conventional LLM in legal
reasoning and higher mastery of unseen programming problems than an LLM-as-Teacher baseline.
