## 核心要点
1. 在**可变解析树拓扑的合成上下文无关文法**中研究学习动力学。
2. **分阶段学习曲线完全可解释**：所有损失阶段 (a) 可解析计算、(b) 可清晰对应到**隐藏潜在树变量**的表示。
3. 探针可视化 inside-outside 计算**沿深度与训练过程**的涌现：**早期阶段只使用局部（bigram/trigram）信息且仅涉及最初几层**。
4. 随后 Transformer 发展出**编码器-解码器结构**——**最抽象的潜在信息编码在中层**，**后半网络用于逐步精化最终 token 预测**。
5. 作者指出这一组织与真实 LLM 的发现相似，但**在合成模型上成立，因而为理论进展打开了门**。

## 摘要（原文）
Variability in syntactic structure and parsing ambiguity are fundamental aspects of natural language
which modern LLMs have to learn to deal with proficiently. Previous "post-mortem" studies have shown
that fully trained Transformers indeed approximate canonical parsing algorithms such as inside-
outside, but little is established theoretically about how this is actually learned. Here, we make a
first step towards addressing this by studying the learning dynamics in synthetic context-free
grammars with variable parse tree topology. The stagewise learning curves are fully interpretable,
in the sense that all of the loss stages a) can be computed analytically, and b) can be clearly
associated to the representation of a hidden latent tree variable, which allows to lift ambiguity
and utilize information from wider contexts. By probing representations, we visualize the emergence
of inside-outside computation across depth and during training: while during early stages local
(bigram/trigram) information is used and involves only a few initial layers, gradually the
Transformer develops an encoder-decoder structure where the most abstract latent information is
encoded in the middle, and the latter half of the network is used to progressively refine the final
token prediction. This organization of computation is reminiscent of findings in real-world LLMs,
and importantly applies here to a synthetic model which allows full analytical control of the data
statistics, thus opening the door to theoretical progress.
