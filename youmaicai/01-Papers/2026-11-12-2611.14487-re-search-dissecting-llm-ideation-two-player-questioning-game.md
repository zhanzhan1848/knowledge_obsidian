## 核心要点
1. **Re-Search 把「二十问」改造成研究构想的诊断基准**：只给模型一篇已发表论文的研究问题，模型必须通过不断提出 yes/no 问题、由 oracle 返回二元反馈，重新发现论文的核心想法，从而把** ideation（构想）与 execution（执行）分离**测量；基准由 **200 篇 ICML 2026 论文**构建。
2. **搜索进展高度前置且很快触顶**：在 **6 个近期 LLM** 上，100 个问题中**几乎全部进展发生在前 30 个问题内**，这 30 问基本决定了游戏结局，之后多数模型进入平台期。
3. **瓶颈在"生成"而非"判断"**：通过对候选问题和假设空间做受控干预，发现模型**被提供好想法/好问题时能识别出来，但常常无法自行生成**；失败模式为**过度挖掘单一方向**或**过度探索过多方向**。由此推导出 autoresearch 系统的设计原则——既然模型"会评不会产"，**向搜索中注入外部来源的想法与问题可能比提升单个模型的生成能力更有效**。

## 摘要（原文）
Research ideation is iterative: researchers refine their ideas step by step based on feedback. Yet
existing evaluations obscure this search process, making it difficult to understand how LLMs explore the
space of research ideas and what limits their progress. We introduce Re-Search, which adapts the format of
Twenty Questions to research ideation: given only the research problem of a published paper, a model must
rediscover the paper's core idea by asking yes-or-no questions. Built from 200 ICML 2026 papers, Re-Search
tracks how a model's idea evolves as it asks questions and receives binary oracle feedback, isolating ideation
from execution. Across six recent LLMs, nearly all progress happens within the first 30 of 100 questions,
which largely decide how the game ends; after that, most models plateau. Through controlled interventions on
the candidate questions and the hypothesis space, we find that the bottleneck is generation, not judgment:
models recognize a good idea or a good question when one is offered, but often cannot produce it on their
own. We further analyze the ideation traces and find that models fail by either over-exploiting one direction
or over-exploring too many. These findings point to concrete design principles for autoresearch systems: since
models can judge candidates they cannot produce, enriching the search with externally sourced ideas and
questions may be more effective than improving any single model's generation ability.
