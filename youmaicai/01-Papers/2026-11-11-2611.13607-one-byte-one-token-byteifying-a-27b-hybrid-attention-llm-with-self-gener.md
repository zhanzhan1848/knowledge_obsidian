## 核心要点
1. 动机：**Byte-level BPE 碎片化中文文本**（Qwen3.8 在中文维基上平均 3.84 字节/token，64% 的 token 是单字符）并对模型隐藏拼写。
2. 方法：从 **Qwen3.8-27B（48 层 Gated DeltaNet + 16 层全注意力）**出发，**词表从 248,320 缩到 384**，**其余权重完全不动**，两阶段课程（先编码后阅读）在 **4.77 GB 监督字节**上继续训练 **178.6 小时（8×H200）**。
3. **全部对话/行为监督由原模型自己 on-policy 生成**（字符词典、词词典、通用对话、编码会话），知识阅读用百科文本（占监督字节 54%）。
4. 同 GPU 同服务栈下：MATH-500 / HumanEval+ / MMLU-Pro / 非思考 GSM8K 的差距**落在配对 bootstrap 区间内**；C-Eval、思考模式 GSM8K、非思考 IFEval 掉 4–6 点。
5. 非思考模式下**在所有字符级任务上领先**（CUTE 75.2 → **84.0**，EXECUTE-zho 81.6 → **93.9**，中文字符套件 54.4 → **64.0**），但**汉字读音、笔画数一类查表知识下降且思考模式救不回来**。
6. 代价是吞吐：**每步速度相同但产出少 3–5 倍**，中文每秒字符数约为原模型 **1/4**，**64K 字节 prefill 慢 6.5 倍**。
7. 记录四种字节模型失效模式（greedy runaway、采样破坏 UTF-8、小词表惩罚项失效、重复）及修复。

## 摘要（原文）
Byte-level BPE fragments Chinese text (for Qwen3.8 on Chinese Wikipedia, 3.84 bytes per token on
average; 64% of tokens are single characters) and hides spelling from the model. We ask whether an
already-trained 27B subword model can be converted directly into a pure byte-level model (one byte,
one token), without a patching structure or a local encoder, while keeping most of its ability.
Starting from Qwen3.8-27B (48 Gated DeltaNet layers + 16 full-attention layers), we shrink the
vocabulary from 248,320 to 384 entries (the 256 byte symbols already in the BPE vocabulary plus
special ids), leave every other weight untouched, and continue training in a two-stage curriculum
(encoding first, then reading) on 4.77 GB of supervised bytes: all dialogue/behavioral supervision
is generated on-policy by the original model itself (character dictionary, word dictionary, general
dialogue, coding sessions), and knowledge reading uses encyclopedia text (54% of supervised bytes).
Training takes 178.6 h on 8xH200. On the same GPU and serving stack, the byte model's gap to the
original falls within the paired bootstrap interval on MATH-500, HumanEval+, MMLU-Pro and non-
thinking GSM8K, and it loses 4-6 points on C-Eval, thinking-mode GSM8K and non-thinking IFEval.
Without thinking it leads on every character-level task (CUTE 75.2 -> 84.0, EXECUTE-zho 81.6 ->
93.9, our Chinese character suite 54.4 -> 64.0), but lookup knowledge about Chinese characters such
as pronunciation and stroke count drops, and thinking does not recover it. The cost is throughput:
per-step speed is identical, but each step yields 3-5x less output; Chinese characters per second is
about 1/4 of the original, and prefill at 64K bytes is 6.5x slower. We also document four byte-model
failure modes (greedy runaway, sampling breaking UTF-8, ineffective penalty terms on a small
vocabulary, repetition) and their fixes.
