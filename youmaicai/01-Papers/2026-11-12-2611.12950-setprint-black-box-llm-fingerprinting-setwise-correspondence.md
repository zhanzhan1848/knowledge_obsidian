## 核心要点
1. 问题设定为**黑盒 LLM 指纹溯源**：只能观测目标模型对输入查询的输出响应，判定目标模型是否派生自某个源模型。做法是扰动每个查询，观察两个模型的响应变化，得到两组对应的响应变化集合。
2. 指出现有方法未充分利用两类互补信息：**集合内部的变化间关系（within-set relations）**与**两个集合之间的变化对应关系（between-set correspondence）**。SetPrint 的核心机制是**免训练（training-free）**地联合捕捉二者：先把每个响应变化表达为**相对于所属集合均值变化**的相对量，再比较**由同一扰动诱发**的两个模型的变化。
3. 在 **LeaFBench** 上，SetPrint **一致优于**既有的免训练黑盒指纹方法；进一步消融分析把性能增益归因于 within-set 关系与 between-set 对应关系**两方面均有贡献**。

## 摘要（原文）
Black-box LLM fingerprinting aims to determine whether a target model was derived from a source model
when only the output responses to input queries are observable. One way to reveal this relationship is to
perturb each query and observe the resulting responses of both models, yielding two corresponding sets of
response variations. These sets provide two complementary sources of information: the relations among
response changes within each set, and the correspondence of response changes between the two sets.
Existing approaches, however, have not fully exploited both. We introduce \textbf{SetPrint}, a training-free
method that jointly captures both within-set relations and between-set correspondence. SetPrint expresses
each response change relative to the mean change of its own set, and then compares changes from the two
models that were induced by the same perturbation. On LeaFBench, SetPrint consistently outperforms existing
training-free black-box fingerprinting methods. Further analyses attribute these gains to both within-set
relations and between-set correspondence.
