| **摘要** | The bottleneck of attention mechanisms, which are the fundamental building block of modern language models, is their quadratic complexity in terms of context size. There has thus been an increasing interest in the compression of the context, also called KV-cache compression. In this article, we consider the problem of compressing the context of attention mechanisms by first formulating it in the Hilbert space associated with its kernel. In this formalism, attention can be represented as the ratio of two linear maps whose interpolant is also the ratio of interpolants, this allows us to consider the interpolation of the linear maps directly. We thus introduce two novel KV-cache compression methods which we call GAIM and Q-DAIM, based on the Generalized Empirical Interpolation Method (GEIM) and the Q-Discrete Empirical Interpolation Method (Q-DEIM), respectively. As the cost of these methods is quadratic in terms of context length, we propose surrogate models based on the Taylor series, the Performer and the Random Fourier Features method which allow the compression to be done in linear time. Finally, we extend this result to the streaming attention setting, providing new methods which allow one to penalize the selection of interpolation nodes based on the previously interpolated context and to update the previous interpolation based on new information. We then provide numerical results on the convergence of the residuals and the output error in future queries and preliminary results on the LongBench-E benchmark showing performance comparable to the state-of-the-art methods. These experiments suggest that the range revealed by our Q-DAIM method could help identify where information is concentrated in the KV cache, thus enabling an efficient compression. |

## 核心贡献

1. **理论视角创新**：不直接工程化地压缩 KV cache，而是**先在其核对应的 Hilbert 空间中形式化问题**。
2. **关键定理式观察**：在该形式下，注意力可表示为**两个线性映射之比**，其插值子仍是插值子之比 —— 这使得**可以直接对线性映射做插值**。
3. 提出两种 KV 压缩方法：**GAIM**（基于广义经验插值法 GEIM）与 **Q-DAIM**（基于 Q-离散经验插值法 Q-DEIM）。
4. **效率补救**：由于原方法在上下文长度上成本为二次，提出基于 **Taylor 级数、Performer 与 Random Fourier Features 的代理模型**，实现**线性时间**压缩。
5. **扩展到流式注意力（streaming attention）**：新方法允许**基于已插值的上下文来惩罚插值节点的选择**，并**基于新信息更新先前的插值**。

### 关键发现

- **这是今天唯一一篇"先把问题重新形式化再谈工程"的论文**，其 Hilbert 空间视角与另外两篇效率论文（树索引、隐空间聚类）的工程视角形成鲜明对比 —— 这三条路线代表了效率研究的三种品味。
- **"注意力 = 线性映射之比，其插值子仍是插值子之比"** 是本文最漂亮的一步：它把"压缩 KV cache"这个组合问题转化成了**可以对线性映射直接做插值**的代数问题。
- **流式扩展里的"惩罚节点选择"** 是一个很实际的设计：不是事后判断节点好坏，而是**在选择时就依据已有上下文做惩罚** —— 这与 [[daily-2026-11-01]] 那批"效率结论由实现而非架构决定"的发现一脉相承。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.42112-subquadratic-attention-with-a-hierarchical-tree-structured-indexer]] / [[2026-11-02-2610.42147-latent-sparse-attention-towards-subquadratic-ultra-long-context-attentio]] 对比：ZHA/LSA 保**注意力表达力**，GAIM/Q-DAIM 保**核形式化下的插值性质** —— 后者可能损失更多但理论基础更扎实。
