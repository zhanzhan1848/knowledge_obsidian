## 核心要点
1. 指出现有上下文压缩检查只比较 reader 模型的**答案**或**最可能的词**，因此漏掉 **silent evidence loss**——**关键证据被移除但答案不变**，于是答案失去支撑，且对复用该上下文的**其他模型**仍构成风险。
2. SELD 的原理：**证据丢失时 reader 模型对「它没说出口的答案」的偏好会改变，即使其答案不变**；比较压缩前后的这些偏好即可揭示答案检查看不到的丢失。
3. **免训练、免标签**。配套 **SEL-Pairs** 基准：来自三个 QA 数据集的**长度匹配**证据删除与对照删除。
4. 五个 reader 模型上：在**答案未变的匹配对中识别出移除证据的编辑 83%**，最强基线 **70%**。

## 摘要（原文）
Context compression is now routine for language models with long inputs, but a compressed context is
useful only if it keeps the evidence for the answer. Existing checks compare the reader model's
answer or its most likely words, and thus miss a frequent failure, silent evidence loss: key
evidence is removed while the answer stays the same, leaving the answer unsupported and risky for
other models that reuse the context. To address this challenge, we propose SELD (Silent Evidence
Loss Detector), which needs no training and no labels. Its principle is that when evidence is lost,
a reader model's preferences among the answers it leaves unsaid shift even if its answer does not,
so comparing these preferences before and after compression reveals losses that answer checks cannot
see. We also build SEL-Pairs, a benchmark of length-matched evidence and control deletions from
three question-answering datasets. Quantitative and qualitative experiments with five reader models
on it show that SELD consistently outperforms strong and recent baselines across compressors,
compression rates and models, identifying the evidence-removing edit in 83% of matched pairs with
unchanged answers, against 70% for the strongest baseline. These results show that evidence loss in
compressed contexts can be detected even when the answer is unchanged.
