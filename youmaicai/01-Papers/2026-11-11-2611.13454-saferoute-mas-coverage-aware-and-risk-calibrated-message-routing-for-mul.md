## 核心要点
1. **SafeRoute-MAS**：覆盖感知、风险校准的消息路由程序，**仅用整合前的信息**决定**保留全部专家消息或移除一条**。
2. 在 **5,328 个委员会**的封存缓存上：源兼容工作点 **+1.80 pp**（95% CI [+1.13, +2.52]），同时移除 **12.76%** 的消息 token。
3. 在公开 gpt-oss-120b 端点上做 **4,580 次受控整合**（分层 458 委员会样本）：样本估计 **−0.09 pp**；按原工作负载做设计加权得 **+0.94 pp**（95% CI [−1.31, +3.50]）与 **18.72%** 的 acted-input 缩减。
4. **加权的 token 匹配比较同样未确立选择器优势**（+1.68 pp，CI [−1.27, +5.10]）。
5. 88 个缓存非中性标签中**仅 13 个（14.8%）**在重复调用多数下保持方向。加权后期望损坏 UCL 为 4.60%，但 11 个预设边际中**仅 2 个**通过支持/风险规则。
6. 作者因此提出一个**带 Keep-All 回退的数值限定**，此处为回顾性应用而非前瞻性验证。

## 摘要（原文）
We introduce SafeRoute-MAS, a coverage-aware, risk-calibrated procedure that keeps all specialist
messages or removes one using only pre-integration information. On a sealed cache of 5,328
committees, its source-compatible operating point gains +1.80 percentage points (95% CI [+1.13,
+2.52]) while removing 12.76% of message tokens. We also run 4,580 controlled integrations over a
stratified 458-committee sample on a public gpt-oss-120b endpoint. The sample estimate is -0.09
points; design weighting to the original workload gives +0.94 points (95% CI [-1.31, +3.50]) and
18.72% acted-input reduction. A weighted token-matched comparison likewise does not establish a
selector advantage (+1.68 points, CI [-1.27, +5.10]). Only 13 of 88 cached non-neutral labels
(14.8%) retain direction by repeated-call majority. Expected-corruption UCL is 4.60% after
weighting, but only 2 of 11 prespecified marginals pass the support/risk rule. We therefore propose
a numerical qualification with Keep-All fallback, applied here retrospectively rather than validated
prospectively.
