## 核心要点
1. 指出医学 MCQ 的核心难点：**选项共享重叠证据**，只有少数决定性因素能区分正确与错误选项。
2. 批评既有方法：检索/推理方法关注**提供宽泛相关知识**或**引出推理路径**，而非**识别区分混淆选项的紧凑差异证据**。
3. 提出检索接地的**差异证据**框架：抽取**选项特定**的差异证据片段，组织成简洁证据 prompt 用于选项选择。
4. 跨多个数据集与目标 LLM 取得一致准确率提升；消融显示优于直接使用检索证据块与闭源 LLM 生成的一般提示。
5. 提升**不能完全由「答案邻近引导」解释**，与「差异证据作为对竞争选项推理的紧凑辅助」一致。

## 摘要（原文）
Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly studied for clinical decision support, and medical
question answering provides a testbed for evaluating this capability. In particular, medical
multiple-choice questions (MCQs) reflect a core challenge in clinical decision-making: plausible
options often share overlapping evidence, while only a few decisive factors distinguish the correct
option from incorrect ones. However, existing retrieval-based and reasoning-oriented methods mainly
focus on supplying broadly relevant knowledge or eliciting reasoning paths, rather than identifying
the compact differential evidence that separates confusing alternatives. In this work, we present a
retrieval-grounded differential evidence framework for medical MCQs. Our method extracts option-
specific pieces of differential evidence and organizes them into concise evidence prompts for answer
selection. This design better aligns external knowledge with differential reasoning while reducing
irrelevant context. Experiments show consistent accuracy gains across multiple datasets and target
LLMs. Ablation studies further show that our framework outperforms both directly using retrieved
evidence chunks and closed-source LLM-generated general hints, and that its improvements are not
solely explained by answer-proximal guidance, but are consistent with differential evidence serving
as a compact aid for reasoning over competing alternatives. Our findings also offer insights for
medical reasoning and other decision-making settings with multiple possible choices.
