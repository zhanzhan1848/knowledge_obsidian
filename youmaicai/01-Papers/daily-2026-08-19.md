## 值得关注的技术点

### GxP-Agent
- DAG拓扑编码领域知识而非依赖LLM推理
- 15节点domain-specific worker agents
- Claude Sonnet 4.6在FDA标准上首次达100%

### Aegis
- "Model proposes; trusted runtime decides"原则
- Fail-closed under uncertainty
- Senate-style settlement for non-unilateral authorization
- 2100行Aegis-governed评估：0风险副作用

### PlanPO
- 成功 ≠ 同样成功（绕路成功vs高效成功）
- Coarse-to-fine advantage signals
- GRPO+27.2%平均提升

### CASE Decodability
- Decodability = gate能否正确排名候选（leakage-free）
- r=0.75预测selection vs voting提升
- 决策阈值 near AUC=0.60

### TileMix
- Tile-centric精度路由，bitmask打包
- FP16/INT8双路径共享online-softmax
- 支持GQA和INT8 KV cache

### SIRLM
- 推理证据感知漂移(reasoning evidence perception drift)
- SIRG: 结构关系记忆 + KG tokenizer + 神经符号推理器
- 可集成SFT和GRPO
