## 核心要点
1. 多教师 on-policy 蒸馏通常给每个样本分配域教师，但**更新共享的学生参数**。DRD-MOPD 指定**每个域的样本更新哪些参数组件**。
2. 学生 = 冻结基座 + 可训练组件（初始化为 expert-minus-base 权重），**所有组件参与每次前向**；**四种更新模式**定义样本域与组件的对应，各有独立优化器状态。
3. 用角度、投影系数与能量测量**原专家几何的保持度**。
4. 关键数字：**Open-MOPD 已发布最终 checkpoint 的增量能量有 97.02% 落在原专家方向张成的空间之外**；DRD-MOPD 的 Own checkpoint 经 20 次更新后**沿每个组件原方向的系数仍接近 1**，两两夹角 **88.04°–92.41°**，导出的 BF16 学生**外部能量仅 4.90%**（对比保存的 FP32 组件之和的 10.52%）。
5. 达到 **32.72%** 宏平均（六基准），对比初始 task-arithmetic merge 的 32.44%。

## 摘要（原文）
Multi-teacher on-policy distillation assigns each sample a domain teacher but typically updates
shared student parameters. We introduce Domain-Routed Delta Optimization (DRD-MOPD), which specifies
which parameter components each domain's samples update. The student is the sum of a frozen base and
trainable components initialized from expert-minus-base weights. All components participate in every
forward pass; four update modes define the correspondence between sample domains and components,
each with independent optimizer states. We analyze these update rules and measure retention of the
original expert geometry through angles, projection coefficients, and energy. Prior work such as
Open-MOPD distills directly from the base model; 97.02% of its released final checkpoint's increment
energy lies outside the span of the original expert directions. In comparison, our Own checkpoint
after 20 updates retains coefficients near one along each component's original direction and
pairwise angles ranging from 88.04 to 92.41 degrees; its exported BF16 student's increment has 4.90%
outside energy, versus 10.52% for the sum of saved FP32 components. The same checkpoint achieves a
32.72% macro score across six benchmarks, compared with 32.44% for its initial task-arithmetic
merge. These measurements describe geometry and capability at the evaluated training stages and
settings.
