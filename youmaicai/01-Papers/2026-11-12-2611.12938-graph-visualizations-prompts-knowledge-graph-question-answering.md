## 核心要点
1. **受控评测设计**：在**固定的 KGQA pipeline** 内，用 **1,500 个 WebQSP 与 CWQ 问题**、**6 个 VLM**，在**逐问题粒度**上比较纯文本 prompting 与"文本 + 图可视化"prompting，并分析性能变化与**推理复杂度、模型规模、图像布局**的关系。
2. **图像带来平均 +1.85 F1，且增益主要来自低复杂度问题的精度**（低复杂度问题精度 **+4.65 F1**）。混合效应与回归分析显示：**多模态增益不强烈依赖模型规模**，但**对图布局高度敏感**——纯图像设置性能急剧下降，**层次化 Graphviz 布局优于更松散的渲染**，而把大子图**分解为彼此分离的路径**带来最大提升。
3. **提出自适应视觉 prompting 策略**：只为**规模小、视觉稀疏的子图**且**布局结构良好**时追加示意图，从而在准确率与推理效率上同时优化，避免在复杂查询上引入**视觉冗余与 token 开销**；并把结论关联到**视觉符号设计理论**，为后续多模态 VLM prompting 指出原则化路径。

## 摘要（原文）
We study when and how multimodal input improves knowledge graph question answering (KGQA) in large
vision--language models. Using a controlled evaluation over 1,500 WebQSP and CWQ questions and six VLMs
within a fixed KGQA pipeline, we compare text-only prompting against text-plus-graph visualization at the
per-question level and analyze performance changes as a function of reasoning complexity, model size, and
image layout. Adding a graph image yields an average gain of 1.85 F1 points, driven by strong precision
improvements on low-complexity questions (+4.65 F1). Mixed-effects and regression analyses show that these
multimodal gains do not depend strongly on model scale, but are highly sensitive to graph layout: image-only
settings sharply degrade performance, hierarchical Graphviz layouts outperform less organized renderings, and
decomposing large subgraphs into separated paths yields the largest improvements. Taken together, the results
indicate that visual prompting primarily aids KGQA by enhancing prediction precision in low-complexity
contexts, provided that visual design respects cognitive and perceptual constraints. To optimize both accuracy
and inference efficiency, we argue for adaptive visual prompting policies that selectively append diagrams to
small, visually sparse subgraphs with well-structured layouts, avoiding unnecessary visual clutter and token
overhead on complex queries. We conclude by relating these findings to a visual notation design theory,
outlining how principled diagram encoding can guide future multimodal VLM prompting.
