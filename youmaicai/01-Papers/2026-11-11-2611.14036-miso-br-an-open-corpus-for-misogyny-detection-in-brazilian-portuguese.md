## 核心要点
1. **MISO-BR**：巴西葡语厌女检测的开放、有文档、版本化语料，**896 条**平衡社媒文本，二元厌女标注，附构建协议与可复现基线。
2. 构建：**词典与目标引导的 X 抓取** + 从 OLID-BR 导入 + **LLM 分诊阶段**（发布 prompt 与解码参数）+ 三名标注者按书面指南人工标注。
3. **Fleiss' κ = 0.788**（816 条）。
4. 单一固定协议下比较四个经典分类器（Multinomial/Bernoulli 朴素贝叶斯、线性 SVM、随机森林）与 **BERTimbau Base/Large**。经典模型聚集在 **0.651–0.676 macro-F1**（偏精确、召回差）；transformer 达 **0.833 macro-F1 与准确率**（Wilson 95% CI [0.77, 0.88]），约 **16 点**差距。
5. **Base 与 Large 在测试集上不可区分**，尽管训练成本差 **2.6 倍**。
6. 作者强调资源价值**不在头部分数而在管线的可审计性**，并列出三条限制：**人工类别平衡**（其对真实流行率下精确率的影响已量化）、**来源与标签的关联**（发布的 source 字段使其可测量）、**词典引导采样与自动分诊引入的选择偏差**。

## 摘要（原文）
Misogyny is among the most prevalent forms of online hate speech directed at Brazilian users, yet
open and auditable resources dedicated to it in Brazilian Portuguese remain scarce. We introduce
MISO-BR, an open, documented and versioned corpus of 896 balanced social media texts annotated for
binary misogyny, released with its construction protocol and reproducible baselines. The corpus
combines lexicon- and target-guided scraping from X with items imported from OLID-BR, a large
language model triage stage whose prompt and decoding parameters are released, and manual annotation
by three annotators following a written guide. Inter-annotator reliability reaches a Fleiss' kappa
of 0.788 over 816 items. Under a single fixed protocol we compare four classical classifiers
(Multinomial and Bernoulli Naive Bayes, a linear SVM and a random forest) with fine-tuned BERTimbau
Base and Large. The classical models cluster between 0.651 and 0.676 macro-F1 and are precision-
oriented but recall-poor. The transformers reach 0.833 macro-F1 and accuracy (Wilson 95% CI for
accuracy [0.77, 0.88]), a gap of roughly 16 points, while Base and Large are indistinguishable on
the test set despite a 2.6x difference in training cost. We argue that the value of the resource
lies less in its headline scores than in the auditability of its pipeline, and we state three limits
that users must reckon with: the artificial class balance, whose effect on precision under realistic
prevalence we quantify; the association between provenance and label, which the released source
field makes measurable; and the selection bias induced by lexicon-guided sampling and automatic
triage. The corpus, annotation guide, models and experimental scripts are publicly available.
