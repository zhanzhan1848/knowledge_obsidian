## 核心要点
1. 提出**可执行基准 DEPUTYBENCH**：**79 个基础条目、375 个实例**，配有**配对的权限上下文**与**分级证据要求（graded evidence requirements）**，用于评测企业级 agent 能否判断常规请求是否被组织记录与政策所允许，同时不打断正常业务。
2. 在 **12 个模型**上做修订审计，汇总**安全性 88.0%**、**配对成功率 79.1%**；受控研究用 **28 个派生任务 × 6 个模型**测试**授权事实翻转、证据遮蔽、无关改写**三种扰动。结果显示：结果确实跟随后端权限，但**证据缺失时触发受限执行的比例（10.3%）显著高于显式否认权限时（3.5%）**，差 6.8 个百分点（95% 区间 1.9–13.0，次要分析）。
3. 事后分析在 4 个隐私任务中定位到 **61/100** 次遮蔽条件下的执行，这些任务需要完整累计计数或个人级同意；剔除最易错模型或贡献最大的任务后差异依旧存在，但**在这 4 个任务之外差异大幅缩小**。结论要点：**有证据时的高安全性并不等于权限无法核实时能可靠停止**。无关改写下配对成功率在预设 5 分容差内等价；评分器验证从 **284 个留出反例中正确判分 178 例提升到 266 例**；两位安全专家用两周人工核验任务 ground truth，但**阶段标签仍由 LLM 生成**。

## 摘要（原文）
Enterprise agents must determine whether ordinary requests are permitted by organizational records and policies
while continuing legitimate work. We introduce DEPUTYBENCH, an executable benchmark with 79 base items, 375
instances, paired permission contexts, and graded evidence requirements. Across 12 models, revised audits give
88.0% pooled safety and 79.1% paired success. A controlled study of 28 derived tasks and six models tests
authorization-fact flips, evidence masking, and irrelevant rewrites. Outcomes track backend permissions, yet
restricted execution is more frequent with missing evidence than explicit non-permission: 10.3% versus 3.5% on
27 matched tasks, a 6.8-percentage-point difference (95% interval: 1.9-13.0; secondary analysis). A post-hoc
analysis locates 61 of 100 masked-condition executions in four privacy tasks requiring complete cumulative
counts or person-specific consent. The difference persists when the most error-prone model or the largest
contributing task is excluded, but is much smaller outside those four tasks. Thus high safety with available
evidence does not establish reliable stopping when permission cannot be verified. Mean paired success is
equivalent within a prespecified five-point margin under irrelevant rewrites, while strong-model safety remains
near ceiling. Scorer validation improves from 178 to 266 correctly scored cases out of 284 held-out
counterexamples. Two security experts manually verified task ground truth over a two-week period; stage labels
remain LLM-generated. The concentration analysis describes task structure and audited outcomes, not an
identified internal mechanism.
