## 核心要点
1. 任务：**字符级幻觉片段检测**，覆盖英、法、意、中四种语言。
2. 架构：**冻结 Gemma-4-12B-it + 约 10.7M 参数 LoRA 适配器**。
3. 关键技术点：因骨干是因果解码器，**单次拷贝的答案无法 attend 后续 token**；作者**在同一个 prompt 中重复候选答案并从第二次出现处提取特征**，从而在一次非自回归前向中给每个位置**近似双向上下文**。
4. 字符级定位器结合中间隐藏状态预测幻觉概率、BIO 边界与响应级弃答分数；另有语言特定分类器为每个预测片段指派五类幻觉之一。
5. 闭测集上字符级宏平均 IoU **0.4404**，**是官方基线（0.1854）的两倍多**，四语言 IoU 排名第 5–6。
6. 消融：**重复答案带来的增益与 LoRA 适配相当**；检测器**依赖图像-答案配对**（打乱图像把 IoU 打到「什么都不预测」的地板或更低）。

## 摘要（原文）
We present our system for the SHROOM-visions 2026 shared task on character-level hallucination span
detection in image-conditioned vision-language model (VLM) responses, evaluated across English,
French, Italian, and Chinese. The detector is built on a frozen Gemma-4-12B-it backbone with
trainable LoRA adapters (approximately 10.7M parameters). Because the backbone is a causal decoder,
hidden states read from a single copy of an answer cannot attend to subsequent tokens; we therefore
repeat the candidate answer within one prompt and extract features from its second occurrence,
giving every position approximate bidirectional context in a single non-autoregressive pass. A
character-level locator combines intermediate hidden states to predict hallucination probabilities,
BIO boundaries, and a response-level abstention score, while separate language-specific classifiers
assign one of five hallucination types to each predicted span. On the closed test set, our system
reaches a macro-averaged character IoU of 0.4404, more than doubling the official baseline (0.1854),
and ranks 5th-6th on IoU across the four languages. On the English tuning split, controlled
ablations attribute a gain to the repeated answer that is comparable to that of LoRA adaptation (its
held-out gain is smaller and concentrated on hallucinated answers), and they show that the detector
relies on the image-answer pairing: shuffling images across examples drives IoU down to the level of
the predict-nothing floor or below it in every language. In deployments where the same
Gemma-4-12B-it model also generates answers, its base weights can be reused, making the approach
practical for local question-answering systems that cannot run a separate verifier.
