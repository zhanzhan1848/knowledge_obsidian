## 技术方案

### 核心思想

KV cache quantization is well studied for autoregressive language models, where cached prefix states
are exact under causal attention and quantization is mainly a cache reconstruction problem.
Diffusion language models (dLLMs) break this assumption. Fast dLLM inference reuses KV states across
denoising steps, but bidirectional token representations can change as masked tokens are revealed,
so the reused cache may already differ from full recomputation before quantization is applied. We
study KV cache quantization under this cache-reuse error. We adapt KIVI and KVQuant to Fast-dLLM-
style prefix and dual cache reuse, and introduce TRACE and dLLM-KVQ. TRACE reconstructs feasible
dLLM reveal trajectories from prompt-answer pairs, providing calibration states that match iterative
masked decoding. dLLM-KVQ uses these trajectories to choose per-layer K-cache entries that remain in
high precision according to output- and attention-level behavior metrics, while applying standard
low-bit value quantization. Across LLaDA and Dream evaluations on GSM8K, HumanEval, MATH, and MBPP,
dLLM-KVQ achieves the highest average score among quantized methods across the reported 4-bit and
2-bit prefix/dual cache settings.
