## 核心要点
1. **LandslideFM**：首个连接视觉感知任务与**滑坡情境解释**的统一基础模型。
2. **Source-Agreement Graduated Trust** 半监督学习框架利用有限标注 + 大量未标注数据适配多视觉任务。
3. 预测的滑坡区域为经 **PEFT 适配的 VLM** 提供**显式空间引导**，实现完整的滑坡推理与场景理解。
4. 配套 **LandslideVL**：首个智能滑坡分析基准，结合视觉图像与新引入的文本 prompt。
5. 跨公开数据集与 LandslideVL 的定量与定性表现均优；**在专业滑坡推理与场景分析上超越通用 VLM**。代码：`github.com/wangshanwen001/LandslideFM`。

## 摘要（原文）
Intelligent landslide detection is essential for disaster prevention. However, existing models
primarily focus on isolated visual tasks, require extensive labeled data, and lack deep analytical
and scene-understanding capabilities. To address these limitations, we propose LandslideFM, the
first unified foundation model that connects visual perception tasks with contextual interpretation
of landslides. Specifically, LandslideFM adapts to multiple visual tasks via a Source-Agreement
Graduated Trust semi-supervised learning framework that leverages limited labeled data alongside
abundant unlabeled data. Furthermore, predicted landslide regions provide explicit spatial guidance
to a Vision-Language Model adapted through parameter-efficient fine-tuning, enabling comprehensive
landslide reasoning. To support training and evaluation in this domain, we also present LandslideVL,
the first benchmark for intelligent landslide analysis, combining visual imagery and newly
introduced textual prompts. Comprehensive experiments across public datasets and LandslideVL
demonstrate that LandslideFM achieves superior quantitative and qualitative performance. Most
importantly, LandslideFM surpasses general-purpose VLMs in specialized landslide reasoning and scene
analysis. Our code is released at https://github.com/wangshanwen001/LandslideFM.
