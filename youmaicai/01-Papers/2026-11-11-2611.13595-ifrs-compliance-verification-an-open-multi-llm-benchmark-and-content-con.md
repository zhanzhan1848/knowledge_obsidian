## 核心要点
1. **IFRS 合规验证**：专家需将披露与数百个细粒度监管条款交叉比对——繁重、昂贵、易错，推动 LLM 作为合规助手。
2. 指出**朴素集成易受系统性逐模型偏差拖累**，可能使聚合准确率低于最强单标注者。
3. **新基准**：**19,998 个 IFRS 条款-披露对**，含 **11,161 个多 LLM 一致标签**与 **8,837 个人工标注**（在 LLM 分歧的条目上）。
4. 框架耦合**三阶段领域适配句子嵌入管线**（SEC EDGAR 文件上的 BERT 掩码语言建模、无监督 TSDAE 与 SimCSE 适配、监督对比对齐）与**学习式集成分类器**（融合冻结嵌入与来自 **GPT-4.1、Claude Sonnet 4、Gemini 2.5 Pro** 的 LLM 概率特征，经 **TPE 优化的梯度提升树**）。
5. 在分歧集上集成就 **81.7% 加权 F1**，超过最强单 LLM **10.4 点**、多数投票 **14.6 点**，并超过十一个无监督聚合基线；检索管线在未见条款上 **Recall@15 = 45.3%**。

## 摘要（原文）
Verifying corporate financial disclosures against the International Financial Reporting Standards
(IFRS) is a core auditing task in which experts cross-reference disclosures with hundreds of fine-
grained regulatory paragraphs: a laborious, costly, and error-prone process that motivates growing
interest in large language models (LLMs) as compliance assistants. Yet single-model accuracy remains
insufficient for high-stakes use, and naive ensembling is vulnerable to systematic per-model biases
that can drag aggregate accuracy below that of the strongest individual annotator. This gap is
addressed with a multi-stage framework, supported by a new benchmark of 19,998 IFRS regulation-
disclosure pairs comprising 11,161 unanimous multi-LLM labels and 8,837 human annotations on items
where the LLMs disagreed. The framework couples a three-stage domain-adapted sentence embedding
pipeline (BERT masked language modeling on SEC EDGAR filings, unsupervised TSDAE and SimCSE
adaptation, and supervised contrastive alignment) with a learned ensemble classifier that fuses
frozen embeddings and LLM probability features from GPT-4.1, Claude Sonnet 4, and Gemini 2.5 Pro via
Tree-structured Parzen Estimator (TPE) optimized gradient-boosted trees. On the disagreement set,
the ensemble achieves 81.7% weighted F1, exceeding the strongest single LLM by 10.4 points and
majority voting by 14.6 points, and outperforms eleven unsupervised aggregation baselines; the
retrieval pipeline attains Recall@15 of 45.3% on unseen regulations. The dataset, model checkpoints,
annotation platform, and classifier implementation will be publicly released.
