## 技术方案

### 核心思想

World models can achieve strong control performance without faithfully capturing the dynamics of
their environment, limiting their potential for mechanistic interpretability and scientific
discovery. Here, we introduce world2vec to learn mechanistic world models without reward information
directly from observations and actions. World2vec learns latent dynamics that faithfully reproduce
the true system over long prediction horizons, with at least one order of magnitude fewer parameters
than DreamerV3, TD-MPC2 and LeWM. This enables state of the art performance in controlling
continuous environments from pixels and accurate system identification across a benchmark of 71
attractors, demonstrating how world models are amenable to scientific discovery.
