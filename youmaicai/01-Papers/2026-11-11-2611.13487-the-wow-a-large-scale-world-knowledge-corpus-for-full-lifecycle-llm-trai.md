## 核心要点
1. **WoW（Wo**rld Kno**w**ledge）**：面向基础模型**全周期训练**的大规模中文世界知识语料。
2. 构建由**教育知识图谱**驱动，作为分类骨架，系统收集并补全各教育层级缺失的模块。
3. 相比前代版本：**系统性与质量提升、规模更大**，并引入**多模态信号**，支持模型开发的每一个阶段。
4. 实验显示 WoW 在多个基准上显著提升模型性能。
5. 提供**图式数据选择与检索的交互式平台**以促进社区研究。

## 摘要（原文）
Existing Chinese educational corpora often suffer from fragmented coverage and a lack of systematic
organization. In this paper, we propose WoW (\textbf{Wo}rld Kno\textbf{w}ledge), a large-scale
dataset designed for the full-cycle training of foundation models. The construction of this dataset
is driven by an Educational Knowledge Graph, which serves as a taxonomic backbone for the systematic
collection and completion of missing educational modules across all levels. Compared to previous
versions, this iteration provides enhanced systematicity, superior quality, and a larger scale,
while incorporating multimodal signals and supporting every stage of model development. Experiments
demonstrate that WoW significantly improves model performance across diverse benchmarks. To
facilitate community research, we further provide an interactive platform for graph-based data
selection and retrieval.
