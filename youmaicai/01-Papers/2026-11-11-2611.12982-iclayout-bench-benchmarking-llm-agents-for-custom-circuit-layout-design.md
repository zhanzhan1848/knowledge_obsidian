## 核心要点
1. **ICLayout-Bench**：agent 使用 **EDA 工具**从晶体管级网表构造并优化 **GDSII 布局**。
2. 评估用**物理验证、寄生参数提取与布局后仿真**；通过物理与功能检查的布局按电气性能与面积评分。
3. **165 个任务跨 13 个 PDK**，技术节点从 **3nm 到 180nm**，涵盖模拟、混合信号与数字电路（开源与商用 EDA 流）。
4. **22 种配置、7 个模型、21 个公开 + 19 个私有任务**；公开任务平均分 **4.22–80.26**（满分 100），私有任务 **1.43–67.01**。
5. **更高的推理投入并不一致地提高平均分**，且**分数相近的配置成本可相差极大**。

## 摘要（原文）
Large language model (LLM) agents are being explored for circuit design, but their ability to
produce high-quality custom layouts has not been extensively evaluated. ICLayout-Bench is a
benchmark in which agents use electronic design automation (EDA) tools to construct and optimize
GDSII layouts from transistor-level circuit netlists. These layouts are evaluated using physical
verification, parasitic extraction, and post-layout simulation. Layouts that pass the physical and
functional checks are scored on electrical performance and area. The benchmark includes 165 tasks
across 13 process design kits (PDKs), covering technology nodes from 3 nm to 180 nm. The tasks span
analog, mixed-signal, and digital circuits in open-source and commercial EDA flows. The experiments
evaluate 22 configurations spanning seven models on 21 public and 19 private tasks. Mean layout
scores range from 4.22 to 80.26 on public tasks and from 1.43 to 67.01 on private tasks, on a
100-point scale. Higher reasoning effort does not consistently improve mean scores, and
configurations with comparable scores can incur substantially different model costs.
