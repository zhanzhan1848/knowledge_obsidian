## 核心要点
1. 提出面向车联网（CAV）的边缘编排框架，**联合决策**三件事：模型切分点、逐层量化位宽、执行节点部署位置；针对静态切分推理无法适配时变链路带宽、负载与多流并发流量的痛点。
2. 将「切分 + 混合精度分配」建模为**混合整数线性规划（MILP）**：在端到端时延与任务质量约束下最小化计算与通信能耗。
3. 在 Jetson Orin Nano（8GB）、Orin NX（16GB）、AGX Orin（32GB）上用 OPV2V 多流负载（K∈{2,4,6,8}）评测：相对 Uniform INT8 切分，**V2V 通信能耗降低 33.3%–36.0%**；K=8 时端到端完成时延在 Orin Nano 降低 34.0%、Orin NX 降低 33.1%，而 3D 检测精度相对 FP16 仅下降 3.1–4.1 个 mAP50 百分点。

## 摘要（原文）
Deploying vision transformer models across connected and automated vehicles (CAVs) requires joint management of
computation and Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication. Static split-inference methods use fixed partition
points and cannot adapt to time-varying link bandwidth, processing load, and concurrent multi-stream traffic.
This paper proposes an edge orchestration framework that jointly selects model partition points, per-layer
quantization bitwidths, and execution placement across heterogeneous vehicular compute nodes. The joint
partitioning and mixed-precision allocation problem is formulated as a Mixed-Integer Linear Program (MILP)
that minimizes compute and communication energy subject to end-to-end latency and task-quality constraints.
The framework is evaluated on Jetson Orin Nano (8~GB), Jetson Orin NX (16~GB), and Jetson AGX Orin (32~GB)
nodes using OPV2V-driven multi-stream workloads with $K \in \{2,4,6,8\}$. Across the evaluated configurations,
the proposed formulation reduces V2V communication energy by $33.3$--$36.0\%$ relative to a Uniform INT8
split. At $K=8$, end-to-end task completion latency is reduced by $34.0\%$ on the Orin Nano and $33.1\%$ on
the Orin NX relative to the same baseline, while limiting the reduction in 3D detection accuracy to
$3.1$--$4.1$ percentage points in $\mathrm{mAP}_{50}$ relative to FP16 execution.
