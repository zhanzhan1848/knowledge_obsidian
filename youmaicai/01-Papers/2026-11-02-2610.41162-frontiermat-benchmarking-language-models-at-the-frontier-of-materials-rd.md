# FrontierMat：材料研发前沿的语言模型基准

| **摘要** | To accelerate materials discovery, language models must be able to address problems at the research frontier. Yet textbook- and examination-derived benchmarks primarily assess established knowledge, leaving performance on frontier research problems largely untested. We introduce FrontierMat, a benchmark of 330 problems drawn from frontier academic and industrial research in fields including aerospace, semiconductors, embodied intelligence and nuclear fusion. Every problem is validated by leading experimentalists and industry experts in its field. Each problem presents a real study and asks the model to infer a withheld outcome or draw a conclusion from selected experimental evidence. Problems are annotated by functional domain, material system and the scale of the governing mechanism; 130 require cross-scale reasoning. The strongest general-purpose model scores only 53.7%, and on matched problems, cross-scale problems score 8.7 points below single-scale ones. Half of the problems are solved by at most one model, and models fail chiefly by endorsing what is plausible rather than what was observed. A domain-adaptation study with meta-weighted continued pretraining and scale-modular self-distillation raises an open-weight model from 36.7% to 55.4%, matching the strongest proprietary model, with improvements on public materials benchmarks and no decline in aggregate performance on general benchmarks. FrontierMat provides a testbed for measuring progress toward language models that support frontier materials R&D. |

## 核心贡献

1. **动机**：教科书与考试衍生的基准主要评估**已确立的知识**，而**前沿研究问题**上的表现基本未被测试。
2. 提出 **FrontierMat**：**330 个问题**，来自航空航天、半导体、具身智能、核聚变等领域的**前沿学术与工业研究**；**每个问题都由该领域的顶尖实验学家与行业专家验证**。
3. **任务形式**：呈现一项**真实研究**，要求模型**推断一个被隐去的结果**，或**从选定的实验证据中得出结论**。
4. **标注维度**：功能域、材料体系、**支配机制的尺度**；其中 **130 个问题需要跨尺度推理**。
5. **关键结果**：
   - 最强通用模型仅 **53.7%**
   - 匹配题目上，**跨尺度问题比单尺度低 8.7 分**
   - **一半的问题最多只有一个模型能解出**
   - 模型**主要失败模式是"认可看似合理的而非实际观察到的"**（endorsing what is plausible rather than what was observed）
6. **领域适应研究**：**元加权持续预训练（meta-weighted continued pretraining）+ 尺度模块化自蒸馏（scale-modular self-distillation）**，将一个开放权重模型从 **36.7% → 55.4%**，**匹配最强专有模型**，且在公开材料基准上有提升、**汇总性能无下降**。

### 关键发现

- **"认可看似合理的而非实际观察到的"** 是本文最有价值的失败模式刻画 —— 与 [[daily-2026-11-01]] 那批"表面成功 vs 真实机制"主线完全同构：**模型的失败不是不会推理，而是把先验当成了证据**。
- **"一半问题最多一个模型能解出"** 说明这个基准确实触及了前沿而非能力下限。
- **36.7% → 55.4% 的领域适应**（**+18.7 分**）且**汇总性能无下降**，是领域专门化仍保持通用能力的少见证据。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.42147-latent-sparse-attention-towards-subquadratic-ultra-long-context-attentio]]（超长上下文）形成上下游：**FrontierMat 的"从实验证据推断"正是超长上下文推理的天然测试场景**。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41710-advancing-clinical-trial-outcome-prediction-with-domain-specific-large-l]]（临床试验预测）对照：那篇的结论是"**没有任何模型超过平凡分类器**"，本篇的结论是"**最强模型只有 53.7%**" —— 两篇共同说明**垂直领域的自由文本仍未被 LLM 真正攻克**。

## 局限性

- 330 题规模偏小，分层后的统计功效有限
- 专家验证为一次性，题目时效性会衰减
- 领域适应方法在公开材料基准上的收益未给具体数字
- 基准可能快速被用于训练而失效（污染风险）
