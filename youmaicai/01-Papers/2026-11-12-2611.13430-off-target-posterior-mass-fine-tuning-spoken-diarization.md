## 核心要点
1. 针对**口语语言 diarization（Spoken Language Diarization, SLD）**任务，指出其建模流程为"预训练语音模型提特征 → 聚类"，而微调预训练语音模型虽能带来性能提升，**收益幅度却依赖于数据集**；因此需要一个**无需训练**就能**提前预测微调收益**的诊断指标。
2. 提出 **OTPM（Off-target Posterior Mass）**：基于**后验语言识别概率（posterior language identification probabilities）**计算而得的诊断指标，用于**预测（forecast）SLD 微调后可获得的性能增益**；其关键优势是**training-free**——无需真的执行微调即可评估候选模型。
3. 实证结果显示 OTPM 与探针（probe）增益呈**正相关**：在**未见过的合成双语音料**上相关系数 **0.56**，在**真实语料**上为 **0.23**，表明 OTPM 作为 SLD 的诊断指标具有可观潜力（真实场景相关性明显更弱是该方法的局限）。

## 摘要（原文）
Spoken Language Diarization (SLD) models provide frame-level language information of a speech recording. SLD
modeling pipeline includes feature extraction from pre-trained speech models followed by clustering. Fine-
tuning pre-trained speech models has shown performance gains for SLD with its magnitude being dataset
dependent. Off-target Posterior Mass (OTPM), computed based on posterior language identification probabilities
is proposed as a diagnostic metric to forecast performance gains from SLD fine-tuning. OTPM obtains positive
correlation with probe gains, 0.56 on unseen synthetic bilingual data and 0.23 on real-world corpora. These
results indicate promising potential of OTPM as a diagnostic metric for SLD.
