## 核心要点
1. 指出 **EDA 工作流高度碎片化**，需工程师协调异构表示、专用软件工具与跨设计阶段的迭代验证。
2. **ChipOmni**：知识增强、上下文演进的 agent 框架，整合多模态理解、动态规划、多 agent 协作与**验证驱动的工具交互**。
3. 通过持续纳入外部工程工具的反馈维持**演化的任务上下文**，整合领域知识，迭代精修中间设计。
4. 跨模拟电路、RTL、testbench 与形式断言生成基准，多项任务特定指标超过所评估基线，并**解出全部 30 个 AnalogCoder-Pro 任务**。
5. 长程案例：把自然语言 RISC-V 规格推进**三个设计层级**到**通过物理 sign-off 的 micro-SoC GDSII 布局**，**初始化后无需人工干预**。

## 摘要（原文）
Electronic design automation (EDA) workflows remain highly fragmented, requiring engineers to
coordinate heterogeneous representations, specialised software tools, and iterative verification
across multiple design stages. Although large language models have brought more general reasoning
and code-generation capabilities to EDA, many existing approaches remain focused on isolated tasks
and struggle to plan, verify, and repair designs autonomously within executable engineering
environments. Here we introduce ChipOmni, a knowledge-enhanced, context-evolving AI agent framework
for EDA. ChipOmni integrates multimodal understanding, dynamic planning, multi-agent collaboration,
and verification-driven tool interaction into a unified autonomous system. By continually
incorporating feedback from external engineering tools, the framework maintains an evolving task
context, integrates domain knowledge, and iteratively refines intermediate designs. Across
benchmarks for analog circuit, RTL, testbench, and formal-assertion generation, ChipOmni improves
multiple task-specific measures over the evaluated baselines and solves all 30 AnalogCoder-Pro
tasks. In a long-horizon case study, ChipOmni advances a natural-language RISC-V specification
through three design levels to a micro-SoC GDSII layout that passes physical sign-off, without human
intervention after initialization. Together, these results show that ChipOmni can coordinate
verification-driven design across heterogeneous tasks and sustain long-horizon engineering
workflows, advancing a shift from fragmented, tool-centred processes towards agent-centred, closed-
loop systems.
