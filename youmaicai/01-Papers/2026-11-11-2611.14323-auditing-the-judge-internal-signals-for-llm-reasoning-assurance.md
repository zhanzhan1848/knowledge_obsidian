## 核心要点
1. 把 judge 的**连续裁决 token 分数**与一个**从同 prompt 的 attention head 激活训练的监督正确性探针**对比。
2. 三个 7–12B 模型在 GSM8K 与 MATH 上：探针在 **5% 审查预算**下多找出 **1.3–22.5 pp** 的错误（**6 个设定全部成立**，中位数 7.4 点）。
3. **跨数据集迁移降低每个模型的探针 AUROC**（两个方向都是）；由于数据集、权重精度与生成预算**同时改变**，这测的是**联合协议偏移**而非纯数据集效应。
4. 10% 路由时，探针引导的分配在两个 GSM8K 实验中优于等成本随机分配，但**仅对较弱的 judge** 优于 judge 引导路由。
5. 结论很克制：内部信号能改善**监督的、同域的**预算分配，**但不是域通用的置信度估计器**。

## 摘要（原文）
Language-model systems use self-judgment to decide which answers to release, review, or recompute.
Because intervention is costly, an assurance score must rank errors well at the available budget,
not merely over the full ranking. We compare the judge's continuous verdict-token score with a
supervised correctness probe trained on attention-head activations from the same prompt. Exact
identities link fixed-budget error recall to residual risk and show that AUROC averages recall over
all budgets. Across three open 7-12B models on GSM8K and MATH, the probe finds 1.3-22.5 additional
percentage points of errors at 5% review in all six model-dataset settings (median 7.4 points). At
10% routing, probe-guided allocation improves accuracy over equal-cost random allocation in both
GSM8K experiments, but beats judge-guided routing only for the weaker judge. Cross-dataset transfer
lowers probe AUROC for every model in both directions relative to target-domain training. Because
dataset, weight precision, and generation budget change together, this measures a joint protocol
shift rather than a dataset-only effect. Internal signals can improve supervised, in-domain budget
allocation, but are not domain-general confidence estimators.
