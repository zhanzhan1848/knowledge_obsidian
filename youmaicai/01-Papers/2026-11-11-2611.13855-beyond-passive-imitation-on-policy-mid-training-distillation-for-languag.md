## 核心要点
1. 指出持续预训练（CPT）的标准 NTP 目标**把每个语料 token 当作固定目标，不管它对当前模型是否有用**。
2. 受控研究（同一 **10 亿 token 数学语料**）：**NTP 提升 Llama-3.2-3B 的数学表现，却显著降低 Qwen3-1.7B-Base**——**同一训练序列在固定配方下有模型依赖的效果**。
3. **OPMD** 把中期训练从离线文本模仿重写为**主动的、查询驱动的知识获取**：在选定 anchor 位置从**演化中的学生策略**采样轨迹，施加来自冻结教师的**稠密 token 级 reverse-KL 奖励**，在**模型实际访问的状态**上训练而非只在 ground-truth 前缀上。
4. 这减小了 teacher-forced NTP 的**训练-生成错配**。
5. 在 MATH-500 与 Minerva 上，OPMD 相对 Qwen 学生的基座有提升，并**超过所评估的 NTP 基线（后者在两个基准上都退化）**。**无额外推理开销**，可集成进 CPT 管线。

## 摘要（原文）
Continual pre-training (CPT) adapts large language models (LLMs) to specialized domains, yet its
standard next-token prediction (NTP) objective treats every corpus token as a fixed target,
regardless of its utility to the current model. In a controlled study on the same 1B-token
mathematical corpus, we observe sharply different outcomes across base models: NTP improves
Llama-3.2-3B's performance on mathematical benchmarks but substantially degrades Qwen3-1.7B-Base's
performance, indicating that, under a fixed training recipe, the same training sequences can have
model-dependent effects. To address this problem, we introduce On-Policy Mid-Training Distillation
(OPMD), which reformulates mid-training from offline text imitation into active, query-driven
knowledge acquisition. At selected anchor positions, OPMD samples trajectories from the evolving
student policy and applies dense token-level reverse-KL rewards from a frozen teacher, training the
model on states it actually visits rather than only on ground-truth prefixes. This on-policy
objective reduces the training-generation mismatch of teacher-forced NTP. On MATH-500 and Minerva,
OPMD improves the Qwen student over its base performance and outperforms the evaluated NTP baseline,
which degrades on both benchmarks. OPMD integrates into CPT pipelines without added inference-time
overhead and offers a model-aware alternative to passive domain adaptation.
