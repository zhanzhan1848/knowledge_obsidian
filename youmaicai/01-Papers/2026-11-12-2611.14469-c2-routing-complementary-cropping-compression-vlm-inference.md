## 核心要点
1. **关键观察：裁剪与压缩是互补的**。**裁剪（cropping）**擅长突出**小目标与局部细节**，而**整图压缩（whole-image compression）**更好地保留**大目标或分布式证据所处的上下文**；因此单一缩减策略难以覆盖不同场景，而**单纯限制视觉 token 上限**虽能降计算，却可能丢弃回答问题所需的信息。
2. **方法设计**：提出 **C$^2$-Routing**，**依据输入图像与问题自适应地在裁剪与压缩之间选择**；由于两种策略都可能丢关键信息，进一步设计**安全门（safety gate）**，**利用原始输入与缩减输入下的预测联合正确性分布**来引导**回退（fallback）**，以保护准确率。
3. **实验与收益**：为适配消费级 GPU，**全部训练与评测在 RTX 3090 上进行**；基线使用**同样 1,024 视觉 token 上限的整图基线**，以隔离"超出 token 截断"的增益。在 **3 个 VLM、18 个数据集**（覆盖局部细节、全局场景、场景文本与文档）上，**在视觉预算已被截断的前提下**，C$^2$-Routing **平均再降低端到端 FLOPs 36.53%、视觉 token 42.36%**，而**任务分数平均相对下降仅 0.77%**。

## 摘要（原文）
Visual Question Answering (VQA) imposes substantial computational overhead on vision-language models (VLMs),
motivating model-agnostic input reduction methods that can be readily applied across diverse VLMs, including in
black-box settings. Simply enforcing a visual-token cap reduces computation, but it can discard information
needed to answer the question. A single reduction strategy also struggles to accommodate different scenarios. Our
key observation is that cropping and compression are complementary: cropping highlights small targets and local
details, whereas whole-image compression better preserves context for large targets or distributed evidence. Based
on this observation, we propose C$^2$-Routing, which adaptively selects cropping or compression according to the
input image and question. Since both strategies may still discard key information, we further design a safety gate
that uses the predicted joint correctness distribution under original and reduced inputs to guide fallback and
protect accuracy. To accommodate consumer GPU resource constraints, all training and evaluation experiments run
on RTX 3090 GPUs. To measure gains beyond token capping, we use full-image baselines with the same cap of 1,024
visual tokens. We conduct extensive evaluation on three VLMs and 18 datasets spanning local details, global
scenes, scene text, and documents. With the visual budget already capped, C$^2$-Routing further reduces
end-to-end FLOPs by 36.53% and visual tokens by 42.36% on average, with a mean relative task-score decrease of
only 0.77%.
