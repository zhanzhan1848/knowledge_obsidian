## 核心要点
1. **核心论点：饱和主要来自"度量方式"而非"任务难度"**。作者重新打分 **8 个基准**（含 SWE-bench Verified、Terminal-Bench 以保证广度）已公开的运行结果，发现多数 agent 基准把一次 run 压缩成**单个 pass/fail 位**，从而既**过粗**又**被参考值封顶**。
2. **两种失效方式**：过粗——SWE-bench Verified 上相邻的前十名 agent 之间的差距**小于其测试用例的误差量级**，修正误差对邻居的重排效果**不超过随机删掉每个 agent 六分之一的通过用例**；封顶——ALE-Bench 前十名按比赛分数区分，但当以**人类中位数**为基准评分时**全部并列**，尽管**近一半的运行超过了该人类**。
3. **解决方案**：在固定任务上"提高门槛"只是把问题平移，**分级给分（graded credit）才能真正修正**。作者推导出一个**带日期锚点（dated anchors）的截断对数比率分数（clipped log-ratio score）**，并把**成本作为独立轴**；在 Terminal-Bench 的一次重跑中，被 pass/fail 判定为并列的模型在**测试所丢弃的那个数量指标上相差超过十倍**。

## 摘要（原文）
Data-science and ML-engineering agents are evaluated on benchmarks such as MLE-bench, ALE-Bench,
SWE-fficiency and AlgoTune, whose tasks yield a quantity (a Kaggle metric, contest score or speedup).
When one stops separating the best models, the usual remedy is a harder version. Re-scoring released runs
of eight benchmarks, including SWE-bench Verified and Terminal-Bench for breadth, we find much of this
saturation lies in the measurement, not the tasks. Most agent benchmarks reduce a run to one pass/fail bit,
which fails in two ways. It is too coarse: neighbouring top-ten agents on SWE-bench Verified differ by less
than its tests' errors, and correcting them reorders neighbours no more than deleting a random sixth of each
agent's passes. It is capped at the reference: ALE-Bench's top ten, separated by contest score, tie when
graded against the median human, although nearly half the runs beat that human. On fixed tasks, a higher bar
only moves the problem; graded credit fixes it. We derive a clipped log-ratio score with dated anchors and
cost as a separate axis. In a Terminal-Bench re-run, pass/fail-tied models differ over tenfold on the quantity
the tests discard.
