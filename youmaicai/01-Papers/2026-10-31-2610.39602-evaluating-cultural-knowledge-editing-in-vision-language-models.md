## 技术方案

### 核心思想

Current Vision-Language Models (VLMs) frequently fail to recognize culture-specific concepts,
especially from underrepresented regions. Effectively injecting this long-tail knowledge into
existing models remains a challenge. In this work, we investigate knowledge editing as a targeted
alternative by introducing CulturalBind, a benchmark comprising 3.2K images of 1K concepts from 6
domains, designed to evaluate culture-aware cross-modal knowledge as well as concept-level knowledge
editing in VLMs. Pre-edit evaluation reveals that textual and visual knowledge is decoupled: models
often demonstrate text-based knowledge of cultural concepts but fail to identify them from images.
We then evaluate editing methods in single and sequential scenarios. Although current methods
successfully correct individual visual recognition errors, they exhibit low cross-modal portability,
failing to integrate edits into the model's broader parametric knowledge. Furthermore, performance
degrades rapidly under sequential editing, highlighting the need for comprehensive adaptation
approaches that better consolidate multimodal knowledge over time.
