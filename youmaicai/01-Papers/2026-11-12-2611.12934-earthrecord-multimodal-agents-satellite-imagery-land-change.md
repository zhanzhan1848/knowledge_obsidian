## 核心要点
1. 提出 **EarthRecord** 基准，建立在**56 个地点的原始卫星档案**之上——**四十年的 Landsat、MODIS、VIIRS 与 Sentinel-2 数据**，且**云掩膜与跨传感器辐射归一化（harmonization）都交给智能体自己完成**。基准共 **238 个土地变化问题**，覆盖 **10 类分析任务**，评分依据是**全球产品的留出参考、多种方法之间的一致性以及视觉解读**三种信号。
2. 设计两个**对照实验（controls）**来检验答案究竟建立在什么之上：**closed book** 去掉全部卫星记录，考察纯先验知识水平；**instrument pairs** 只改动**某一颗卫星的数据**而地面完全不变，用来测试答案是否真的绑定了具体观测源。
3. 对 **12 个多模态智能体**的评测显示：领先智能体能**端到端完成大部分分析**，但在需要**从影像做映射（mapping from imagery）**时明显失手；进一步分析表明记录只是把答案**从先验知识推到了像素**，尚未到达**地面上真实的土地变化**——当某颗卫星的数据被篡改时，**约一半原本正确的答案会跟着篡改一起变**。项目页：https://ranranrannervous.github.io/EarthRecord/

## 摘要（原文）
While multimodal agents now carry out complex data analysis with tools, their ability to turn decades of
satellite imagery into reliable land-change analysis, crucial for Earth observation science, remains
unmeasured. Existing benchmarks fall short: they pose questions on curated images or give agents
preprocessed inputs, leaving out the raw multi-sensor archives where such analysis begins. We introduce
EarthRecord, a benchmark built on the raw record of 56 sites: four decades of Landsat, MODIS, VIIRS, and
Sentinel-2 data, with cloud masking and cross-sensor harmonization left to the agent. It poses 238 land-change
questions across ten analysis tasks, scored against withheld references from global products, agreement among
methods, and visual interpretation. Two controls test what answers rest on: the closed book removes the
record, and instrument pairs alter one satellite's data while nothing changes on the ground. Evaluations of
twelve multimodal agents reveal that leading agents complete most analyses end to end but falter when an answer
requires mapping from imagery. Further analysis shows that the record moves answers from prior knowledge to the
pixels but not yet to the real change on the ground: when one satellite's data are altered, half of the right
answers follow the alteration. EarthRecord thus provides a principled platform for measuring and advancing
agents from pixels to land change.
