## 核心要点
1. 面向**同态加密（HE）下 LLM 推理的算力瓶颈**，MOAI 是一个**非交互式**的安全 Transformer 推理框架，其三项关键设计为：① **列打包 + 对角打包结合**的统一求值流，在所有层保持一致策略，**消除昂贵的格式转换**；② **无旋转（rotation-free）**的 Softmax 与 LayerNorm 算法，大幅削减昂贵的 HE 旋转，在 BERT-base 推理中**移除 2,448 次 HE 旋转**；③ 列打包消除明文-密文矩阵乘中的旋转，**交错批处理（interleaved batching）**进一步减少密文-密文矩阵乘中的旋转。
2. 量化收益：在 BERT-base 的**全部矩阵乘**中，MOAI 使用的 HE 旋转次数**至少比 SOTA 方法少 1.7 倍**；由此相比 SOTA 的 HE 非交互式安全 Transformer 推理 **THOR**（CCS 25）**评估时间降低 52.8%**。
3. 迁移性验证：把 MOAI 应用到 **Powerformer** 框架（ACL 25，用更简单函数近似 Softmax 与 LayerNorm）上，相比 Powerformer **评估时间降低 55.7%**；单 GPU 环境下**每输入摊薄耗时 2.36 分钟**。并展示了对 **LLaMA-3-8B** 的可扩展性。实现已开源。

## 摘要（原文）
Privacy concerns have been raised in Large Language Models (LLM) inference when models are deployed in
Cloud Service Providers (CSP). Homomorphic encryption (HE) offers a promising solution by enabling
secure inference directly over encrypted inputs. However, the high computational overhead of HE
remains a major bottleneck. To address this challenge, we propose MOAI, an efficient HE-based,
non-interactive framework for secure transformer inference. MOAI gains significant efficiency
improvement from: (1) a novel evaluation flow that combines column and diagonal packing with consistent
strategies across all layers, eliminating expensive format conversions. (2) rotation-free algorithms for
Softmax and LayerNorm that significantly reduce the number of costly HE rotations, removing 2,448 HE
rotations in BERT-base inference. (3) Column packing removes rotations in plaintext-ciphertext matrix
multiplications and interleaved batching further reduces the rotations in ciphertext-ciphertext matrix
multiplications. MOAI uses at least 1.7x fewer HE rotations compared to the state-of-the-art works across
all matrix multiplications of BERT-base. As a result, we achieve a $52.8\%$ reduction in evaluation time
compared to the state-of-the-art HE-based non-interactive secure transformer inference, THOR (Moon et
al., CCS 25). We then apply MOAI on the Powerformer's framework and achieve a $55.7\%$ reduction in
evaluation time compared to Powerformer (Park et al., ACL 25), which approximates Softmax and LayerNorm
with simpler functions in transformer and proposes HE-based non-interactive transformer inference. We
report an amortized time of 2.36 minutes per input on a single GPU environment. We show the
extendibility by applying MOAI in LLaMA-3-8B. Our implementation is publicly available as open source.
