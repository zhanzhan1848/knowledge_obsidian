# Apriel Voice 27B Preview：语音、推理与工具调用的开放模型

| **摘要** | A capable voice assistant must do more than recognize words; it must understand requests, reason over context, sustain conversations, and act through tools. We present Apriel Voice 27B Preview, an open speech-language model that unifies these capabilities within a shared reasoning backbone while retaining text and image input modalities. Built on the post-trained Gemma 4 26B-A4B checkpoint, Apriel Voice directly processes speech and generates both responses and tool calls without requiring a separate dialogue model. We introduce a two-stage training procedure to adapt the model to speech. The first stage establishes the speech interface, while the second jointly adapts the projector and backbone through supervised fine-tuning, text replay, and source-dependent cross-entropy and forward Kullback--Leibler divergence supervision. Automatically constructed speech-conditioned dialogues provide conversational training data, while local teacher serving, overlapping teacher and student computation, and fused full-vocabulary losses enable efficient online distillation. In thinking mode, Apriel Voice achieves a score of 98.90 on Big Bench Audio, 81.13 on Speech-BFCL, and a task success rate of 65.51% on -Voice, with a mean latency of 1.91 seconds. It also achieves 51.27% English EVA accuracy and 74.13% experience pass@1. Finally, we deploy Apriel Voice 27B Preview on a custom serving platform to support interactive voice interactions. |

## 核心贡献

1. 发布 **Apriel Voice 27B Preview**：开放语音-语言模型，在**共享推理骨干**上统一语音理解、推理、对话与工具调用，同时保留文本与图像输入。
2. 基于**后训练过的 Gemma 4 26B-A4B** checkpoint，直接处理语音并同时生成回复与 tool call，**无需独立对话模型**。
3. **两阶段训练**：第一阶段建立语音接口；第二阶段通过 **SFT + 文本回放（text replay）+ 源相关的交叉熵与前向 KL 散度监督**联合适配 projector 与骨干。
4. **在线蒸馏工程**：自动构建语音条件对话、本地教师服务、**教师与学生计算重叠（overlapping teacher/student computation）**、**融合全词表损失**。
5. thinking mode 下：**Big Bench Audio 98.90**、**Speech-BFCL 81.13**、**τ-Voice 任务成功率 65.51%**、平均延迟 **1.91 秒**；English EVA 准确率 **51.27%**，experience pass@1 **74.13%**。已部署到自研 serving 平台。

## 技术方案

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 共享推理骨干 | 语音/文本/图像 + 工具调用统一于 Gemma 4 26B-A4B |
| 两阶段适配 | 语音接口建立 → projector + 骨干联合适配 |
| 文本回放 | 防止语音适配遗忘文本能力 |
| 源相关交叉熵 + 前向 KL | 源依赖的双重监督信号 |
| 教师-学生计算重叠 | 在线蒸馏的吞吐优化 |
| 融合全词表损失 | 避免稀疏监督信号 |

### 关键发现

- "教师与学生计算重叠"这一条是**纯粹的系统工程智慧**：把"教师产出监督信号"和"学生自己也在算"这两件事**在时间上对齐**，避免串行等待。
- "文本回放"和"前向 KL"共同对付的是语音适配的经典病：**灾难性遗忘**。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.40883-incongruence-aware-evaluation-of-multimodal-speech-emotion-recognition-i]] 互补：这篇给能力，那篇给诊断。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41292-from-transcription-to-reformulation-defining-and-benchmarking-grounded-g]] 同一语音大方向，但一个偏"能做什么"，一个偏"怎么评"。

> **注意**：τ-Voice 65.51% 与 EVA 51.27% 说明**开放语音模型与闭源语音助手仍有明显差距**，这是选型时必须知道的现实。
