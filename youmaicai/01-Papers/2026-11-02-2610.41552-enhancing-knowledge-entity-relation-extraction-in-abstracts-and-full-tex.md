| **摘要** | This study investigates the effectiveness of large pre-trained language models (LLMs) for knowledge entity relation extraction (KERE) from academic papers. Existing studies have examined individual types of LLM-based strategies and evaluated model performance using metrics such as precision and recall. To provide a broader evaluation, this study investigates multiple strategies, including closed-source LLM-based CoT prompting, LoRA-adapted open-source LLMs, LLM-enhanced small pre-trained language models (SLMs), and SLM-enhanced closed-source LLMs. In addition, this study introduces two strategies, namely SLM-enhanced open-source LLMs and closed-source LLM-enhanced open-source LLMs. These strategies are evaluated from multiple perspectives, including metrics, their ability to mitigate errors observed in SLMs, and potential directions for model optimization. This study manually constructs two annotated datasets in the NLP domain, covering Abstract and Full-Text datasets. Experimental results show that the LoRA-adapted T5 model achieves the highest macro F1 scores of 0.7408 and 0.7013 on the Abstract and Full-Text datasets, respectively. SLMs also benefit from LLM-generated entity descriptions under the LLM-enhanced SLM strategy. Error analysis further reveals that the effectiveness of LLM-based strategies in mitigating SLM errors varies across error types. Based on the identified limitations and error patterns of these strategies, several optimization directions are proposed. In addition, combining strategies with different error patterns further improves KERE performance. |

## 核心贡献

1. **目标**：评估**大型预训练语言模型**在**学术论文**中做**知识实体关系抽取（KERE）**的有效性。
2. **既有不足**：先前研究只考察**个别类型的 LLM 策略**，且只用 precision/recall 评估。
3. **本文评估的策略矩阵**（四类 + 两个新策略）：
   - **闭源 LLM 的 CoT 提示**
   - **LoRA 适配的开源 LLM**
   - **LLM 增强的小型预训练模型（SLM）**
   - **SLM 增强的闭源 LLM**
   - **（新）SLM 增强的开源 LLM**
   - **（新）闭源 LLM 增强的开源 LLM**
4. **数据**：人工构建两个标注数据集 —— **Abstract** 与 **Full-Text** 两个学术 NLP 域数据集。
5. **结果**：**LoRA 适配的 T5** 在两个数据集上取得**最高 macro F1：0.7408（Abstract）与 0.7013（Full-Text）**。
6. **SLM 也能从 LLM 生成的实体描述中获益**（LLM-enhanced SLM 策略）。
7. **误差分析**：LLM 策略在**缓解 SLM 错误上的效果因错误类型而异**；**组合具有不同错误模式的策略可进一步提升 KERE 表现**。
8. **基于误差模式提出多个优化方向**。

### 关键发现

- **"组合具有不同错误模式的策略可进一步提升"** 是本文最有方法论价值的结论 —— 它把策略组合从"堆料"变成了**互补性设计**。这与 [[2026-11-02-2610.38977-beyond-passing-tests-a-systematic-review-and-controlled-re-evaluation-of]]（匹配预算下的正面对决）、[[2026-11-02-2610.41604-real-computation-or-merely-naming-a-pre-registered-mechanism-audit-of-25]]（按错误类型做诊断修复）是同一个思路：**按误差模式而非平均分做决策**。
- **Abstract 的 F1 (0.7408) 高于 Full-Text (0.7013)**，差了 4 个点 —— 说明**更长的上下文没有带来更好结果**，这与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41391-comparative-evaluation-of-llm-based-review-paradigms-for-academic-paper]]（摘要-only 优于全文摘要化）**独立印证**：长文本在结构化抽取任务上反而引入噪声。
- **两个新策略（SLM 增强开源 LLM、闭源 LLM 增强开源 LLM）** 反映了一个正在成型的模式：**小型领域模型承担结构，小型通用模型承担知识**。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41710-advancing-clinical-trial-outcome-prediction-with-domain-specific-large-l]]（BioBERT/BioMistral）同属**垂直领域小模型**路线，结论一致：**在垂直任务上，预训练/领域知识的贡献常被低估**。
