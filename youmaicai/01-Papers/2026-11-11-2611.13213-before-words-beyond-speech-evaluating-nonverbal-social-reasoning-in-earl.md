## 核心要点
1. **NEST**：首个**以儿童为中心**的非言语社会推理基准，手工标注片段（**约 12 秒**），覆盖自然场景、文化语境与发展阶段。
2. 问题：**粗粒度评估 schema 把基础感知与高阶推理混为一谈**。
3. 引入**组合式 schema**：把原子交互线索组合进递进的社会推理层级（情境 / 行为 / 人际 / 开放式描述）。
4. 对 SOTA VLM 的分析显示**性能陡降**：上下文识别强，但**解码精确的行为与人际线索存在大幅差距**。
5. 追溯到**后果落地不足（poor consequence grounding）与时间敏感性差**等偏差；并用领域自适应策略做评估。

## 摘要（原文）
Children's earliest years are a period of intense social learning, during which meaning is built
through gaze, gesture, and physical contact. Yet, this critical aspect of social interaction remains
under-evaluated in multimodal video understanding, as existing benchmarks prioritize the
conversation-driven interactions of adults. We address this gap with \bench ($\textbf{N}$aturalistic
$\textbf{E}$arly-childhood $\textbf{S}$ocial in$\textbf{T}$eractions), the first child-centric
benchmark for nonverbal social reasoning. \bench comprises \totalvids manually annotated clips
($\sim$12s) curated for diversity across naturalistic settings, cultural contexts, and developmental
stages. Beyond a lack of data, targeted model improvement in this domain is hindered by coarse
evaluation schemas that conflate basic perception with higher-order reasoning. To resolve this,
\bench introduces a compositional schema that builds atomic interaction cues into progressive levels
of social reasoning: contextual, behavioral, interpersonal, and open-ended descriptions. Extensive
analysis of state-of-the-art Vision-Language Models (VLMs) reveals a stark performance collapse:
despite strong contextual recognition, models exhibit substantial gaps in decoding precise
behavioral and interpersonal cues. We trace these failures to specific biases, including poor
consequence grounding and temporal sensitivity. Finally, we ground \bench in real-world applications
by evaluating VLMs using domain adaptation strategies. Ultimately, \bench provides a rigorous
testbed for advancing socially grounded artificial intelligence in early childhood. Project page:
https://laubravo.github.io/nest_website/.
