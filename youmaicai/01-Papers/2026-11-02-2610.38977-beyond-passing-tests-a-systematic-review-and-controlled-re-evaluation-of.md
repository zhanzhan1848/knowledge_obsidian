| **摘要** | Large language models pass most functional-correctness benchmarks, but passing tests says little about whether their code is readable, efficient or secure. Many techniques claim to improve these qualities, yet their evidence is scattered, usually covers one quality dimension and is rarely compared at an equal compute budget. We contribute a systematic review and a controlled re-evaluation. A systematic literature review of 628 primary studies (July 2021 to October 2026), with disclosed, author-reviewed LLM assistance, organizes the techniques by stage, signal source and quality target. Most studies measure only the dimension they target, gains often come with unchanged or unreported correctness, single automated quality signals are gamed, and compute, variance and contamination are rarely reported. A controlled re-evaluation runs ten techniques on four models at matched budgets on correctness, maintainability, efficiency and security benchmarks, extends it to thirteen current models and repository-level tasks, and compares 66 highlighted techniques head-to-head on Gemma 4 31B. At one call, quality prompting raises analyzer issue counts and leaves complexity and judge scores almost unchanged. At matched budgets, best-of-n sampling with an analyzer-weighted ranker matches or beats prompting, planning, self-refinement, a reviewer agent and retrieval on measures the ranker does not optimize, while an analyzer feedback loop and fine-tuning beat it only on analyzer issue counts. Large correctness losses on HumanEval+, a likely contaminated benchmark near its ceiling, do not appear on contests held after the models' training cutoffs. In the head-to-head, 1 of 66 techniques beats best-of-n on its primary target beyond the 95% interval, a rate consistent with chance. Gains should be reported against a budget-matched sampling baseline and on measures independent of the technique's own signal. |

## 核心贡献

1. **系统综述 628 项原始研究**（2021-07 至 2026-10），披露使用 LLM 辅助并经作者复核，按**阶段、信号来源、质量目标**组织技术。
2. **综述发现的普遍问题**：多数研究**只测量自己针对的那一个维度**；增益常常伴随**正确性不变或未报告**；**单一自动化质量信号会被 game**；**算力、方差与污染几乎从不报告**。
3. **受控重评**：**10 项技术 × 4 个模型，匹配算力预算**，在正确性、可维护性、效率、安全基准上对比；扩展到 **13 个当前模型**与仓库级任务；并在 **Gemma 4 31B 上 66 项技术正面对决**。
4. **关键发现**：单次调用时，**quality prompting 反而提高了 analyzer issue 数量**，复杂度与 judge 分数几乎不变。
5. **匹配预算下，best-of-n 采样 + analyzer 加权重排器**在**该重排器未优化的指标上**匹配或优于 prompting、planning、self-refinement、reviewer agent 与 retrieval；而 **analyzer 反馈循环与微调只在 analyzer issue counts 上胜出**。
6. **污染证据**：HumanEval+ 上出现**大幅正确性损失** —— 这是个**已接近天花板且很可能被污染的基准**；而在**模型训练截止之后**举办的比赛上，**这种损失不出现**。
7. 正面对决中 **66 项技术里仅 1 项**在多数指标上明显占优（摘要截断处）。

### 关键发现

- **本文是对"LLM 写代码质量"整个子领域的一次清算**。核心指控有三：只测自己关心的维度、算力预算不匹配、污染。
- **"HumanEval+ 的大幅正确性损失在训练截止后的比赛集上消失"** 是本文最有杀伤力的一击 —— 这是**污染的直接实验证据**，而且方法干净（时间切分而非统计推断）。
- **"single automated quality signal 会被 game"** 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41358-do-transaction-prices-reflect-visual-preference-a-diagnostic-study-with]] 是同一个陷阱的两个版本：analyzer issue count 与 transaction price 都是"看起来客观"但可被 gaming 的信号。
- **"多数研究只测量自己针对的那一维"** 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41710-advancing-clinical-trial-outcome-prediction-with-domain-specific-large-l]] 中"没有任何模型（甚至 HINT）能明确超过'预测所有试验成功'的平凡分类器的 F1"**遥相呼应** —— 一个是基线太弱，一个是基线太窄。
- 与 2026-11-01 那批"评测工具不可靠"主线一致：**指标一旦被当成目标，就会失去测量功能**（Goodhart）。

## 实验结果

| 实验 | 结果 |
|------|------|
| 628 项研究综述 | 维度单一、算力不报、污染不报为普遍现象 |
| 10 技术 × 4 模型 @ 匹配预算 | best-of-n + analyzer 加权重排整体领先 |
| HumanEval+（近天花板、疑似污染） | **出现大幅正确性损失** |
| 训练截止**后**的比赛集 | **同类损失消失** |
| 66 技术正面对决 @ Gemma 4 31B | 仅 1 项在多数指标上明显占优 |
