## 核心要点
1. 在 **Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct 与 Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct** 中研究从 **GoEmotions** 提取的 **8 个情绪方向**，通过情感探针、激活干预与监督纠正三条路径。
2. **仅情绪的探针**在二分类 TweetEval 上达 **88.66%** 与 **90.70%**。
3. 探索性**匹配消融**：**原始情绪空间移除**比**匹配的各向同性随机子空间移除**多损失 **2.39 与 1.10 pp**（0.5B 与 3B）；四个 TweetEval 原始与去噪效应经 Holm 校正后在八次比较中**全部显著**。
4. SST-2 扩展显示更小的原始超额损失（0.66 与 0.57 pp），**无效应在 Holm 校正后存活**。
5. 在 **20,000 条 Amazon Polarity** 评审上，**基于情绪的纠正优于未纠正的 Qwen，但不 Consistently 超过仅 margin 的基线**。
6. 作者谨慎区分「表征中可用的情感信息」「改变该表征的效果」与「其分类实用价值」，**并声明不确立一种独特的情绪机制**。

## 摘要（原文）
Emotion vectors are directions in a language model's internal representations that are associated
with emotional concepts. Prior work shows that these vectors can influence model behavior, but their
role in sentiment classification remains unclear. This study investigates emotion directions in
Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct. Eight directions extracted from GoEmotions are
evaluated through sentiment probing, activation interventions, and supervised correction. Emotion-
only probes achieve 88.66% and 90.70% accuracy on binary TweetEval. In exploratory matched-ablation
experiments, raw emotion-space removal causes 2.39 and 1.10 percentage points more accuracy loss
than matched isotropic random-subspace removal for 0.5B and 3B, respectively. All four TweetEval raw
and denoised effects are significant after Holm adjustment across eight comparisons. An exploratory
SST-2 extension shows smaller raw excess losses of 0.66 and 0.57 points, with no effects surviving
Holm adjustment. Frozen sentiment-correction classifiers are also tested on 20,000 Amazon Polarity
reviews. Emotion-based correction improves accuracy over uncorrected Qwen, but does not consistently
outperform margin-only baselines. These results distinguish sentiment information available in model
representations, the effects of changing those representations, and their practical value for
classification. They do not establish a uniquely emotional mechanism.
