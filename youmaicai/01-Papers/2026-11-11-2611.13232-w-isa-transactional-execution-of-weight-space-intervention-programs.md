## 核心要点
1. **W-ISA** 是执行权重空间干预程序的**软件指令集与事务式运行时**。
2. **形状检查的指令**表达参数更新、投影、消融与 rank-1 变换；运行时在**请求作用域的执行上下文**内应用程序，并在**完成或中断后恢复保存的参数**。
3. **基于激活的匹配-动作规则**把测得模型状态连接到干预选择。
4. 在 SmolLM-135M-Instruct 与 Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct 上评估恢复与重放；**45 次计时请求**中，物化的 rank-1 更新与等价 PyTorch hook 在 32/128/512 token 上产生**完全相同的贪心序列**，最大 prompt-logit 差异 **0.000184**。
5. WikiText-2 完整 raw 测试集上，组合的 SmolLM 注意力头消融 + MLP 投影把困惑度从 22.2353 变为 22.3188（相对上升 **0.3754%**）。
6. 价值主张：**把干预规格、执行与测量分离，使参数级实验可复现且可组合**。

## 摘要（原文）
We present W-ISA, a software instruction set and transactional runtime for executing weight-space
intervention programs on pretrained language models. Shape-checked instructions express parameter
updates, projections, ablations, and rank-one transformations. The runtime applies these programs
within request-scoped execution contexts and restores saved parameters after completion or
interruption. Activation-based match-action rules connect measured model states to intervention
selection. We evaluate restoration and replay on SmolLM-135M-Instruct and Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, and
compare materialized rank-one updates with equivalent PyTorch hooks on the latter. In 45 timed
requests, both intervention implementations produce identical greedy sequences at 32, 128, and 512
tokens; their maximum prompt-logit difference is 0.000184. On the complete WikiText-2 raw test
split, the combined SmolLM attention-head ablation and MLP projection changes perplexity from
22.2353 to 22.3188, a relative increase of 0.3754%. W-ISA separates intervention specification,
execution, and measurement, making parameter-level experiments reproducible and composable.
