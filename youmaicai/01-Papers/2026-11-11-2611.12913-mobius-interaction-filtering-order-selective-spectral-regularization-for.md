## 核心要点
1. **Möbius Interaction Filtering (MoIF)**：多模态幽默检测需联合解释语言、声音表达与视觉行为；**表达性融合还必须调节不同阶的交互如何进入最终判断**。
2. **阶选择性谱正则**：私有时序编码器与平衡互惠交互构造监督的单模态/双模态/三模态预测器；**它们的均匀聚合恰好是一个 Möbius 域滤波器**，具有几何递减的交互阶响应。
3. **保留高阶表示的同时调节其对预测的贡献**——这是纯表达性融合做不到的。
4. 受控 UR-FUNNY 扰动揭示**跨交互阶的标签对齐信噪比显著下降**；诱导出的滤波器将最终比率相对共同训练的三模态预测器提升 **1.62–2.79 倍**，同时在扰动下保持自身干净的类对齐信号。
5. 五种子实验达 UR-FUNNY 上 **70.6%** 准确率与加权 F1，超过 **13 个复现方法**；CMU-MOSI 与 CMU-MOSEI 验证了向情感预测的适用性。

## 摘要（原文）
Multimodal humor detection requires interpreting language, vocal delivery, and visual behavior
jointly. Expressive fusion must also regulate how interactions of different orders enter the final
judgment. We propose M\"obius Interaction Filtering (MoIF), which learns a modality-subset
prediction function and imposes order-selective spectral regularization through its aggregation
structure. Private temporal encoders and balanced reciprocal interaction construct supervised
unimodal, bimodal, and trimodal predictors. Their uniform aggregate is exactly a M\"obius-domain
filter with a geometrically decreasing interaction-order response. This formulation retains higher-
order representations while regulating their contribution to prediction. Controlled UR-FUNNY
perturbations reveal a pronounced decline in label-aligned signal-to-noise ratio across interaction
orders. The induced filter improves the final ratio by 1.62-2.79 times relative to the co-trained
trimodal predictor while preserving its own clean class-aligned signal under perturbation. Five-seed
experiments achieve 70.6% accuracy and weighted F1 on UR-FUNNY, exceeding thirteen reproduced
methods on these detection metrics. CMU-MOSI and CMU-MOSEI evaluations establish applicability to
sentiment prediction, and cross-seed analyses show more stable estimates on all three datasets. MoIF
connects the organization of multimodal interactions to their statistical reliability through an
executable spectral constraint. Code is available at https://github.com/D-ST-Sword/MoIF.
