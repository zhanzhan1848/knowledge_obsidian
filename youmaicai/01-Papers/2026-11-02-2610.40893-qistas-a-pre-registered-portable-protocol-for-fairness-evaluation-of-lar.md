# Qistas：LLM 公平性评测的预注册、可移植协议

| **摘要** | Fairness benchmarks for large language models (LLMs) are overwhelmingly English-born and American in their social categories; translated versions carry those categories into societies where they do not apply, and almost none fix their hypotheses before the models are run. We present Qistas (Arabic qistas, "the just balance"), a protocol for evaluating how LLMs treat the social, confessional, and regional constituencies of a society, portable across countries and instantiated first for Iraq. In its pilot form it measures only what needs no verdict on contested truth: procedural symmetry across matched counterfactual pairs, differential refusal, stereotype reliance under ambiguity, and terminological framing of documented events. Every measurement is paired by construction and analysed with paired machinery (sign-flip permutation tests, exact McNemar tests, template-clustered bootstrap intervals, Holm-corrected families), with a power analysis that states, before any data exist, the smallest effect each axis can detect after the design effect of template clustering. We release the authoring apparatus (a pruned 3x5 taxonomy with four cells closed by design, per-type checklists, a cumulative rulings log, a structural audit, and a human review notebook) and report its first field test: a semantic review of a 58-item seed bank in which 24 of 25 template groups failed at least one check. The defect patterns it exposed (evaluation criteria leaking into prompts, token-level swaps that break Arabic agreement, epistemically unbalanced pairs, single-theme refusal probes, unwritten dialect twins counted as coverage) are, we argue, general hazards of counterfactual fairness benchmarks. This is a protocol paper, not a results paper: no model has been evaluated, and the accompanying pre-registration commits the hypotheses, metrics, exclusion rules, and analysis plan that the pilot will follow. |

## 核心贡献

1. 直指现有 LLM 公平性基准的三个结构性问题：**以英语和美国社会类别为原型**、**翻译版本把不适用的类别带进其他社会**、**几乎没有在跑模型前固定假设**。
2. 提出 **Qistas**（阿拉伯语 qistas，"公正的平衡"）：评估 LLM 如何对待一个社会的**社会、宗派、地区选民群体**的协议，可跨国家移植，首个实例化为**伊拉克**。
3. 试点形态只测量**不需要对争议真相下判断**的东西：匹配反事实对上的**程序对称性**、**差异化拒答**、**模糊条件下的刻板印象依赖**、**已记录事件的术语框架化**。
4. **方法学严谨**：所有测量**构造上即配对**，用配对统计工具分析（符号翻转置换检验、精确 McNemar 检验、模板聚类 bootstrap 区间、Holm 校正族），并做**功效分析** —— 在数据产生**之前**就声明每个轴最小可检测效应。
5. **发布编写工具**：剪枝后的 3×5 分类法（4 格按设计封闭）、逐类清单、累积裁决日志、结构审计、人工复核笔记本。
6. **首次实地测试的坦白**：58 项种子库的语义复核中，**25 个模板组中有 24 个至少在一项检查上失败**。暴露的缺陷模式：评估标准泄漏进 prompt、破坏阿拉伯语一致性的 token 级替换、认识论不平衡的配对、单主题拒答探针、未书写的方言孪生被计为覆盖。

## 技术方案

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 预注册 | 跑模型前固定假设 |
| 配对构造 | 所有测量构造上成对 |
| 符号翻转置换检验 | 非参数配对检验 |
| 精确 McNemar 检验 | 小样本配对二分类 |
| 模板聚类 bootstrap | 区间估计考虑聚类 |
| Holm 校正 | 多重比较校正 |
| 功效分析 | 事前声明最小可检测效应 |
| 3×5 剪枝分类法 | 4 格按设计封闭 |

### 关键发现

- **本文最刺眼的数据是那个 24/25 失败率** —— 也就是说，**它自己做的第一批评审发现自己的模板有 24 组不合格**。这是极罕见的学术诚实度，也立刻把"其他公平性基准有多可靠"这个问题摆上台面。
- 缺陷模式列表本身就是可复用的**评审清单**。
- "只测量不需要对争议真相下判断的东西"是重要的设计克制 —— 很多公平性 benchmark 恰恰栽在偷偷引入争议判断上。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41486-multi-test-nation-bias-benchmarking-of-llms-with-explicit-unbiased-basel]] 呼应：后者用**行为基线**（投票记录）定义"无偏"，Qistas 用**程序对称性**定义无偏 —— 两条互补路径。
