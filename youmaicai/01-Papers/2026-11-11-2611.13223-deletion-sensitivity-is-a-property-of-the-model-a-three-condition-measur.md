## 核心要点
1. 推理模型在多轮工具会话每轮写推理轨迹，后续轮读取该历史；上游供应商把轨迹绑定到签发方，**跨签发方转发的请求会带着被删除的历史抵达**。
2. 首个**直接、盲、多随机种子**的全量推理历史删除测量：**三种渲染条件 × 七个开源权重推理模型 × 三个种子 × 794 个任务 × 官方 checker**。
3. 同样的删除跨度为 **−4.4 到 +51.3 pp**（两端均排除 0）；对唯一被重新剂量（0 → 100% 删除）的模型，**读数变化不超过 1.5 点**。
4. 结论：**删除敏感性是模型级属性**，读数随模型本身走、跨部署恒定；重训或重发的模型**就是新模型**，需用两格筛查（一次保留跑 + 一次删除跑）以两次运行的代价重新读数。
5. 该读数**必须进入路由与聚合的先验信息**，而不是作为隐形默认成本持续存在。

## 摘要（原文）
Reasoning models write a reasoning trace at every turn of a multi-turn tool session, and later turns
read this history. Across 94k ShareGPT conversations, 73 percent of user interactions span multiple
turns. Upstream providers bind each trace to its issuer, so a request forwarded across issuers
arrives with its history deleted, and the task cost of this stripping remains unmeasured. To the
best of our knowledge, this paper presents the first direct, blind, multi-seed measurement of full
reasoning-history deletion in multi-turn tool calling, through three rendering conditions, seven
open-weight reasoning models, three seeds, 794 tasks, and the official checker. The same deletion
spans -4.4 to +51.3 points with both ends excluding zero, and for the one model re-dosed from 0 to
100 percent deletion, the reading varies by at most 1.5 points. Deletion sensitivity is a model-
level property. The reading follows the model itself and stays constant across deployments, and a
retrained or re-released model is a new model that a two-cell screen, one retention run and one
deletion run, re-reads for the cost of two runs. Deletion sensitivity is thus a measurable property
of the model, and the reading must enter routing and aggregation as prior information rather than
persist as an invisible default cost.
