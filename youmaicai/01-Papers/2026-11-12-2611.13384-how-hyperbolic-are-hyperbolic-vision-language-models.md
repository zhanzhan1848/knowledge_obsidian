## 核心要点
1. 论文在**指数映射（exponential map）**这一唯一把曲率引入欧氏网络的操作点上追问"双曲 VLM 到底有多双曲"。在 **MERU、HyCoCLIP、ARGENT 与 PHyCLIP** 上发现：**指数映射可被恒等函数很好地近似**——映射前欧氏空间中的成对距离与夹角在投影后几乎不变，且在**推理时直接移除该映射不会改变评测分数**。
2. 据此，作者在 **CC3M** 上从零训练**欧氏版 VLM**，使用与双曲版**完全相同的层级损失（hierarchy losses）**：在训练**最后约 90% 的步数中**，训练统计量与双曲模型几乎无法区分，所得模型**取得相同的评测分数并复现相同的径向分布（radial distributions）**。
3. 结论是：**欧氏空间足以作为当前实践下层级视觉-语言学习的基底**，而双曲几何要兑现其理论承诺需要新的范式。代码见 https://github.com/fbyrman/hyperbolic-vlms

## 摘要（原文）
Hyperbolic vision-language models (VLMs) have emerged as a rapidly growing area of research, reporting strong
zero-shot performance alongside an interpretable embedding space in which radius orders representations by
generality. But how hyperbolic are hyperbolic VLMs in practice? We investigate this at the exponential map,
the single operation through which curvature enters an otherwise Euclidean network. We show that across MERU,
HyCoCLIP, ARGENT, and PHyCLIP, the exponential map is well approximated by the identity function, i.e.,
pairwise distances and angles in the pre-map Euclidean space barely change under projection, and removing the
map at inference leaves evaluation scores unchanged. Motivated by this observation, we train Euclidean VLMs on
CC3M from scratch under the same hierarchy losses. Over roughly the final 90% of the training steps, the
training statistics are nearly indistinguishable from those of their hyperbolic counterparts, and the
resulting models match the evaluation scores and recover the same radial distributions. Our findings establish
Euclidean space as a sufficient substrate for hierarchical vision-language learning as it is currently
practiced, and motivate new paradigms in which hyperbolic geometry can deliver on its theoretical promise.
Code is available at https://github.com/fbyrman/hyperbolic-vlms.
