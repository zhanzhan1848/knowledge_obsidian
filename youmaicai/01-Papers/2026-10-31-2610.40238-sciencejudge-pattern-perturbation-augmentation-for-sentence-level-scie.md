## 技术方案

### 核心思想

Sentence-level scientific claim verification determines whether each sentence in an AI-generated
scientific paragraph is supported by a given multimodal evidence bundle. Fine-grained reporting
errors often preserve fluent and plausible statements while introducing subtle evidence-claim
deviations, making them difficult to detect under sparse and imbalanced supervision. We propose
Pattern Perturbation Augmentation (PPA), which models reporting errors as label-specific
faithfulness breaking transformations and induces reusable error patterns from labeled examples. PPA
applies these patterns to supported claims to construct controlled hard negatives and expands their
coverage through pattern-level, operation-level, and cross-domain augmentation. Based on these
augmented views, we develop ScienceJudge, which integrates PPA-trained vision-language predictors
and instruction-guided LLM predictors through class-weighted ensemble inference. On the local
development set, ScienceJudge improves the average score from 0.8124 to 0.8692, achieving a Macro-F1
of 0.8466 and a Paragraph Exact Match score of 0.8919. Our submitted system ranks first on the
official NLPCC 2026 Task 10 Track 1 leaderboard with a final score of 0.6890.
