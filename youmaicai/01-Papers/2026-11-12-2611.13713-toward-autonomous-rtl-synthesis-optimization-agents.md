## 核心要点
1. 论文提出面向自主 RTL 与综合优化的三条相互关联的路线：**(1) 真实基准**——讨论参考式（reference-based）与开放式（open-ended）两类 RTL 优化基准，用于在**贴近真实 EDA 工作流**的条件下评估优化机会；**(2) 工具驱动的 RTL 优化**——提出 **Dr. RTL** 多智能体框架；**(3) 设计特定（design-specific）的综合优化**——探索智能体驱动的综合脚本调优与可执行综合算法的发现。
2. **Dr. RTL** 是一个多智能体框架，自动化**关键路径分析（critical-path analysis）、RTL 重写与实现级评估**三个环节，并通过**可复用的优化技能（reusable optimization skills）**持续自我改进；在 **20 个真实 RTL 设计**上评测，相对商用综合工具实现平均 **WNS/TNS 提升 21%/17%，面积减少 6%**。
3. 第三条路线将智能体用于**跨中间表示（intermediate representation）、技术无关优化（technology-independent optimization）与技术映射（technology mapping）**层面的综合脚本调优与可执行综合算法发现，并提出最终愿景：**AI-native 设计自动化**，在验证约束与实现反馈的引导下联合探索架构、RTL 与 EDA 算法设计空间。

## 摘要（原文）
Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) and autonomous agents are creating new opportunities for
hardware design optimization. This paper examines three connected directions toward autonomous RTL and
synthesis optimization: (1) Realistic benchmarking. We discuss reference-based and open-ended RTL optimization
benchmarks that evaluate optimization opportunities under realistic EDA workflows. (2) Tool-grounded RTL
optimization. We present Dr. RTL, a multi-agent framework that automates critical-path analysis, RTL rewriting,
and implementation-level evaluation, while continuously improving through reusable optimization skills.
Evaluated on 20 real-world RTL designs, Dr.~RTL achieves average WNS/TNS improvements of 21\%/17\% with a 6\%
area reduction over commercial synthesis. (3) Design-specific synthesis optimization. We explore agent-driven
synthesis script tuning and the discovery of executable synthesis algorithms across intermediate
representations, technology-independent optimization, and technology mapping. Finally, we envision AI-native
design automation that jointly explores architectural, RTL, and EDA algorithm design spaces, guided by
verification constraints and implementation feedback.
