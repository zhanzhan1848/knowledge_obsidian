## 核心要点
1. **Brasilidade**：巴西葡语评估方法论，含四个领域的标准化任务；本文发布**第一个领域**的任务（语言、语法与巴西葡语变体的细微差别）。
2. **反直觉结论：模型规模本身不决定性能**——若干**中等规模模型超过更大模型**。
3. **专为葡萄牙语训练的模型总体并未高于平均**，尽管经过针对性训练。
4. 不同任务层级差异显著：部分任务对多数模型容易，部分构成极端挑战，部分结果混合、性能方差大。

## 摘要（原文）
This work presents a new benchmark that aims to address the limitations of current methods for
evaluating LLMs in Brazilian Portuguese. We introduced Brasilidade, an assessment methodology
comprised of standardized tasks distributed across four different domains, and we are making
available the tasks from its first domain, which address nuances of the language, grammar, and
variations of Brazilian Portuguese. The results show that model size, by itself, does not determine
performance; several medium-sized models outperformed larger models in the protocols evaluated.
Specific models for Portuguese, in general, did not perform above average, despite their targeted
training. Different task levels revealed significant nuances: while some tasks proved easy for most
models, others posed extreme challenges, and some yielded mixed results, with wide variations in
performance. This clearly illustrates the need for benchmarks that address the limitations of the
models in Brazilian Portuguese, allowing future training cycles to seek to overcome them. Project
repository https://github.com/soberania-oficial/brasilidade
