## 核心要点
1. 观察量化 agent 的失败 rollout：**忽略任务要求或环境条件**，产生与全精度参考分歧的环境转移。
2. 难点：离线数据可能错过学生的失败情境，学生自己的 rollout 在这些情境中又少有正确响应。
3. **SteerDistill** 用全精度教师指导学生**修正失败响应**，并用学生执行反馈**迭代精炼指导**，形成**闭环**，产生**局部验证过的行为修正**。
4. 随后**撤除指导**训练学生：在修正轮匹配被引导的学生、在其他轮匹配全精度教师，从而支持无指导推理。
5. 跨五个 agentic 基准与三个模型，**W4A16** 下平均分超过最强量化基线 **1.67–2.18 点**，距 BF16 在 1 点内。
6. 在 **τ²-Bench** 上成功率超 **QeRL 1.84 pp**，**少用 67% 训练 GPU 小时**，且成功轨迹更短。

## 摘要（原文）
Low-bit quantization reduces the inference cost of LLM agents but can compromise task success.
Examining failed rollouts, we find that quantized agents overlook task requirements or environmental
conditions, producing environment transitions that diverge from successful full-precision
references. Correcting these behavioral divergences remains difficult: offline data may miss the
student's failure contexts, while its own rollouts may provide few correct responses in those
contexts. We introduce SteerDistill, a quantization-aware distillation method in which a full-
precision teacher guides the student to revise failed responses and iteratively refines its guidance
using feedback from student execution. This closed-loop teacher steering produces locally verified
behavioral corrections. We internalize these corrections by training the student without guidance to
match the steered student's predictions at corrected turns and the full-precision teacher's at other
turns, enabling unassisted inference. Across five agentic benchmarks and three models, SteerDistill
exceeds the strongest quantized baseline by 1.67--2.18 points in average score under W4A16 and stays
within one point of BF16. On $τ^2$-Bench, it outperforms QeRL by 1.84 percentage points in success
rate using 67% fewer training GPU hours and produces shorter successful trajectories than the
quantized baselines.
