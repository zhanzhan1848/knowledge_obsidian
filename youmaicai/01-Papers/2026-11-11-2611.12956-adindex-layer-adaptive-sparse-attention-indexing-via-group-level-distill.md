## 核心要点
1. **DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA)** 的 indexer 仍要为每个 query 评分整个因果前缀，**indexer 单独可占 prefill GPU 时间的一半以上**。
2. **ADIndex** 引入：(i) **层级校准策略**（基于**层间异质性**与跨 query 检索重叠的观察）；(ii) **组级蒸馏**（利用层间异质性高效训练自适应层级组索引器）；(iii) **低延迟推理**（查询组内 token bank 共享减少重复全前缀扫描，同时保留 query 特定精修）。
3. 在 **DeepSeek-V3.2 与 GLM-5.1** 上，**256K 上下文**下 prefill 与解码 kernel 分别最高加速 **6.22×** 与 **3.40×**，**128K** 端到端服务加速 **1.54×**。
4. LongBench / LongBench v2 / RULER 性能**近乎无损**。

## 摘要（原文）
DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) reduces the computational cost of attention through token-level
selection. However, its indexer still scores the entire causal prefix for each query, making
indexing a dominant source of latency in long-context inference. Notably, the indexer alone can
account for more than 50% of total prefill GPU time at long context lengths. To address this
bottleneck, we propose ADIndex, an Adaptive and Distillation-driven Indexing method for token-level
sparse attention. ADIndex integrates three key components: (i) a layer-wise calibration strategy
that adaptively accommodates cross-query retrieval overlap, motivated by our observation of
pronounced inter-layer heterogeneity; (ii) a group-level distillation scheme that leverages this
heterogeneity to efficiently train adaptive layer-wise group indexers, capturing salient retrieval
signals from individual queries; and (iii) a low-latency inference strategy that reduces repeated
full-prefix scans through token bank sharing within query groups while retaining query-specific
refinement. Experiments on DeepSeek-V3.2 and GLM-5.1 demonstrate that ADIndex preserves near-
lossless task performance on LongBench, LongBench v2, and RULER while delivering substantial
efficiency gains over standard DSA, including prefill and decoding kernel speedups of up to 6.22x
and 3.40x, respectively, at a context length of 256K across the layer-wise reuse configurations, as
well as an end-to-end serving speedup of 1.54x at a context length of 128K.
