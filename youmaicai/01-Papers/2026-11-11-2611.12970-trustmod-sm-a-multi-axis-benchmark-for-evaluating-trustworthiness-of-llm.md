## 核心要点
1. **TrustMod-SM**：约 **29,000 个实例**、8 个数据集，在信任度/公平性/安全性/鲁棒性/情境完整性五个维度评估 **16 个开源权重模型（0.5B–284B，含纯文本与视觉语言架构）**与一个闭源前沿模型。
2. **更高的检测准确率往往伴随更大的群体差异**。
3. 模型经常**服从隐藏或夸大提示**；**对错误预测给出高置信度**；**很少升级模糊案例**。
4. 难以区分仇恨言论与**反话语（counter-speech）或 reclaimed language**，**尤其在 8B 以下**。
5. **规模放大并不统一解决问题，且没有单一模型在五个维度上都强**。

## 摘要（原文）
Large Language Models (LLMs) show strong potential for social media content moderation, but their
trustworthiness beyond overall accuracy remains underexplored. We introduce TrustMod-SM, a benchmark
of roughly 29,000 instances across eight datasets, to evaluate LLM moderators on five dimensions:
trustfulness, fairness, safety, robustness, and context integrity. Evaluating 16 open-weight models
(0.5B-284B parameters, spanning text-only and vision-language architectures) and one closed frontier
model, we find persistent trustworthiness gaps. Higher detection accuracy often coincides with
greater demographic disparity, and models frequently comply with concealment or exaggeration
prompts, assign high confidence to wrong predictions, rarely escalate ambiguous cases, and struggle
to separate hate speech from counter-speech or reclaimed language (especially below 8B parameters).
Scaling does not uniformly resolve these issues, and no single model excels across all five
dimensions.
