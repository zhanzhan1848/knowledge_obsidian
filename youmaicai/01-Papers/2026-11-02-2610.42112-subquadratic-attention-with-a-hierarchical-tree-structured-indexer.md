# 带层次树结构索引器的次二次注意力

> **原题**: Subquadratic Attention with a Hierarchical Tree-Structured Indexer

| **摘要** | The quadratic scaling of attention is a fundamental bottleneck limiting the long-context capabilities of large language models (LLMs). A variety of approaches have attempted to circumvent this, but they either fail to match attention's expressiveness (e.g., sliding-window or linear attention variants with fixed-size state) or only achieve constant-factor speedups (e.g., sparse attention methods whose indexers themselves scale quadratically). In this paper, we derive an asymptotically subquadratic sparse attention method based on a hierarchical, tree-structured indexer, which we call Zyphra Hierarchical Attention (ZHA). ZHA constructs a tree of recursively compressed key summaries over the KV cache, which future queries traverse from the top down to retrieve a sparse subset of keys. Crucially, by iteratively growing the tree's depth, the total cost including routing and final attention grows sublinearly in sequence length, resulting in overall subquadratic (but superlinear) scaling for attention. We demonstrate that ZHA can be cheaply applied to existing pretrained models with only a short KL distillation training stage over a few billion tokens, and maintains performance on long-context retrieval and challenging reasoning, mathematical, and agentic coding evaluation tasks. Moreover, we show that our method is highly robust to tree geometry including very deep trees of up to 6 levels. Finally, we develop a detailed performance model and show that our approach scales subquadratically, dramatically outperforms competing methods beyond 1 million context, and ultimately has the potential to extend effective context windows to the hundreds of millions or billions of tokens. |

## 核心贡献

1. 指出现有绕开二次注意力的方案的两难：**滑动窗口/线性注意力**（固定大小状态）**损失表达力**，而**稀疏注意力方法的索引器本身仍是二次** —— 只拿到常数因子加速。
2. 提出 **ZHA（Zyphra Hierarchical Attention）**：在 KV cache 上构建**递归压缩的 key 摘要树**，未来的 query **自顶向下遍历**以检索稀疏的 key 子集。
3. **复杂度论证**：通过迭代加深树深度，**包含路由与最终注意力的总成本随序列长度次线性增长** —— 整体**次二次（但超线性）**缩放。
4. **可廉价应用于已有预训练模型**：只需**短程 KL 蒸馏训练阶段（数十亿 token 量级）**。
5. **鲁棒性**：对树几何高度鲁棒，包括**深达 6 层**的极深树。
6. 建立详细的**性能模型**，证明该方法次二次缩放，在**超过 100 万上下文**后大幅超越竞争方法，**潜在把有效上下文窗口扩展到数亿乃至数十亿 token**。

### 关键发现

- 这是今天**效率主线的集大成者**，与 [[2026-11-02-2610.42147-latent-sparse-attention-towards-subquadratic-ultra-long-context-attentio]]（LSA，Google DeepMind 系）构成本日**次二次注意力的双雄**。
- **两者路径不同**：ZHA 用**层次摘要树**做索引（结构化、可解释），LSA 用**学习到的隐空间层次聚类**（更灵活、需训练）。ZHA 可通过 KL 蒸馏套用现成模型，LSA 的 Native 变体需原生训练。
- 共同点是都正面回应了"**索引器自己不能是二次的**"这个关键约束 —— 这正是过去稀疏注意力方法失效的根因。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.40943-kernel-based-interpolatory-methods-for-context-compression-of-attention]]（GAIM/Q-DAIM，核方法路线）形成三种流派：树结构 / 隐空间聚类 / 核插值。**数学工具完全不同，但都在做"在不丢表达力的前提下把二次降下来"**。
- 值得注意：ZHA 的"超线性"承认了一个诚实的事实 —— **没有免费午餐**，线性注意力之所以线性是因为它放弃了表达力。
