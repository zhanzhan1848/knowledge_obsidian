| **摘要** | Existing visual preference evaluation relies on costly human annotations or preference reward models, whereas online marketplace transaction prices are readily available and reflect real purchasing behavior, offering potential low-cost supervision. However, transaction prices conflate visual preferences with non-visual factors such as provenance, demand, and competition, making aggregate accuracy insufficient to distinguish model errors, unreliable price signals, and protocol confounds. To address this identifiability challenge, we introduce MarketImage, a diagnostic framework that identifies when transaction prices reflect visual preferences, where they fail, and whether multimodal language models can recover human-supported visual preference signals. MarketImage comprises three components: (i) MarketImageArt, 266 six-image comparison sets with transaction prices and price-blind human rankings; (ii) MarketImageLens, a tie-preserving protocol that partitions 3,990 within-set image pairs into price-human agreement, conflict, and price-tie regions while separately auditing presentation-order effects; and (iii) MarketValuator, six elicitation methods spanning direct comparison, independent scoring, budget constraints, and multi-agent aggregation to evaluate signal recovery by static visual scorers and three multimodal language models. Experiments show that only 24.2% of image pairs have a non-tied price direction, of which 34.7% agree with the human majority. Within this region, HPSv2 leads at 66.4% Recoverable Accuracy; Qwen's budgeted and aggregated variants trail baselines, while GPT interface gains remain statistically unresolved. Thus, transaction prices encode recoverable visual preferences only under limited conditions and should be treated as uncertain proxies rather than ground truth. |

## 核心贡献

1. **动机**：现有视觉偏好评估依赖**昂贵的人工标注**或**偏好奖励模型**；而**在线市场的交易价格**随手可得且反映真实购买行为，是潜在的低成本监督信号。
2. **核心困难（可识别性问题）**：交易价格**混淆了视觉偏好与非视觉因素**（产地、需求、竞争）—— 因此**聚合准确率不足以区分"模型错了"、"价格信号不可靠"、"协议有混杂"**。
3. 提出 **MarketImage** 诊断框架，回答三个问题：交易价格何时反映视觉偏好、何时失效、MLLM 能否恢复有类支持的视觉偏好信号。
4. **三个组件**：
   - **MarketImageArt**：**266 组六图比较**，含交易价格与**价格盲（price-blind）人类排序**
   - **MarketImageLens**：**保平局（tie-preserving）协议**，将 **3,990 个组内图像对**划分为**价格-人类一致 / 冲突 / 价格平局**三个区域，并**单独审计呈现顺序效应**
   - **MarketValuator**：**六种引出方法**（直接比较、独立打分、预算约束、多 agent 聚合），评估静态视觉评分器与**三个多模态语言模型**的信号恢复能力

## 技术方案

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 价格盲人类排序 | 剥离价格信息的真值参照 |
| 保平局划分 | 一致 / 冲突 / 平局三区域 |
| 顺序效应审计 | 呈现顺序作为独立混杂因素 |
| 六种引出方法 | 比较/打分/预算/多 agent 聚合 |
| 可识别性框架 | 区分三类失败来源 |

### 关键发现

- **"价格盲人类排序"** 是解决混杂的关键设计：有了不看价格的人类偏好作参照，才能**判断价格信号本身是否有效**，而不是把价格当真值。
- **"保平局划分"** 反映了统计上的诚实：市场价格**大量并列**（同价商品），把这些当作"冲突"会污染整个分析。
- **"单独审计呈现顺序效应"** —— 图像比较任务中，位置偏好是经典混杂（第一张/最后一张被选更多），很多偏好研究忽略它。
- **可识别性框架（区分模型错 / 信号不可靠 / 协议混杂）** 是本文的方法论内核，与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41803-what-standard-evaluations-hide-about-detectors-of-hijacked-llm-agents-ra]]（评测隐藏了什么）同属"**先厘清信号来源，再谈方法比较**"。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.38977-beyond-passing-tests-a-systematic-review-and-controlled-re-evaluation-of]] 的"单一自动化信号会被 game"直接相关 —— 交易价格就是一个**看似客观实则可被混杂**的信号。
