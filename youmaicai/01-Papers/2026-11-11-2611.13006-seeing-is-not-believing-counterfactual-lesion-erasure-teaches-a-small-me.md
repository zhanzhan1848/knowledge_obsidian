## 核心要点
1. 指出医学 VLM 常常因为**问题里提到了某个发现**就报告该发现，而标准基准测不出来。
2. 从公开分割数据集构造**反事实视图**（inpainting 擦除病灶、同形状对照编辑、原始无病灶图），**免人工标注**。
3. **evidence sensitivity gap (ESG)** = 擦除后的「缺失或转述」回答率 − 对照编辑后的该比率——**对忽略图像或对两种编辑同等反应的模型，ESG 为零**。
4. **MedScope-4B**（Qwen3.5-4B + LoRA + 图像工具）在**507 例内部案例、5 种影像类型**上达 **ESG 0.758**，擦除病灶的虚假声称率 **9.6%**，而 frontier 模型为 **0.27–0.47** 与 **41–62%**（全部 p<0.001），真实病灶准确率无显著损失。
5. **作者诚实报告失败**：没有反事实视图时 ESG 降至 0.279±0.107；**在四个外部中心的全新外部测试上 MedScope-4B 检出 0/48 例气胸片**，净效用低于 gemini-4-argon（−0.36 [−0.50, −0.23]）；**匹配对照编辑未被证明重要**；配对视图一致性目标未击败等算力对照。
6. 结论：**在擦除与编辑图像上训练给小型模型一种证据门控的处置倾向，这是提示无法给予 frontier 模型的；但它不改善感知，罕见关键发现仍需覆盖。**

## 摘要（原文）
Medical vision-language models (VLMs) often report a finding because the question names it; standard
benchmarks miss this. From public segmentation datasets we build counterfactual views without manual
annotation: the lesion erased by inpainting, a same-shape control edit elsewhere, and unedited
lesion-free images. The evidence sensitivity gap (ESG) is the rate of absent-or-refer answers after
erasure minus the rate after the control edit; it is zero for a model that ignores the image or
reacts to both edits alike. MedScope-4B (Qwen3.5-4B, LoRA, image tools) was trained on label-derived
trajectories, selected by a pre-registered rule and tested once against nine frontier and six open
VLMs; four pre-registered checks then probed the mechanism. On 507 internal cases across five
imaging types, MedScope-4B reached ESG 0.758 and claimed an erased lesion in 9.6% of cases, against
0.27-0.47 and 41-62% for the frontier models (all p<0.001), without significant loss of accuracy on
real lesions. Without counterfactual views ESG fell to 0.279+-0.107 (three seeds); the effect
replicated on MedGemma 1.5 4B and two unseen inpainting tools. At four external centres the
advantage held but frontier models detected more real lesions; on a fresh external test MedScope-4B
detected 0 of 48 pneumothorax films, with net utility below gemini-4-argon's (-0.36 [-0.50, -0.23]).
Matching the control edit was not shown to matter; over three seeds no fresh-external ESG advantage
over removal-based DPO was established; a paired-view consistency objective did not beat a matched-
compute control, while that continuation alone cut erased-lesion claims on unseen tools at every
seed, at a cost in sensitivity at one. Training on erased and edited images gives a small model an
evidence-gated disposition that prompting does not give frontier models; it does not improve
perception, and rare critical findings need coverage.
