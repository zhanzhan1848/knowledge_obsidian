## 核心要点
1. 指出自改进系统的有效性高度依赖**验证器（verifier）**质量，而多数任务的验证器靠**手写**，昂贵且无法扩展到本来就没有验证器的任务。**Veritas** 是一个**自动生成验证器**的 harness，与通用智能体 harness 相比额外引入三个机制：**evidence header**（声明验证器将观测到什么）、**领域规则库**（补齐任务未言明的隐含规则）、以及**撰写原则**（部分由自我改进循环迭代精修）。
2. 在**六个基准**上评测：其中**四个按执行结果打分**（论文先对其官方验证器做认证，certify），**两个做成对回答比较**。在**固定模型**的条件下，Veritas 生成的验证器在**全部四个执行打分基准**上都比另外**七种 harness** 的验证器与官方判定**一致性更好**；在两个成对比较基准上则**达到或接近最优**。
3. 跨模型家族与跨机制消融显示：Veritas 的验证器在**另外三个模型家族中的两个**上也优于该厂商自研 harness 的验证器；其三项机制（evidence header、领域规则库、撰写原则）在**三种 harness × 12 个基准组合中有 9 个**（按执行打分）带来改进。

## 摘要（原文）
The effectiveness of a self-improving system relies heavily on the quality of its verifier, which judges
the outcome of each improvement round. However, the verifiers of most tasks are written by hand, which is
expensive and does not scale to tasks that come without one. We propose Veritas, a harness that generates
verifiers automatically. Unlike general-purpose agent harnesses, Veritas adds three mechanisms for verifier
generation: an evidence header stating what the verifier will observe, a library of domain rules that tasks
leave unstated, and authoring principles, partly refined by a self-improvement loop. We evaluate on six
benchmarks: four scored by execution, whose official verifiers we first certify, and two that compare pairs of
responses. With the model fixed, the verifiers Veritas generates agree better with the official verdicts than
those of seven other harnesses on all four execution-scored benchmarks. On the two pairwise benchmarks they
are at or near the best. They also agree better than those of the vendor's own harness for two of three other
model families. Its mechanisms also improve 9 of 12 harness-benchmark pairs scored by execution across three
harnesses.
