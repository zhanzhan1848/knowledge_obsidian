## 核心要点
1. 主张**微调留下的决定性属性是从基座继承的**——词表、知识、文化对齐、记忆数据与许可证。
2. 测量覆盖 **31 种语言、7 个 2023–2024 年的分词器**，语料为《世界人权宣言》27 条文章，**显式校正 unknown-token 崩塌**。
3. **阿姆哈拉语溢价在旧分词器达 10 倍，在最新商用分词器仍有 6.8 倍**；南美原住民语言在全球分词器下付出 **1.6–2.7 倍**。
4. 用葡萄牙语构建的词表能让 **13 种拉丁字母系全球南方语言中的 11 种**在所有受测全球分词器之上降低溢价（瓜拉尼语、克丘亚语约 **24%**）。
5. 提出**八项黑盒主权审计**（买家无需权重即可运行），并给出**制度性论证**：在能源与责任约束下，**每焦耳准确率、校准度与选择性风险应取代原始准确率**，且指标必须**不能被弃答灌水**。

## 摘要（原文）
Most language models deployed outside the United States and China are fine-tuned descendants of a
small number of pretrained families. We argue that fine-tuning leaves the decisive properties of a
model inherited from its base: vocabulary, knowledge, cultural alignment, memorized data and
license. We contribute (i) an updated, reproducible measurement of the tokenization premium across
31 languages and 7 tokenizers released in 2023-2024, on 27 articles of the Universal Declaration of
Human Rights, with explicit correction for unknown-token collapse. Premiums reach 10x for Amharic in
older tokenizers and remain at 6.8x in the newest commercial one; Indigenous South American
languages pay between 1.6x and 2.7x under global tokenizers. A vocabulary built from Portuguese
lowers the premium for 11 of 13 Latin-script Global South languages relative to every global
tokenizer tested, by about 24% for Guarani and Quechua. (ii) An eight-test black-box sovereignty
audit that a buyer can run without access to weights. (iii) A regime argument: under energy and
liability constraints, accuracy per joule, calibration and selective risk should replace raw
accuracy as design objectives, with metrics that abstention cannot inflate. We close with
preliminary internal measurements from a proprietary architecture built around these objectives,
reported with their limitations. Data and measurement code are included.
