## 核心要点
1. **SLIC（Small LM Iterating on Circuits）**：强化微调框架，把 4B 规模**可本地部署**的语言模型训练用于闭环模拟电路优化。
2. 结构化电路表示 + 离散化相对更新动作 + 领域与成功门控的多目标奖励 + **Reward-Guided Rollout Steering (RGRS)**（把每步模拟器反馈同时转为策略学习信号与在线 rollout 构造信号）。
3. **反常识结果**：**SLIC-Random 在分布内四项规格 Pass@1 达 87.1%（八轮内），超过 763B 的 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash Think 达 48.5 个百分点**。
4. 留出拓扑上 **SLIC-Softmax 达 72.6% Pass@1**；策略在**五个未见技术节点**上无需额外微调即可迁移。
5. 迁移到**商用 TSMC 65nm CMOS PDK**，留出拓扑 Pass@1 为 **49.9%**，且**本地推理不外传专有数据**。
6. 测试时模拟器交互增加可继续提升：32 轮预算下达 **83.2%** 留出拓扑 Pass@1。

## 摘要（原文）
Analog integrated circuit (IC) design is a multi-turn, simulator-grounded engineering task that
demands iterative reasoning over coupled performance tradeoffs, yet proprietary process design kits
(PDKs) make hosted large language model workflows difficult to deploy in practical design settings.
We present SLIC (Small LM Iterating on Circuits), a reinforcement fine-tuning framework that trains
a 4B-scale, locally deployable language model for closed-loop analog circuit optimization. SLIC
pairs structured circuit representations with discretized relative-update actions, domain- and
success-gated multiobjective rewards, and Reward-Guided Rollout Steering (RGRS), which turns per-
step simulator feedback into signals for both policy learning and online rollout construction in
this sparse, highly constrained domain. We further release an open, goal-conditioned benchmark of 41
amplifier topologies with standardized protocols for in-distribution validation, held-out topology
evaluation, and cross-technology-node transfer. Trained solely with open source 65 nm CMOS
predictive technology models (PTM), SLIC-Random achieves 87.1% in-distribution four-specification
Pass@1 within eight turns, surpassing the 763B DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash Think by 48.5 percentage points.
Beyond the training distribution, SLIC-Softmax reaches 72.6% Pass@1 on held-out topologies, and the
trained policies consistently transfer across five unseen technology nodes without additional fine-
tuning. This transfer extends to a commercial TSMC 65 nm CMOS PDK, where SLIC-Random achieves 49.9%
held-out-topology Pass@1, demonstrating practical deployment through local inference without
transmitting proprietary data to external LLM services. Performance further scales with test-time
simulator interactions, reaching up to 83.2% held-out-topology Pass@1 at a 32-turn budget.
