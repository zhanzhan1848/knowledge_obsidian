## 核心要点
1. 测量 **9 个公开分词器**（GPT-4、GPT-4o、Qwen2.5、DeepSeek-V3、Mistral-Nemo、4 个 Sarvam）在 FLORES-200 全部 **25 种印度语言**（2,009 条专业翻译句）与 **13,732 条**人类翻译 Hinglish 社交媒体对（PHINC）上的 token 差距。
2. GPT-4o 上同等内容需英语的 **1.57 倍**（印地语）到 **5.01 倍**（奥迪亚语），**桑塔利语在 Ol Chiki 文字下 13.7 倍**；GPT-4 最高 **12.8 倍**。
3. **Sarvam-30B/105B 是唯一在所有语言上都低于 2.6 倍的第三方分词器**（中位数 1.53）。
4. 成本估算（2026-11-05 官方价）：10 万会话/月的 GPT-4o 支持机器人英文约 **₹56,640**、马拉地语约 **₹102,829**（作者标注为估算）。
5. 对照公开授权的印地语/马拉地语形态切分集：**没有分词器把超过 35% 的词内边界放在形态素边界上**（边界 F1 最高 0.48）。

## 摘要（原文）
Commercial language model APIs bill per token, so a tokenizer that needs more tokens for the same
content in one language makes that language more expensive, slower, and able to fit less text in a
fixed context window. We measure this disparity for nine public tokenizers, including those of
GPT-4, GPT-4o, Qwen2.5, DeepSeek-V3, Mistral-Nemo and four Sarvam models, on all 25 languages of
India available in FLORES-200 (2,009 professionally translated sentences aligned with English) and
on 13,732 human-translated Hinglish-English social media pairs from PHINC. On GPT-4o's tokenizer the
same content needs between 1.57 times (Hindi) and 5.01 times (Odia) the tokens of English, and 13.7
times for Santali in Ol Chiki script; GPT-4's tokenizer needs up to 12.8 times. Sarvam-30B/105B is
the only third-party tokenizer that stays below 2.6 times on every language (median 1.53). In an
illustrative scenario using official list prices accessed on 5 November 2026 and stated assumptions,
a support bot handling 100,000 chats a month on GPT-4o would cost about INR 56,640 in English and
INR 102,829 in Marathi; these are estimates, not measured production costs. Against an openly
licensed Hindi and Marathi morphological segmentation set, no tokenizer places more than 35% of its
word-internal boundaries on morpheme boundaries (boundary F1 at most 0.48). In a companion paper we
present a tokenizer that reduces this tax. Code, pinned artifacts and raw token counts are available
at https://github.com/HavonAI/indic-token-tax.
