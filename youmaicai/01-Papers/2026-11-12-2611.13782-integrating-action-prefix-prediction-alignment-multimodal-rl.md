## 核心要点
1. **问题诊断**：已有方法 Multimodal Joint Predictive Representation (MJPR) 的两个弱点——其**递归 rollout 依赖更早的预测结果**（误差会沿时间步累积），且其**成对对比学习只建模模态间的对应关系**（缺少观测锚点处的对齐约束）。
2. **提出 Improved Predictive Representation (IPR)**：结合 **action prefix（动作前缀）预测**与**在观测锚点处的跨模态对齐**（基于 Contrastive Multimodal learning）。在每个预测视野上，模型**直接从观测锚点 + 对应的动作序列**预测未来表征，绕开 MJPR 的递归依赖。
3. **实验设置与结果**：两种裁剪配置 **IPR-S**（最小保留裁剪面积 10%）与 **IPR-H**（50%）；在 **5 个 DeepMind Control Suite 任务**上，IPR-H 的平均跨任务回报比基线**高 10.0%**（视觉+深度图像）以及**高 3.4%**（视觉图像+位置向量），且两种配置的相对优劣随任务而变。在 **Webots** 中，两种 IPR 配置的最终平均回报均优于**无 LiDAR** 的基线，但在 **有 LiDAR 时不再领先**。

## 摘要（原文）
Learning representations from multimodal observations is difficult in reinforcement learning with limited
interaction data. Multimodal Joint Predictive Representation combines multimodal latent prediction with
contrastive learning across modalities, but its recursive rollout depends on earlier predictions and its
pairwise alignment focuses on correspondence between modalities. We propose Improved Predictive
Representation, which combines prediction from action prefixes with alignment at the observed anchor based on
Contrastive Multimodal learning. At each horizon, it predicts future representations directly from the
observed anchor and the corresponding sequence of actions. We evaluate two crop configurations: IPR-S with a
minimum retained crop area of 10%, and IPR-H with 50%. Across five DeepMind Control Suite tasks, IPR-H
achieves an average return across tasks that is 10.0% higher than the baseline with visual and depth images
and 3.4% higher with visual images and position vectors. The relative performance of the two configurations
varies across tasks. In Webots, both IPR configurations achieve higher mean final returns than the baseline
without LiDAR, but not when LiDAR is available.
