## 核心要点
1. **权重空间编程（WSP）**可直接生成神经网络权重而无需基于梯度的优化；本文把 WSP 扩展到**偏微分方程求解**。
2. **Physics2Weight (PtW)**：基于控制方程与初边值条件**直接生成神经网络权重并编辑架构**。
3. 引入**物理约束 prompting** 与**物理引导的架构编辑**，结合 WSP 的迭代权重精炼。
4. 在四个 PDE（**热、对流、Burgers、Allen–Cahn**）上解误差低于**常规物理信息神经网络（PINN）**。
5. 同时**揭示了在非线性 PDE 上满足控制方程的局限**。

## 摘要（原文）
Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) have enabled weight-space programming (WSP), which
directly generates neural network weights without gradient-based optimization. However, extending
WSP to physical problems remains challenging, particularly in achieving both solution accuracy and
physical consistency. In this paper, we propose Physics2Weight (PtW), a framework that extends WSP
to solving partial differential equations by directly generating neural network weights and editing
architectures based on governing equations and initial and boundary conditions. PtW introduces
physics-constrained prompting and physics-guided architecture editing, combined with iterative
weight refinement from WSP, to improve solution accuracy and physical consistency without gradient-
based optimization. Experiments on four PDEs, namely the heat, advection, Burgers', and Allen--Cahn
equations, demonstrate lower solution errors than conventional physics-informed neural networks,
while revealing limitations in satisfying the governing equations for nonlinear PDEs.
