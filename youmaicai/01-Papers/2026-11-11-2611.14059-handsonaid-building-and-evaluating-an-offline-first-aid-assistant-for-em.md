## 核心要点
1. **HandsOnAid**：离线急救助手，**4-bit Gemma 2B** + **QLoRA** 在 **FirstAidQA（5,550 对）**上微调，比较七种配置（含基座、4-bit、8-bit，及长度下限/安全门/检索变体）。
2. **41 对**经医学审阅、遵循 **ANZCOR 指南**的问题库，严格 **0–5 分** rubric 与 **12 条安全覆盖**。
3. **四个前沿 LLM judge**（GPT-5.6 Sol、Claude Opus 5、DeepSeek V4 Pro、GLM-5.3）盲评全部答案，用 **45 条隐藏对照**检查 judge 可靠性。
4. 微调把平均分**提升 +1.08 至 2.34**；最佳配置 **2.51/5**（4-bit 微调 + 检索）。
5. 一位医学专业人士对 40 条答案的盲评**与 LLM judge 平均分相似**——这是对 judge 有效性的一个外部佐证。
6. **关键词审计发现训练语料对 ANZCOR 指南中若干 rubric 必需细节覆盖有限**，作者归因于训练数据信息缺失导致低分。
7. **Pixel 7 Pro**、六 CPU 线程、**无网络**：首个可显示文本 **6.86 秒**，完整答案平均 **14.88 秒**（十个问题均值）。

## 摘要（原文）
Emergencies can occur where there is no phone signal and no ambulance nearby. A small language model
on a phone can be helpful in such situations, but its advice needs to be safe and accurate. We built
HandsOnAid, a 4-bit Gemma 2B model fine-tuned with QLoRA on FirstAidQA dataset, a corpus of 5,550
first-aid question-answer pairs. We compared seven configurations, including the base, 4-bit and
8-bit fine-tuned models, and versions with a length floor, safety gate, or retrieval. We used a
41-pair question-answer bank with medically reviewed references that followed ANZCOR guidelines, and
a strict 0-5 score rubric, with twelve safety overrides. Four frontier LLM judges (GPT-5.6 Sol,
Claude Opus 5, DeepSeek V4 Pro, GLM-5.3) blindly scored every answer, with 45 hidden controls used
to check judge reliability. Fine-tuning improved the mean score by +1.08 over the base model to
2.34. The best configuration scored 2.51 out of 5 which was the fine-tuned 4-bit model with
retrieval. A medical professional also conducted a blind evaluation of 40 answers across the base,
the 4-bit fine-tuned, and the retrieval configurations, and the scores were similar to the LLM
judges' average scores. A keyword audit found limited coverage of several details of ANZCOR
guidelines in the training corpus, that are required by the rubric, which suggests that missing
information in the training data could be a cause of the low scores. On a smartphone device (Pixel 7
Pro) with six CPU threads and no network connection, the 4-bit fine-tuned model produced its first
displayable text in 6.86 seconds and a complete answer in 14.88 seconds on average over ten
questions.
