## 核心要点
1. 把注意力重新表述为**接收器与上下文 token 的同步**：每个历史 token 是一个振荡器，状态携带内容、频率随年龄漂移；当前 token 通过内容选择它们（天线对准 query）、通过位置设置频率（接收器旋钮调频）。
2. **求解 Kuramoto 问题得到闭式注意力层**，包含两个相：**高于临界耦合**时接收器锁定、权重归一化；**低于临界耦合**时保持静默、权重和**小于 1**（即「弃权」）。
3. **训练出的网络用两个相来选择、弃权与计数**，**在理论预测之处失败**，并能**远超训练长度继续阅读**。
4. 叙事极具张力：「注意力的一切各归其位——它调准该听什么、何时听，没relevant信号时保持静默。」

## 摘要（原文）
Attention, the core of language models, is a digital calculation over tokens: an exponential selects
them, a division normalizes and a positional code sets the order. We show that attention can instead
emerge from the synchronization of a receiver with its context tokens. Each past token is an
oscillator whose state carries its content and whose frequency drifts with its age. The present
token selects them by content, aligning an antenna with its query, and by position, setting the
frequency of a receiver with its dial. Solving Kuramoto's problem for this receiver yields an
attention layer in closed form with two phases: above a critical coupling set by the context, the
receiver locks and its weights normalize; below it, it stays silent and its weights sum to less than
one. Trained networks use both phases to select, abstain and count, fail where the theory predicts
and keep reading far beyond the training length. Each part of attention finds its place in one
device, a radio head: it tunes in to what to hear and when, and stays silent when nothing relevant
is on air.
