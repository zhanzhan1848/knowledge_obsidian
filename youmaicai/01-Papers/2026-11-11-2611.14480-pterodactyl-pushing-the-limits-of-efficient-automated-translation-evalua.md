## 摘要（原文）
In this paper, we present 1020 Group's submission to the WMT26 shared task on automated quality
evaluation. Our system, Pterodactyl, was engineered specifically for subtasks 2 and 3, that is,
segment-level quality score prediction and the detection of error-free segments. Pterodactyl is a
highly customizable, ultra-low cost system optimized for binary classification in translation triage
and data curation. It is based on an LLM-as-a-judge approach, with prompt engineering and model
selection guided by ROC analysis. We also developed several variants of Pterodactyl that were
specifically adapted for subtasks 3.
