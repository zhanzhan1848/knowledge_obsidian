# UpLoop：零额外预训练算力下用上循环训练 Looped Transformer

| **摘要** | Looped language models can strengthen reasoning through repeated computation with shared weights, increasing execution depth without adding independent parameters. However, each additional pass increases training floating-point operations (FLOPs), making these gains more expensive to obtain. To address this cost, we propose UpLoop, a pretraining schedule that uses upcycling to train looped Transformers while exactly matching a standard dense baseline in final parameter count, total training tokens, and training FLOPs. Specifically, UpLoop begins with a shallow Transformer, progressively grows its depth by copying learned layers, and uses the computation saved early to support repeated execution of a shared middle block later in training. Experiments across model scales show improvements in language modeling and downstream performance, with the average score across 22 downstream tasks increasing from 22.99 to 27.52 at 1.3B parameters. These gains also extend to mathematical reasoning after supervised fine-tuning. |

## 核心贡献

1. **背景**：Looped 语言模型通过**共享权重的重复计算**强化推理，提升执行深度而**不增加独立参数**。
2. **成本问题**：但每一遍额外 pass 都**增加训练 FLOPs**，使这些增益变得更昂贵。
3. 提出 **UpLoop**：一个**预训练调度**，通过**上循环（upcycling）**训练 looped Transformer，同时**精确匹配标准稠密基线**的**最终参数量、总训练 token 数、训练 FLOPs**。
4. **机制**：从**浅层 Transformer 起步**，通过**复制已学层逐步加深**，并把**早期节省的计算量**用于**后期重复执行一个共享的中间块**。
5. **结果**：跨模型规模在语言建模与下游表现上均有提升，**1.3B 参数时 22 个下游任务的平均分从 22.99 → 27.52**；**增益在 SFT 后的数学推理上同样成立**。

## 技术方案

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 上循环 (upcycling) | 浅层起步，复制已学层逐步加深 |
| 共享中间块重复执行 | 后期用节省算力做重复计算 |
| 严格算力对齐 | 参数/token/FLOPs 三项精确匹配稠密基线 |
| Looped Transformer | 共享权重重复计算 |

### 关键发现

- **"精确匹配 FLOPs"是本文的方法论要点** —— 这与 [[2026-11-02-2610.38977-beyond-passing-tests-a-systematic-review-and-controlled-re-evaluation-of]] 的"匹配算力预算重评"是**同一科学规范的两种应用**（一个在综述里要求，一个在方法里实践）。今日两篇论文共享这一规范，是值得注意的社区共识形成。
- **"早期省下的算力后期花掉"** 是一个漂亮的调度思想：深度在前半程是负担，在后半程是资产 —— 通过上循环让模型**按需变深**。
- **22.99 → 27.52** 在 1.3B 上是很显著的提升（+19.7% 相对）。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.42149-latent-reasoning-in-continuous-space-for-unified-multimodal-models]] 呼应：两者都在问"**推理应该放在哪里做**" —— UpLoop 放"更多层"，LARC 放"连续空间"。

## 局限性

- 1.3B 规模的结论外推到更大模型需谨慎
