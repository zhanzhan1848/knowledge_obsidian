## 核心要点
1. 挑战多模态遥感预训练的一个隐含前提：严格**实例级（instance-level）跨模态配对**被视为学到可迁移表征的前提。论文提出 **SemPair（Semantic Pairing）**——一种**配对松弛**的预训练策略，用**类别一致（class-consistent）的跨实例配对**替换精确的实例配对，从而把对应关系从实例级下放到语义级。
2. 为刻画"对应关系松弛"的影响，作者构造了一个**潜在因子模型（latent-factor model）**，把跨模态依赖拆分为**共享语义结构**与**实例特定相关性（instance-specific correlation）**，并证明 SemPair **保留了共享语义结构**，同时在期望意义上**边缘化掉实例特定的跨模态相关性**；同时提出 **MixPair（Mixed Pairing）** 作为语义配对与精确配对之间的**受控插值**，用于沿连续谱研究对应粒度。
3. 大量实验一致表明：**SemPair 的迁移性能与精确配对（ExaPair）相当**，说明精确实例对应并非有效多模态遥感预训练的先决条件；而 **MixPair 揭示了下游迁移与实例配对比例之间的非单调关系**。结论把**跨模态对应粒度**确立为可迁移多模态表征学习的一个关键设计维度，并给出**降低对昂贵的实例对齐数据依赖**以扩展预训练规模的实用路径。

## 摘要（原文）
Multimodal remote sensing pretraining commonly relies on exact instance-level correspondence across
modalities, implicitly treating strictly paired observations as a prerequisite for learning transferable
representations. In this work, we revisit this assumption by relaxing cross-modal correspondence from the
instance level to the semantic level. We introduce Semantic Pairing (SemPair), a pairing-relaxed pretraining
strategy that replaces exact instance pairs with class-consistent cross-instance pairs. To characterize the
effect of correspondence relaxation, we formulate a latent-factor model that separates cross-modal dependence
into shared semantic structure and instance-specific correlation, showing that SemPair preserves the former
while marginalizing instance-specific cross-modal correlations in expectation. We further introduce Mixed
Pairing (MixPair) as a controlled interpolation between semantic and exact pairing to study correspondence
granularity along a continuous spectrum. Extensive experiments consistently show that SemPair achieves
transfer performance comparable to exact pairing (ExaPair), while MixPair reveals a non-monotonic
relationship between downstream transfer and the instance-pairing ratio. These results demonstrate that exact
instance correspondence is not a prerequisite for effective multimodal remote sensing pretraining and establish
cross-modal correspondence granularity as an important design dimension for transferable multimodal
representation learning. More broadly, this finding suggests a practical route toward scaling multimodal
pretraining with reduced dependence on costly instance-aligned data.
