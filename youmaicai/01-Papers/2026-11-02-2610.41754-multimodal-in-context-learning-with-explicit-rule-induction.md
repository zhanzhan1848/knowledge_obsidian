| **摘要** | A central challenge in multimodal in-context learning (MICL) arises from tasks that are ill-defined without context and require uncovering the underlying rules from demonstrations. Existing methods predominantly treat MICL as direct input-output mapping, overlooking the importance of explicitly inducing and reasoning over such rules, limiting both performance and interpretability. We propose ERIM, Explicit Rule-Induction for Multimodal In-Context Learning, a unified framework that enhances MICL by explicitly inducing the latent rules governing demonstration samples. Leveraging the reasoning ability of multi-modal large language model (MLLM), ERIM constructs rule-centric MICL datasets, introduces explicit supervision for rule induction, and fine-tunes MLLM to perform explicit rule-induction via a two-stage training strategy that combines mixed cold-start supervised fine-tuning with reinforcement learning. Extensive experiments show consistent performance improvements across diverse MICL settings and demonstrate that correctly inducing underlying rules directly leads to stronger and more stable performance. |

## 核心贡献

1. **问题定位**：多模态上下文学习（MICL）中最难的任务是**没有上下文就无法定义**、必须从示例中**挖掘潜在规则**的任务。
2. **现有方法的缺陷**：把 MICL 单纯当作**输入-输出映射**，忽视**显式归纳并推理规则**的重要性，限制了性能与可解释性。
3. 提出 **ERIM**（Explicit Rule-Induction for Multimodal In-Context Learning）：**显式归纳支配示例样本的潜在规则**。
4. **方法构成**：
   - 借助 MLLM 的推理能力**构建以规则为中心的 MICL 数据集**
   - **为规则归纳提供显式监督**
   - 通过**两阶段训练**微调 MLLM 执行显式规则归纳：**混合冷启动 SFT + 强化学习**
5. **关键结论**：**正确归纳出底层规则直接带来更强、更稳定的性能** —— 性能与可解释性同时改善。

### 关键发现

- **"把 ICL 从映射学习变成规则学习"** 是一个视角转换，与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41089-circe-divines-llm-task-complexity-from-constraint-structure]]（CIRCE 从约束结构推断任务复杂度）同源 —— 两篇都主张**结构是答案**，且都用了便宜 LLM 做结构抽取。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41951-aligning-mllms-with-the-latent-structure-of-human-cognition-via-behavior]] 形成有趣对照：ERIM 让模型**归纳任务中的规则**，CogAlign 让模型**对齐人类认知中的语义维度** —— 一个是任务内规则，一个是人类先验。
- 与 [[2026-11-01-2610.39182-mechanistic-insights-rl-trained-pattern-exploitation]]（RL 只是重排特征）形成张力：如果 ERIM 的 RL 阶段真的让模型**获得了规则归纳这一新机制**，那"RL 不创造新概念"的结论就有边界条件 —— **取决于 RL 是否有结构化的中间奖励信号**。这与 2026-11-01 那天的 H²R（用子问题层级作中间奖励）异曲同工。
- "可解释性"在这里不是事后解释工具，而是**训练目标本身** —— 这比事后解释路线更有前景。
