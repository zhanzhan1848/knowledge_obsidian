## 核心要点
1. **范式转变：从"固定 tactic 生成循环"到"coding-agent 式连续推理"**。已有的 LLM 证明合成方法把模型限制在僵硬的 tactic-generation loop 中，由外围系统掌管前提检索、证明状态管理与回溯；ProofCoder 面向 **Rocq** 证明器，在**整个证明搜索过程中持续推理**，用**证明助手反馈**决定何时收集额外信息、检视证明状态、**修改已生成的证明片段**或**回溯到更早的证明位置**。
2. **可在 tactic 级或整证明（whole-proof）粒度上迭代构造、修复、精炼证明**。在 **AutoRocq Benchmark 的 280 个高难度验证任务**上，最佳配置把证明成功率从 SOTA 的 AutoRocq 系统的 **47% 提升到 60%**，即 **27.7% 的相对提升**，且**成本约为其一半**。
3. **消融表明连续推理是关键设计选择**：去掉连续推理后，agent **解决的引理更少**、**成本显著更高**、**回溯频率增加 4 到 6 倍**，且**更频繁地丢弃自己的修改**。整证明生成与 tactic 级生成的成功率相近，但**采取不同的搜索与修复策略**——说明有效的自动软件验证需要的不只是更好的 tactic 选择，**连续推理 + 反馈驱动的证明修订**才是稳健证明合成的关键成分。

## 摘要（原文）
Formal verification using proof assistants provides strong correctness guarantees for software, but
constructing proofs remains labor-intensive and requires significant expertise. Recent approaches based on large
language models (LLMs) have shown promise in automating proof synthesis. However, these approaches typically
constrain the model to a rigid tactic-generation loop, in which the surrounding system controls premise
retrieval, proof-state management, and backtracking. We introduce ProofCoder, an agentic proof-synthesis system
for Rocq that approaches theorem proving from a coding-agent perspective. Rather than generating tactics within
a fixed loop, ProofCoder reasons continuously throughout proof search and uses proof-assistant feedback to
decide when to gather additional information, inspect proof states, revise previously generated proof
fragments, or backtrack to an earlier point in the proof. This allows it to iteratively construct, repair, and
refine proofs, at either tactic-level or whole-proof granularity. We evaluate ProofCoder on 280 challenging
verification tasks from the AutoRocq Benchmark. On the best-performing configurations, ProofCoder increases
proof success from 47% to 60% over the state-of-theart AutoRocq system, corresponding to a 27.7% relative
improvement at approximately half the cost. Our ablations identify continuous reasoning as a key design
choice: without it, the agent solves fewer lemmas at substantially higher cost, backtracks four to six times
more often, and more frequently discards its own revisions. Whole-proof and tactic-level generation achieve
similar success rates but follow distinct search and repair strategies. These results suggest that effective
automated software verification requires more than improved tactic selection: continuous reasoning and
feedback-guided proof revision are critical ingredients for robust proof synthesis.
