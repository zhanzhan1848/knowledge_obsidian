## 核心要点
1. 用神经科学的 **off-state**（瞬态神经元静默）做类比，在 GSM8K 上评估 **250 对推导**、两个 8B 模型（Qwen3-8B、LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct），对比**聚集的头掩蔽突发**与**预算匹配的 i.i.d. 弥散 dropout**。
2. 起始参数 **B=0.10**（调度约 **5.7%** 脆弱 head-token 体积）时，Qwen3-8B 从弥散噪声下的 **91.2%** 跌到 **78.8%**（**Δ=−12.4 pp**，精确 McNemar **p=5.5×10⁻⁶**），LLaMA 从 **84.0%** 到 **76.8%**（Δ=−7.2 pp，p=0.0198）。
3. **把同样的累计预算通过分层五分位匹配均匀分散到生成步上，退化显著更轻**（Qwen 保留 91.2%、LLaMA 保留 84.0%）。
4. 探索性析因解离（N=100）：单步 dropout 密度与急剧下降相关（Qwen **−10.0 pp**，Holm p=0.0425），**多 token 连续性效应不明确**（−7.0 pp，p_Holm=0.1892）。
5. 误差分析：Qwen 失败是**格式响应内的推导错误**（条件准确率 −11.4 pp）；LLaMA 有 **22.2%** 不一致损失**缺少最终答案格式行**（条件差距收窄至 −5.0 pp）。
6. 结论：**自回归推理链对聚集性结构扰动特别脆弱**，并给出 transformer 容错性的经验基准。

## 摘要（原文）
During extended wakefulness, mammalian neocortical networks experience localized periods of
transient neuronal silence termed off-states. Here, we examine how analogous spatiotemporally
concentrated disruptions affect sequential reasoning in autoregressive Large Language Models (LLMs).
Evaluating 250 paired derivations on the GSM8K benchmark across two 8B models (Qwen3-8B and
LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct), we contrast clustered bursts of attention head masking against budget-
matched diffuse i.i.d. dropout. Concentrated bursts induce substantial reasoning degradation: at
onset parameter $\mathcal{B}=0.10$ (scheduling $\sim 5.7\%$ of vulnerable head-token volume),
Qwen3-8B accuracy drops from $91.2\%$ under diffuse noise to $78.8\%$ under burst dropout ($Δ=
-12.4$ percentage points [pp], exact McNemar $p = 5.5 \times 10^{-6}$), while LLaMA-3.1-8B-Instruct
drops from $84.0\%$ to $76.8\%$ ($Δ= -7.2$ pp, $p = 0.0198$). Conversely, dispersing the identical
cumulative budget uniformly across generation steps via stratified quintile matching yields
substantially milder degradation (retaining $91.2\%$ in Qwen and $84.0\%$ in LLaMA). In an
exploratory factorial dissociation ($N=100$), single-step dropout density is associated with an
acute drop in a single schedule draw ($-10.0$ pp on Qwen, $p_{\text{holm}} = 0.0425$), while multi-
token continuity exhibits an inconclusive compounding trend ($-7.0$ pp, $p_{\text{holm}} = 0.1892$).
Error analysis reveals that Qwen failures reflect derivation errors within formatted responses
(conditional accuracy drop of $-11.4$ pp), whereas in LLaMA $22.2\%$ of discordant losses lack a
final answer format line (conditional gap narrows to $-5.0$ pp). These findings demonstrate that
autoregressive reasoning chains are particularly vulnerable to concentrated structural disruptions,
providing empirical benchmarks for fault tolerance in transformer architectures.
