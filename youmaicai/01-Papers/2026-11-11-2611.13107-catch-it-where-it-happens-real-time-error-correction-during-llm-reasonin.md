## 核心要点
1. 提出 **online, deterministic verification**：许多推理步骤可由**代码**验证，因此可在生成时**实时验证**，**无需任何模型调用或 GPU 占用**。
2. 检查失败时**指出错误并让模型从同一位置继续**，而非从头重来。
3. 在符号数学上（推导中已含显式表达式，CAS 毫秒级检查），**ASyMoB 基准**上三个开源权重模型准确率提升 **1.4–5.6 pp**，错误答案净减少 **1.8–14.9%**，token 消耗仅增 **6–7%**。
4. 验证器与模型独立，**每个新模型都能立即受益**，收益随确定性检查器覆盖的推理步骤种类增长而增长。

## 摘要（原文）
Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly trusted with mathematical, scientific and engineering
work, yet their mistakes are often confidently written and hard to spot. A single wrong step tends
to propagate, making the model waste inference tokens following a false premise. Existing safeguards
either check only the final answer and restart from scratch, or rely on a verifier LLM that competes
for GPU capacity and slows down response time. We propose online, deterministic verification: many
kinds of reasoning steps can be validated by code, so a step can be verified in real time, as it is
written, without any model call or GPU use. Whenever a check fails, we point out the mistake and let
the model continue from the same spot. We demonstrate this approach on symbolic mathematics, where
derivations already contain explicit expressions that a computer algebra system can check in
milliseconds. Tested on the ASyMoB benchmark with three open-weights models, our online validation
improves accuracy by 1.4-5.6 percentage points, corresponding to a 1.8-14.9% net reduction in
incorrect answers, while increasing token usage by only 6-7%. Because the verifier is independent of
the model, the approach complements progress in LLMs rather than competing with it: every new model
can benefit immediately, and the benefit grows as deterministic checkers extend to more kinds of
reasoning steps.
