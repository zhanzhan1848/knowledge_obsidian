## 核心要点
1. 指出常见流程的隐患：「测量表征上某统计量 → 发现它很大 → 架构上移除 → 报告改进」，解释该序列需三个**可复用检查**：统计量是否超噪声底、是否在几何零模型下存活、训练收益是否**需要**被移除的那个特定方向。
2. 应用于**自注意力移除**（删除注意力头输出中与自身 value 向量对齐的成分）。**九个预训练多头模型（5,408 个头，124M–6.9B 参数）**上，**更早位置的因果零模型解释了 48%–63% 的 head-value 余弦对齐**，且无单调规模趋势。
3. **GQA 下该对齐特定于每个头自己的 key-value 组**，跨组**反转**（组内 +0.24~+0.27，跨组 −0.11~−0.19）。
4. **分裂半信度**：per-head 干预效应在 **GPT-2 可分辨（r_Δ=0.80）**，但在 **Pythia-160M（0.42）与 Pythia-410M（0.53）**衰减；而**结构性自余弦信度三者都近乎完美（r_stat > 0.99）**——**哪个统计量能预测其自身干预是模型依赖的**。
5. **预注册 3 臂 × 8 种子配对析因**（每轮 4.0×10⁸ token，共 399,900,672 token）：**固定任意 rank-1 方向与基线无差异（+0.0011 nats, p=0.197）**，**自定向移除降低验证损失（−0.0029 nats, Holm p=0.0023）**并直接超过固定对照（−0.0040 nats, p=0.0018）。
6. **在 50.9M 规模，训练收益需要 own-value 方向，但大余弦对齐本身并不确立冻结模型中的冗余。**

## 摘要（原文）
Transformer architectural modifications frequently measure a statistic on internal representations,
observe that it is large, remove it architecturally, and report an improvement. Interpreting that
sequence requires evaluating whether the statistic exceeds its noise floor, whether it survives a
geometric null, and whether the training gain requires the specific direction removed. We formulate
these requirements as three reusable checks and apply them to self-attention removal, which deletes
the component of an attention head's output aligned with its own value vector. Across nine multi-
head pretrained models (5,408 heads, 124M to 6.9B parameters), an earlier-position causal null
accounts for 48% to 63% of the head-value cosine alignment, with no monotone scale trend. Under
grouped-query attention, this alignment is specific to each head's own key-value group and reverses
across groups (+0.24 to +0.27 within-group, -0.11 to -0.19 across-group). Split-half reliability
demonstrates that the per-head intervention effect is resolvable in GPT-2 ($r_Δ= 0.80$) but
attenuated in Pythia-160M (0.42) and Pythia-410M (0.53), whereas structural self-cosine reliability
is near-perfect in all three ($r_{\text{stat}} > 0.99$); which statistic predicts its own
intervention is model-dependent. In a pre-registered 3-arm by 8-seed paired factorial at $4.0 \times
10^8$ tokens per run (399,900,672 tokens), a fixed arbitrary rank-one direction does not differ from
baseline (+0.0011 nats, $p = 0.197$), while self-directed removal lowers validation loss (-0.0029
nats, Holm-corrected $p = 0.0023$) and outperforms the fixed arbitrary control directly (-0.0040
nats, $p = 0.0018$). At 50.9M scale the training benefit requires the own-value direction rather
than a fixed arbitrary one, but a large cosine alignment alone does not establish redundancy in
frozen models.
