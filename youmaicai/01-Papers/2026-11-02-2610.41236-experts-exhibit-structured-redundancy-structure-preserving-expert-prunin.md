# 专家存在结构化冗余：MoE 大语言模型的结构保持型专家剪枝

| **摘要** | Sparse MoE mechanism significantly improves the trade-off between model capacity and inference efficiency. However, practical deployment still requires storing all experts, resulting in substantial memory overhead. We show that expert outputs in MoE layers exhibit structured redundancy. Across different models and task categories, the stable rank and entropy effective rank of expert outputs are consistently far below the nominal number of experts, while expert output distributions also exhibit fewer effective modes. Motivated by these observations, we reformulate expert pruning as a structure-preserving expert subset selection problem and efficiently optimize it via a greedy strategy, rather than only independently scoring and ranking individual experts or exhaustively searching over expert combinations. We propose two expert pruning methods under the unified structure-preserving framework: Projection Energy Expert Pruning (PEEP), which preserves the functional subspace by maximizing weighted projection energy, and Distributional Coverage Expert Pruning (DCEP), which preserves the distributional geometry by minimizing weighted coverage error. Both methods incorporate router-derived weights as priors, enabling them to jointly preserve the global structural relationships among experts while accounting for the local importance of individual experts. Experiments on DeepSeek-V2-Lite and Qwen3-30B-A3B demonstrate that, at the expert retention ratios of 75% and 50%, our methods outperform the state-of-the-art baselines on the majority of benchmarks and consistently achieve the best average performance across both general tasks and mathematical reasoning tasks. |

## 核心贡献

1. 实证发现：**MoE 层的专家输出存在结构化冗余** —— 跨不同模型与任务类别，**稳定秩（stable rank）与熵有效秩（entropy effective rank）持续远低于名义专家数**，且专家输出分布的有效模式数也更少。
2. **问题重构**：把专家剪枝从"独立打分排序"或"穷举组合搜索"重新表述为 **结构保持的专家子集选择问题**。
3. **高效优化**：提出**贪心策略**求解。
4. 两个具体方法：
   - **PEEP**（Projection Energy Expert Pruning）—— **最大化加权投影能量**以保留功能子空间
   - **DCEP**（Distributional Coverage Expert Pruning）—— **最小化加权覆盖误差**以保留分布几何
5. 两者都**引入 router 导出的权重作为先验**，从而**同时保留专家间的全局结构关系与单个专家的局部重要性**。
6. 在 **DeepSeek-V2-Lite 与 Qwen3-30B-A3B** 上，**专家保留率 75% 与 50%** 时，在**多数基准上超越 SOTA 基线**。

## 技术方案

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 稳定秩 / 熵有效秩 | 度量专家输出的结构冗余 |
| 结构保持子集选择 | 剪枝问题的新形式化 |
| 贪心优化 | 高效求解子集选择 |
| PEEP | 最大化加权投影能量，保留功能子空间 |
| DCEP | 最小化加权覆盖误差，保留分布几何 |
| Router 权重先验 | 兼顾全局结构与局部重要性 |

### 关键发现

- **"稳定秩远低于名义专家数"** 是一个可直接验证的观察，它把 MoE 剪枝从"启发式打分"提升到了"有结构依据"的层次 —— 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41292-from-transcription-to-reformulation-defining-and-benchmarking-grounded-g]]（语音识别基准）这类"重新定义问题"是同类思路。
- 与 [[daily-2026-11-01]] 那批"压缩会不会坏"的关注点一致：本文的答案不是"压缩更安全"，而是"**压缩应当保持什么结构**" —— 明确指出应保持功能子空间（PEEP）或分布几何（DCEP）。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41822-trainable-vector-quantization-for-large-language-models]] 同属今日**压缩主线**，但 TVQ 是**权重侧**（VQ 量化），本文是**专家侧**（MoE 剪枝）—— 两条正交的压缩路径。
- "Router 权重作为先验"这个设计值得注意：它没有把 router 当黑盒，而是**把 router 的决策分布当作结构信息反向利用**。
