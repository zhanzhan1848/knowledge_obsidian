## 技术方案

### 核心思想

Large language models support personalized tutoring, content generation, and adaptive learning
paths. However, capability development requires these resources and interactions to form a coherent
learning process that evolves with the learner. A central challenge is to align goals, activities,
and assessment while using learning evidence to decide what should follow. We introduce Human-
Participatory Multi-Agent Organization and instantiate it in Agentic University (AU). In AU,
learners participate as organization members and work with specialized agents to maintain a
revisable Capability Program. The program connects personal goals, learning activities across
modalities, and layered assessment, while learner feedback and performance guide subsequent support.
Our evaluation shows advantages in program organization, assessment design, and revision quality.
Users achieved larger mean assessment gains (12.19 versus 5.61 points on a 50-point scale) and
reported stronger cognitive support. This work contributes an organizational approach to adaptive
learning that makes learner participation a continuing basis for multi-agent coordination. The
Agentic University project is available at
\href{https://github.com/AgenticOrgLab/AgenticUniversity.git}.
