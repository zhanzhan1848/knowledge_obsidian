## 技术方案

### 核心思想

Web agents are typically evaluated with goals fully specified before execution and fixed thereafter,
whereas real users often revise their requirements during interaction. To study such interactions,
we introduce DynaRev, a benchmark of 181 tasks to evaluate web agents under dynamic goal revision.
DynaRev uses semantic milestones to deliver revisions at task-defined stages, covers additive
revisions, unconditional overrides, and runtime-conditioned overrides, and evaluates correctness,
responsiveness, and efficiency through trajectory-grounded evaluation. Five-model evaluations show
that agents often satisfy only part of the task requirements, with performance varying across task
sources, revision types, and models. Further analyses reveal varied milestone timing, mixed effects
of dynamic delivery, and contrasting revision-handling outcomes, with gains from instruction
rewriting while preserving browser state and losses from browser resets. DynaRev provides a testbed
for studying how web agents adapt to evolving user goals. The code and data are available at
https://github.com/zsq259/DynaRev.
