| **摘要** | Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have achieved impressive performance across a wide range of reasoning tasks, yet it remains unclear whether their internal representations and decision criteria align with the latent structure of human cognition. In this work, we propose a novel framework to align MLLMs with the multidimensional mental representations underlying human similarity judgements. We build on a previously established behavior-derived semantic embedding space, in which objects are represented along 66 interpretable dimensions derived from 4.70 million human Odd-One-Out (O1O) judgements. For each triplet, we infer the most salient decision dimension and convert it into a dimension-grounded linguistic rationale. This enables MLLMs to learn both the final choice and a justification consistent with the inferred behavioral criterion. Experimental results demonstrate that our approach improves model consistency with human similarity judgements while retaining competitive performance on standard multimodal benchmarks. Furthermore, using searchlight Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA) on an independent fMRI Natural Scenes Dataset (NSD), we observe increased representational alignment between model and human. Ultimately, our findings point to a data-driven route for incorporating human cognitive structure into MLLMs alignment. The code is available . |

## 核心贡献

1. **出发点**：MLLM 在推理任务上表现优异，但其**内部表征与决策准则是否与人类认知的潜在结构对齐**并不清楚。
2. **方法**：基于一个既有的**行为衍生语义嵌入空间** —— 其中物体被表示在 **66 个可解释维度**上，这些维度来自 **470 万条人类 Odd-One-Out（O1O）判断**。
3. **训练信号构造**：对每个三元组，**推断最具显著性的决策维度**，并将其转换为**以维度为依据的语言化理由（rationale）**。
4. **学习目标**：让 MLLM 同时学到**最终选择**与**与推断出的行为判据一致的理由**。
5. **神经科学验证**：使用**搜索光式 RSA（searchlight Representational Similarity Analysis）** 在**独立的 fMRI Natural Scenes Dataset（NSD）** 上观察到**模型与人类的表征对齐增强**。
6. **结果**：提升模型与人类相似性判断的一致性，同时在标准多模态基准上保持有竞争力的表现。

### 关键发现

- **用 fMRI 验证"模型更像人"是本文的独特之处** —— 大多数对齐论文止步于行为一致性，这篇做到了**神经表征层面**。
- **470 万条 O1O 判断 → 66 个可解释维度**：这是一个已经存在的"人类认知的降维表示"，本文的价值在于把它变成**可训练的对齐目标**。
- **"选择 + 理由"的双重目标** 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41754-multimodal-in-context-learning-with-explicit-rule-induction]]（显式规则归纳）几乎同构 —— 都在把**可解释的中间表征**变成训练信号而非事后解释。
- 与 [[2026-11-01-2610.37157-what-survives-distillation-mechanistic]] 那种"聚合指标掩盖机制"的批评形成对照：这篇的思路是**主动把人类机制写进训练目标**，从源头避免 mismatch。
- 值得警惕：fMRI NSD 是**独立的**数据集这一点很关键 —— 否则就成了循环论证。

## 局限性

- 66 维语义空间本身来自先前工作，其维度是否完整刻画人类相似性判断存疑
- fMRI 验证在 NSD（视觉场景）上完成，推广到其他认知域需额外验证
- 摘要未给出具体数值
