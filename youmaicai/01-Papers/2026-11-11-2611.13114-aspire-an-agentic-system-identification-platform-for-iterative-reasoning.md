## 核心要点
1. **ASPIRE**：LLM agent **自主选择并执行系统辨识方法**，从实测数据构建**闭式非线性静态/动态模型**，可选辅以系统描述、模型预期用途与以形式化或自然语言表达的物理要求。
2. 每个模型含参数、估计记录、项的解释与在 **agent 不可访问的测试数据**上评估的误差；**形式化物理要求被独立验证**。
3. 可自主运行或交互运行——agent 可提问，用户可请求进一步分析与辨识轮次。
4. 三个实测系统（移动机器人、带干摩擦旋转系统、实验室发酵罐），每任务五次运行：**全部 30 次运行交付有效模型**，**每次运行成本 0.96–3.38 美元**。
5. 对 30 个推荐模型的作者评估：**150 个评估项中 132 项完全满足、18 项部分满足**；最常见差异（9 个模型）是**关于模型结构选择的不准确或无依据的声明**——这本身是有价值的诊断信号。

## 摘要（原文）
System identification requires expert decisions on the identification method, incorporation of prior
physical knowledge, and model validation. We propose ASPIRE, a framework for nonlinear system
identification in which a large language model agent selects and executes the identification method.
ASPIRE constructs closed-form, nonlinear static or dynamic models from measured data, optionally
supplemented by a system description, the intended use of the model, and physical requirements
expressed formally or in natural language. Each model includes its parameters, an estimation record,
an interpretation of its terms, and an error evaluated on test data inaccessible to the agent.
Formal physical requirements are verified independently. ASPIRE operates autonomously or
interactively, allowing the agent to ask questions and the user to request further analyses and
identification rounds. We evaluate ASPIRE on three measured systems: a mobile robot, a rotational
system with dry friction, and a laboratory fermenter. Each system is tested with and without a
description, in five runs per task. All 30 runs delivered valid models, at a cost of 0.96 to 3.38 US
dollars per run. An assessment by the authors found that, for the 30 models recommended by the
agent, one per run, the interpretation of the model and the documentation of its structure selection
and parameter estimation fully satisfied 132 of 150 assessed items and partly satisfied 18. The most
frequent discrepancy, found in nine models, concerned inaccurate or unsupported claims about model
structure selection.
