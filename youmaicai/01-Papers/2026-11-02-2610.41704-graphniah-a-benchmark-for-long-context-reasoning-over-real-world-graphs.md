| **摘要** | The needle-in-a-haystack (NIAH) benchmark was introduced as a test for measuring a model's ability to find small relevant facts within a very large body of input text. However, existing NIAH benchmarks each suffer from one or more of the following limitations: (1) reliance on synthetic data, so their evaluations may not generalize to real-world settings; (2) reuse of previously published datasets, leading to the risk of benchmark contamination; (3) dependence on manual annotation, which requires human effort to create and update data and is prone to future benchmark contamination; and (4) unrealistic simplification of long-context multi-hop reasoning where each next step is trivial given previous steps, while real-world reasoning often requires planning over multiple possible proof paths. To address these limitations, we introduce \Benchmark, a dynamic benchmark built from real citation relationships among scientific papers published after the evaluated models' knowledge cutoff date, which utilizes real-world information while ensuring no benchmark contamination. The fully automated benchmark construction pipeline samples representative subgraphs of the citation graph formed by these papers and converts each subgraph into a long-context QA example with a verifiable answer. We use GraphNIAH to evaluate eight LLMs from the GPT-5.6, Gemini-3, and Qwen3.5 families. We find that accuracy falls as questions require longer reasoning paths: at question depths of 16 and 32, the GPT-5.6 and Qwen3.5 models answer YES/NO questions no better than random guessing, mostly by answering NO; four of the eight models answer no counting example correctly, and the others model get worse as the subgraph size increases. These findings suggest that current LLMs often stop searching the citation relationships in their context before they reach the answer, even though every fact they need is in the context. |

## 核心贡献

1. **既有 NIAH 基准的四个局限**：(1) **依赖合成数据**，泛化性存疑；(2) **复用已发表数据集**，有**基准污染风险**；(3) **依赖人工标注**，成本高且易被未来污染；(4) **不现实地简化了长上下文多跳推理** —— 每一步在前一步已知时都是平凡的，而**真实推理需要在多条可能的证明路径上做规划**。
2. 提出 **GraphNIAH**：**基于真实论文引用关系**构建的**动态基准**，且**刻意选取模型知识截止日期之后发表的论文**，从而**保证零污染**。
3. **全自动构建流水线**：采样引用图的代表性**子图**，将每个子图转换为**带可验证答案的长上下文 QA 示例**。
4. 评估 **8 个 LLM**（GPT-5.6、Gemini-3、Qwen3.5 家族）。

### 关键发现

- **"用知识截止日期之后的论文"** 是一个极干净的抗污染设计 —— 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.38977-beyond-passing-tests-a-systematic-review-and-controlled-re-evaluation-of]] 中"训练截止后的比赛集上污染消失"的发现**方法论一致**：都是用时间切分做污染检验。
- **"每一步都平凡"是既有 NIAH 的根本缺陷** —— 这解释了为什么 NIAH 分数可以很高而真实长上下文能力仍存疑。**图结构引入了分支与规划**，这才是真实推理的难点。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.42112-subquadratic-attention-with-a-hierarchical-tree-structured-indexer]] / [[2026-11-02-2610.42147-latent-sparse-attention-towards-subquadratic-ultra-long-context-attentio]]（超长上下文方法）构成**方法-评测闭环**：今天出方法，明天就要有基准来验。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41162-frontiermat-benchmarking-language-models-at-the-frontier-of-materials-rd]] 同属今日**新基准**浪潮 —— 共同特征是**主动规避污染**。
