## 核心要点
1. **跨人格范式**，**14 个模型变体**（Llama 3 与 Qwen 2.5，0.5B–14B，base 与 instruct），以 **272 名台湾大学生**真实心理量表回答训练，预测另外六个量表。
2. 行为层面：**更大的变体复现并放大人類的跨量表相关**；**在校正七量表人类相关的测量衰减后，放大依然存在**。
3. 内部层面：对比方向分析发现**较大的 instruct 变体**的几何与人类矩阵对齐，但 **base 变体在该检测下几乎没有对齐**。
4. 跨模型，**几何强度与行为放大相关（r=0.68, p=0.008；控制 log 规模后偏 r=0.69）**。
5. 匹配激活探针范式显示**探针斜率变化小于行为斜率（1.38–1.77 vs 0.52–1.53）**；**合成对照显示 ridge 探针会抬高这些斜率**，扣除可靠性代理基线后调整斜率仍与行为相关（r=0.88）。作者称之为 **readout attenuation**。
6. **明确声明：在该检测下建立了可解码性与几何对齐，但未证明被测方向中介了跨人格答案。**

## 摘要（原文）
Do large language models represent psychological constructs internally, or do they only generate
outputs that resemble psychometric structure? We address this question using a cross-persona
paradigm across 14 model variants from two families (Llama 3 and Qwen 2.5, 0.5B-14B, base and
instruct). Given responses on one psychological scale from real individuals (N = 272 Taiwanese
university students), models predict responses on six other scales. At the behavioral level, larger
variants reproduce and amplify human cross-scale correlations; amplification persists after
correcting the seven-scale human correlations for measurement attenuation. We then examine whether
this structure is reflected internally. Contrastive direction analysis finds geometry aligned with
the human matrix in larger instruct variants, but little alignment in base variants under this
assay. Across models, geometry strength is associated with behavioral amplification (r = 0.68, p =
0.008; partial r = 0.69 controlling for log size). To relate internal structure to output behavior,
a matched activation-probing paradigm shows that probe slopes vary less than behavioral slopes
(1.38-1.77 vs 0.52-1.53). A synthetic control shows that ridge probing can inflate these slopes;
after subtracting a baseline based on a reliability proxy from another analysis, adjusted slopes
remain associated with behavior (r = 0.88). We term the measured probe-behavior gap readout
attenuation. These results establish decodability and geometric alignment under the tested assays,
without showing that the measured directions mediate the cross-persona answers.
