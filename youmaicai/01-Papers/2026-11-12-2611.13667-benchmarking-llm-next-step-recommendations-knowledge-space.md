## 核心要点
1. 用**知识空间理论（KST）**作为形式化评测参考：给定任务目录与已有的先修关系，推导**可接受的知识状态**以及每个状态下**全部合法的直接下一步集合**，再把模型在指定 prompt 下生成的推荐与这些参考集合逐一比对。
2. 样例评测规模为 **2,400 条响应**，覆盖 **6 个模型 × 4 个数学领域 × 4 种 prompt 条件 × 2 种目录顺序**；精确集合准确率随信息量递增：**仅给描述 61.3%**，让模型重建先修关系 **62.8%**，直接提供先修关系 **96.3%**，给出完整知识空间 **98.2%**。
3. 表现跨模型与跨领域差异明显；两种目录呈现顺序下推荐集合在 **85.25%** 的配对中一致。论文明确限定结论边界：该分数只刻画**结构化推荐准确率**，**不证明学习收益**，也**不证明使用 LLM 做选择是必要的**。

## 摘要（原文）
Selecting an LLM for next-step recommendation requires benchmarks that reflect the intended educational
task and domain. We present a benchmarking method that uses Knowledge Space Theory (KST) as a formal
evaluation reference. Given a task catalogue and an existing prerequisite relation, the method derives
admissible knowledge states and the complete set of immediate next steps for each state. Recommendations
generated by a model under a specified prompt are compared with these reference sets. A sample evaluation
study uses 2,400 responses from six models across four mathematics domains, four prompt conditions, and two
catalogue orders. Exact-set accuracy was 61.3% with descriptions only, 62.8% when models were asked to
reconstruct prerequisites, 96.3% with supplied prerequisite relations, and 98.2% with the complete knowledge
space. Performance varied across models and domains, and recommendation sets agreed across the two
presentations in 85.25% of pairs. The benchmark supports comparisons of model-prompt combinations against a
context-specific reference. Its scores describe structural recommendation accuracy under the evaluated
conditions; they do not establish learning benefit or the necessity of using an LLM for selection.
