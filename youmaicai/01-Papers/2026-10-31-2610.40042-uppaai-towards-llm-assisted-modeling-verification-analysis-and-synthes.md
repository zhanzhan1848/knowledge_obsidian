## 技术方案

### 核心思想

For more than three decades, UPPAAL has supported the modelling and analysis of real-time and cyber-
physical systems. Yet building a suitable model, formalising the intended properties, choosing
analysis techniques and interpreting counterexamples remain iterative tasks that demand substantial
expertise. Large language models (LLMs) can offer plausible explanations and even solutions, but
they are not necessarily correct. We present UPPAAI, an extension of UPPAAL in which users
construct, explain, debug and specify models in dialogue with an LLM. The guiding principle is that
the user requests, the LLM proposes, explains and orchestrates, and UPPAAL verifies. UPPAAI exposes
the UPPAAL editor, queries and verifier through a Tools API. We report on seven case studies, from
explaining to a newcomer to learning a controller, covering timed automata, stochastic hybrid
automata and strategy learning. LLMs proved to be extremely helpful in not just generating solutions
but also pro-actively suggesting relevant UPPAAL features it was not asked about.
