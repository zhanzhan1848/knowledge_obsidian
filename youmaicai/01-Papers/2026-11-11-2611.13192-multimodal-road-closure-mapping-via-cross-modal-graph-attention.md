## 核心要点
1. **问题**：道路封闭的识别需要**联合推理视觉证据、空间上下文与路网拓扑**；既有方法依赖人工地图更新，或只检测相关物体而**不解析影响哪一段道路**。
2. 提出统一的**多模态多任务框架**，在单一学习架构中**联合执行封闭分类（无/部分/完全）与路段关联**。
3. 整合**图结构地图数据**、投影了街级检测的卫星影像、以及街景图像，使用**模态特定编码器 + 跨模态 transformer 注意力**。
4. 在**近 2 万个**来自大规模商用车辆车队的真实场景上：封闭分类准确率 **72.7%**，路段关联准确率 **60%**，封闭分类**超过前沿视觉语言模型 28 个百分点以上**，同时**模型小 4000 倍、推理快 100 倍**。
5. 消融显示**跨模态注意力、边感知池化与图消息传递**对准确地理空间推理的重要性。
6. 建立**首个联合封闭分类与路段级归因的基准**；结论：**任务特定架构归纳偏置大幅优于通用多模态模型**处理结构化地理空间理解。

## 摘要（原文）
Accurate and timely identification of road closures is critical for large-scale navigation and
logistics systems, yet remains challenging due to the need to jointly reason over visual evidence,
spatial context, and road network topology. Existing approaches either rely on manual map updates or
focus on detecting closure-related objects without resolving which road segment is affected. In this
work, we introduce a unified multimodal and multitask framework that jointly performs road closure
classification (no, partial, complete) and road segment association within a single learning
architecture. Our model integrates graph-structured map data, satellite imagery augmented with
projected street-level detections, and street-view images using modality-specific encoders and
cross-modal transformer attention, enabling explicit reasoning over visual cues and network
topology. Evaluated on nearly 20K real-world scenarios collected from a large-scale commercial
vehicle fleet, our approach achieves 72.7% closure classification accuracy and 60% road segment
association accuracy, outperforming frontier vision-language models by over 28 percentage points on
closure classification while being over 4,000x smaller and 100x faster at inference. Extensive
ablations demonstrate the importance of cross-modal attention, edge-aware pooling, and graph message
passing for accurate geospatial reasoning. This work establishes the first benchmark for joint road
closure classification and segment-level attribution, and shows that task-specific architectural
inductive biases substantially outperform general-purpose multimodal models for structured
geospatial understanding.
