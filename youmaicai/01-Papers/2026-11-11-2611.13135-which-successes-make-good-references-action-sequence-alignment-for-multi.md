## 核心要点
1. 多轮工具使用 agent 的 on-policy 蒸馏中，用**同任务已验证的成功轨迹**作参考可提供事后洞见，但**不同成功可能抵达不兼容的交互状态**，提供误导性监督。
2. 把成功参考选择形式化为**自由目标最优传输**，归约为**给每条失败轨迹分配最兼容的成功参考**。
3. 由于潜在状态路径不可见，用**动作序列对齐**作为可观测的兼容性代理，以 **LCS（最长公共子序列）**实例化——识别共享的**有序动作骨干**，同时允许可恢复的绕路。
4. 在 ALFWorld 与 τ³-bench 航空域上，**LCS 是唯一在 ALFWorld 总分与航空域三项指标上均超过无参考 OPD 的参考选择器**。
5. 航空域训练的模型**零额外优化**即可迁移到零售域，LCS 在三项指标上全部改善。

## 摘要（原文）
On-policy distillation (OPD) reduces train-inference distribution mismatch by supervising a student
on its own trajectories. For multi-turn tool-use agents, conditioning the teacher on a validated
same-task successful trajectory as a reference can provide useful hindsight, but different successes
may reach incompatible interaction states and offer misleading supervision. We cast successful-
reference selection as a free-target optimal transport problem, which reduces to assigning each
failed trajectory its most compatible successful reference. Because latent state paths are hidden,
we use action-sequence alignment as an observable proxy for compatibility and instantiate it with
longest common subsequence (LCS), which identifies a shared ordered backbone of actions while
allowing recoverable detours. This perspective makes trajectory compatibility the central criterion
for reference selection in multi-turn OPD. Experiments on ALFWorld and the airline domain of
$\tau^3$-bench show that LCS is the only evaluated reference selector to improve over reference-free
OPD on both ALFWorld overall scores and all three airline metrics. Without further optimization, the
airline-trained models also transfer to the retail domain, where LCS improves all three metrics.
