# 注意力图知道何时该思考：免训练的难度感知 CoT 推理分割

| **摘要** | Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting has become a standard reasoning strategy in Multi-modal Large Language Models (MLLMs), showing strong performance in complex vision-language tasks such as reasoning segmentation. However, our analysis reveals that indiscriminate CoT usage can lead to degraded performance by inducing unnecessary reasoning, a phenomenon we term overthinking. In reasoning segmentation, overthinking often leads to misalignment between the query's intent and visual grounding. To address this, we propose Difficulty-Aware Chain-of-Thought (DA-CoT), a training-free adaptive framework that selectively triggers CoT based on the input's intrinsic difficulty. We find that the entropy of the model's attention maps reliably reflects task difficulty: low entropy indicates confident, localized grounding, while high entropy suggests uncertainty and potential benefit from CoT. DA-CoT uses this entropy signal to determine whether to apply CoT prompting. Additionally, we introduce a Bidirectional Rethinking mechanism that enables the model to automatically flip its reasoning decision when the initial output fails, enhancing robustness by recovering from failures induced by overthinking and underthinking. DA-CoT demonstrates its effectiveness by achieving state-of-the-art performance on the Reasoning Segmentation benchmarks while significantly improving inference efficiency. |

## 核心贡献

1. 揭示了 MLLM 中**无差别使用 CoT 的反作用**：引发不必要的推理（**overthinking**），在推理分割任务中导致 query 意图与视觉接地错位。
2. **关键发现**：**模型注意力图的熵**可靠反映任务难度 —— 低熵 = 自信的局部接地，高熵 = 不确定、可能受益于 CoT。
3. 提出 **DA-CoT**（Difficulty-Aware CoT）：训练无关的框架，用注意力熵信号**选择性地触发 CoT**。
4. 引入**双向反思（Bidirectional Rethinking）**机制，在初次输出失败时自动翻转推理决策，从 overthinking 和 underthinking 两类失败中恢复。

## 技术方案

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| 注意力熵 | 作为任务难度的免费代理信号 |
| DA-CoT | 依据熵阈值选择性触发 CoT |
| 双向反思 | 失败时翻转"是否思考"的决策 |
| Overthinking | 过度推理导致意图-接地错位 |
| 免训练 | 无需任何参数更新即可部署 |

### 关键发现

- 这是 **test-time compute 分配**的另一种答案：不是"多想一会儿"，而是"**先判断值不值得想**"。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.41089-circe-divines-llm-task-complexity-from-constraint-structure]] 形成有趣的对照：CIRCE 用**问题结构**（约束图）预测难度，DA-CoT 用**模型内部注意力**预测难度。前者是事前外部判据，后者是事中内部判据。两者可组合。
- 与 [[daily-2026-11-01]] 那批"过度思考"类发现互相印证：**推理的边际收益不是单调的**。

## 实验结果

- Reasoning Segmentation 基准上达到 **SOTA**
- 相比无差别 CoT 显著提升推理效率（只在想的时候才想）
- 双向反思机制可恢复 overthinking 与 underthinking 两类失败

---

## 局限性

- 仅在推理分割任务上验证，跨任务泛化性未知
- 注意力熵阈值需要标定，对不同模型/任务可能需要重新校准
- 免训练方法的上限受限于基础 MLLM 本身的接地能力
