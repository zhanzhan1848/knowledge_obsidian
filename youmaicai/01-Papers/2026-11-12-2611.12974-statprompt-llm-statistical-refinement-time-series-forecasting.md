## 核心要点
1. 指出 foundation 风格时序模型（把大规模预训练语言架构适配到序列数据）的开放难题：如何把**趋势、尺度、时间依赖**等基本时间结构有效暴露给模型，尤其是在**可用观测有限**时。
2. 提出 **StatPrompt-LLM**，对 **Time-LLM** 做轻量级统计精修：(a) 在其既有 prompting 机制上注入**数据集级统计信息**；(b) 引入**零初始化的自回归旁路（zero-initialised autoregressive bypass）**，作为一条互补的**线性预测通路**。
3. 在 electricity 与气象基准（**ETTh1、ETTh2、Weather**）上按 full-prefix 与 reduced-prefix 两种配置评测：**10% reduced-prefix** 下 StatPrompt-LLM 的样本内误差优于 Time-LLM，跨数据集**未加权平均相对降低 MSE 40.2%、MAE 21.4%**；full-prefix 结果则更具数据集依赖性。消融显示屏蔽部分统计描述符组带来的变化相对温和且依数据集而异；计算剖析表明相对 Time-LLM **增加了模型参数量与运行时开销**。

## 摘要（原文）
Time-series forecasting has recently seen a paradigm shift with the introduction of foundation-style models
that adapt large pretrained language architectures to sequential data. However, effectively exposing
fundamental temporal structures, such as trend, scale, and temporal dependence, to these models remains an
open challenge, particularly when the available observations are limited. In this paper, we conduct an
exploratory empirical study across different generations of forecasting architectures and propose
StatPrompt-LLM, a lightweight statistical refinement of Time-LLM. StatPrompt-LLM augments the existing
Time-LLM prompting mechanism with dataset-level statistical information and introduces a zero-initialised
autoregressive bypass as a complementary linear forecasting pathway. We evaluate our framework on
electricity and meteorological time-series benchmarks, including ETTh1, ETTh2, and Weather, under
full-prefix and reduced-prefix configurations. Under the 10% reduced-prefix configuration, StatPrompt-LLM
achieved lower in-sample prediction errors than Time-LLM, with unweighted average relative reductions across
datasets of 40.2% in MSE and 21.4% in MAE. Full-prefix results were more dataset-dependent. Ablation
analyses revealed relatively modest and dataset-dependent changes when selected additional statistical
descriptor groups were masked within the combined architecture. Computational profiling indicated additional
model parameters and runtime overhead relative to Time-LLM.
