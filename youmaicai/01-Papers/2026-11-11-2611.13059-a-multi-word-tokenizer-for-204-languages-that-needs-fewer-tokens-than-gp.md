## 核心要点
1. 从零训练约 200 种语言的人类文本分词器。**Havon v3**：byte-level BPE，262,144 词表，pre-tokenizer 保留组合记号与字母绑定、给无空格脚本分块。
2. v3 在 FLORES-200 devtest 上**印度 25 种语言全部**比 GPT-4o 的 o200k_base 少（**中位数 0.74 倍**），204 种语言中 **172 种**更少，但**英文多 7%**。
3. **Havon v4** 加多词训练阶段（SuperBPE）：在**与 GPT-4o 完全相同的 200,019 词表**下，英文比 GPT-4o **少 6.1%**，161 种语言更少，印度 25 语言全部更少（中位数 0.74）；262,144 词表下英文少 **9.6%**、182 种更少；524,288 词表下 **203/204** 更少。
4. **v5** 把代码样本换成 15 种语言宽松授权代码，代码上需 GPT-4o 的 1.03–1.22 倍（v4 为 1.11–1.29），保持印度 25 语言中位数 0.74、英文 0.94。
5. **作者明确声明：这些只是压缩结果，尚未评估用 v4/v5 训练的模型。**

## 摘要（原文）
Languages of India need 1.6 to 14 times as many tokens as English on GPT-4o's tokenizer for the same
content, which makes them more expensive and slower to serve. We train tokenizers from scratch on
human-written text in about 200 languages. Havon v3 is a byte-level BPE tokenizer with 262,144
tokens and a pre-tokenizer that keeps combining marks with their letters and chunks scripts written
without spaces. On FLORES-200 devtest it needs fewer tokens than GPT-4o's o200k_base on all 25
languages of India (median 0.74 of GPT-4o's count) and on 172 of 204 languages, but 7% more on
English. Havon v4 adds a second, multi-word training stage (SuperBPE) to v3. At exactly GPT-4o's
vocabulary size of 200,019 tokens, v4 needs 6.1% fewer tokens than GPT-4o on English and fewer on
161 of 204 languages and all 25 languages of India (median 0.74); at the same size, v3 needs 9.4%
more on English and wins on 133. With 262,144 tokens, v4 needs 9.6% fewer tokens on English and
fewer on 182 of 204 languages, and with 524,288 tokens it needs fewer on 203 of 204. The gains are
largest for English, Hindi and the Romance languages; scripts without spaces, such as Khmer, Burmese
and Tibetan, gain little and lose subword merges. Havon v5 replaces v4's Python-only code sample
with permissively licensed code in 15 languages: on code it needs 1.03 to 1.22 times GPT-4o's
tokens, against 1.11 to 1.29 for v4, while keeping all 25 languages of India (median 0.74, all
significant under a sentence-level bootstrap) and English (0.94). These are compression results
only: we have not yet evaluated a model trained with v4 or v5.
