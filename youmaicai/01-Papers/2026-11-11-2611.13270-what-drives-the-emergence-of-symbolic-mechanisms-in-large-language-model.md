## 核心要点
1. 从零训练网络于抽象序列任务，同时变化**架构**与**数据分布**因素，用表征、注意与因果中介分析研究符号机制的涌现。
2. 确认从头训练的 transformer 确实发展出符号机制。
3. **在多个数据多样性轴上，更高的多样性产生更强的符号签名。**
4. **令人意外的是，同样的签名出现在改造过的 transformer 架构乃至多层感知机中出现——架构归纳偏置并非符号机制发展的必要条件。**
5. 综合结论：**数据多样性，而非架构，是神经网络中涌现符号计算的首要驱动因素。**

## 摘要（原文）
Recent work has identified a set of emergent symbolic mechanisms that support abstract reasoning in
large language models, but it remains unclear what factors drive the emergence of these mechanisms.
To address this question, we trained neural networks from scratch on abstract sequence tasks,
varying both architectural and data distributional factors, and investigated the effects of these
factors on the emergence of symbolic mechanisms. Using representational, attentional, and causal
mediation analyses, we first confirmed that transformer-based language models trained from scratch
on our task developed symbolic mechanisms. We then found that, across multiple axes of data
diversity, greater diversity produced stronger symbolic signatures. Surprisingly, the same
signatures emerged in modified transformer architectures and even multilayer perceptrons, indicating
that architectural inductive biases are not necessary for the development of symbolic mechanisms. We
also observed consistent patterns in an entity-attribute binding task. Together, these findings
identify data diversity, not architecture, as the primary driver of emergent symbolic computation in
neural networks.
