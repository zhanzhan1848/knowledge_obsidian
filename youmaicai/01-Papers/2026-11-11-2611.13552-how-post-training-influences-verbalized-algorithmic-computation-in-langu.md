## 核心要点
1. 用 **Olmo-3-7B-Instruct** 的连续 **SFT → DPO → RLVR** checkpoint 在**多位数加法**上，以 **carry 值**作为已知中间状态。
2. 探针在 SFT 与 RLVR 中以相似精度从内部激活恢复 carry 信息，**但操纵这些信息并不能可靠控制模型输出**。
3. **言语化推理的有用性不同**：**RLVR 产生的 CoT 在所有接收 checkpoint 上准确率都高于 SFT 或 DPO 产生的 CoT**。
4. RLVR 后 carry 相关陈述的**重要性分数更高**（对照匹配的随机控制）；**DPO 表现出类似的敏感性但没有可比的准确率增益**。
5. 结论：**后训练改变了书面推理的有用性与对其内容的敏感性，而更高敏感性并不必然伴随更好的问题表现。**

## 摘要（原文）
Post-training improves language-model reasoning, but how it changes the use of internal information
and verbalized chain of thought (CoT) remains unclear. We study successive SFT, DPO, and RLVR
checkpoints of Olmo-3-7B-Instruct on multi-digit addition, using carry values as known intermediate
state. Probes recover carry information from internal activations with similar accuracy in SFT and
RLVR, although manipulating this information does not reliably control model outputs. In contrast,
the usefulness of verbalized reasoning differs: CoTs produced by RLVR yield higher accuracy across
receiving checkpoints than those produced by SFT or DPO. We also show that carry-related statements
receive higher importance scores after RLVR than matched random controls. DPO shows similar
sensitivity to carry-related statements but without comparable accuracy gains. These results suggest
that post-training changes the usefulness of written reasoning and sensitivity to its content in
meaningful ways, although greater sensitivity does not necessarily coincide with better problem
performance.
