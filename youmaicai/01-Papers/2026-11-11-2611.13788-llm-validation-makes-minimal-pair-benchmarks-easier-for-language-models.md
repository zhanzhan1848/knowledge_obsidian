## 核心要点
1. 指出**最小对基准的质量上限就是其标签质量**，而 LLM 是昂贵人工验证的显然替代品。
2. 在 **BLiMP（英语）**与 **QFrBLiMP（魁北克法语）**上测试——两个公开最小对数据集**释放了全部强制选择人工判断**。
3. 三个 LLM 以**强制选择、评分、作为评审团**三种方式验证，对照 **1、3、5 名评委的人工评审团**，在**两个预注册阶段**进行。
4. BLiMP 上：**强制选择验证器与留出的人类多数意见一致的比例高于五评委评审团**，但**捕获人类拒绝其标签的对子远少于单评委**。按点估计，**没有验证器值得超过一名评委**。
5. **错误不是随机的**：两个数据集上、10 个开放 LM 中，**预注册 LLM 过滤器标记的人类支持对子比它接受的对子难 18.9 个百分点**（全部 70 个 LM-验证器组合），且难于单名人类评委标记的对子——因此**过滤移除的是 LM 失败但人类不失败的条目，提升精度超过随机移除**。
6. 在任何输出之前冻结的协议在 **荷兰语 BLiMP-NL 上复现了差距与提升**。结论：**LLM 验证让最小对基准对它们本要测试的模型变得更容易**。

## 摘要（原文）
Minimal-pair benchmarks are only as good as their labels, and LLMs are an obvious substitute for
costly human validation. We test it on BLiMP (English) and QFrBLiMP (Quebec French), the two public
minimal-pair datasets releasing every forced-choice human judgment. Three LLMs validate by forced
choice, by rating, and as a panel, scored against human panels of one, three and five judges in two
pre-registered phases. On BLiMP, forced-choice validators agree with held-out human majorities more
often than five-judge panels, yet catch far fewer pairs whose label humans reject than one judge
does. By point estimate, no validator is worth more than one judge. The errors are not random. On
both datasets, across 10 open LMs, human-supported pairs that the pre-registered LLM filter flags
are 18.9 points harder than those it accepts, in all 70 LM-validator combinations, and harder than
pairs a single human judge flags. Filtering thus removes items on which LMs fail but humans do not,
raising accuracy more than random removal. A protocol frozen before any output replicates the gap
and the lift on Dutch BLiMP-NL. LLM validation makes minimal-pair benchmarks easier for the models
they are meant to test; we release code, validator outputs and item-level scores.
