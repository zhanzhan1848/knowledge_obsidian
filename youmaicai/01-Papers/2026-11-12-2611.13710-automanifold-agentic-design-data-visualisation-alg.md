## 核心要点
1. 问题定位：流形嵌入（t-SNE、UMAP、PaCMAP 等）由**人工手写**少数 SOTA 算法主导，其设计目标在开发时即被固定，无法按用户指定的**结构保留偏好**（如保留全局距离、局部邻域、簇结构）灵活转向。
2. 提出 **AutoManifold**：在**受限原语词表**（affinity 亲和、cost 代价、optimization 优化三类）之上，用**多智能体 LLM 编排**自动合成新的流形嵌入算法；每一步设计都以用户指定的结构保留偏好为条件，并通过 **LLM 引导的、以评测指标为依据（metric-grounded）的迭代循环**反复精修算法配置。
3. 在**相同评测基础设施**下、跨不同难度区间的真实数据集上，与 t-SNE / UMAP / PaCMAP 三个最强且最常用方法对比：生成的算法在**不损害原始数据内在结构**的前提下，比传统手设计方法更能导向用户指定的性质，说明 LLM 驱动的条件化算法合成**超越了超参数调优**的范畴；代码见 https://github.com/buraksusam/AutoManifold_NeurIPS2026。

## 摘要（原文）
Manifold embedding for data visualisation is dominated by a small set of hand-designed state-of-the-art
(SOTA) algorithms. Their design targets are fixed at development time, resulting in inflexible
algorithmic behaviour that cannot be readily steered towards user-specified structural priorities.
Hand-designing new embedding algorithms to address user priority requires highly specialised expertise
and is time consuming. To address autonomous algorithm design tailored to user preference, we introduce
an agentic algorithm generation pipeline AutoManifold. It composes new manifold embedding algorithms
from a constrained vocabulary of affinity, cost, and optimisation primitives, supported by
multi-agent large-language-model (LLM) orchestration. AutoManifold conditions every stage of its
design on user-specified structural preservation preferences, and iteratively refines the algorithm
configuration through an LLM-guided, metric-grounded iterative loop. We compare the generated
algorithms against the three strongest and most frequently used SOTA methods (t-SNE, UMAP, and PaCMAP)
on real-world datasets spanning different difficulty regimes under identical evaluation infrastructure.
The results demonstrate that LLM-driven, priority-conditioned algorithm synthesis can move beyond
hyperparameter tuning, producing genuinely new algorithms that outperform traditional hand-designed
methods in their ability to steer towards user-specified properties without compromising the intrinsic
structure in the original data. The code of AutoManifold is available at
https://github.com/buraksusam/AutoManifold_NeurIPS2026.
