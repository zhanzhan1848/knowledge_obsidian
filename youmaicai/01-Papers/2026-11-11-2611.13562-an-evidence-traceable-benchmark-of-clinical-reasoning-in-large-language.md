## 核心要点
1. **Med Evidence Bench (MEB)**：**1,536 个医生构建条目**，三个层级（指南知识 / 标准化临床场景 / 真实世界实验室场景）。
2. **每条陈述医学主张的评分标准都链接到版本化的源文本片段**——这正是它与既有基准的差别。
3. 跨 **10 个 LLM**，**Evidence Trace Score (ETS)**：指南知识 **0.975–0.997**，标准化场景 **0.292–0.658**，真实世界场景 **0.182–0.447**，且**跨层级排名不一致**。
4. 真实世界场景中：**识别临床信息（均值 0.770）远超匹配证据（0.165）或形成建议（0.160）**。
5. **1,296 条标准级判定**上，自动评分与三位医生多数投票一致（**宏 F1 0.930**，95% CI 0.915–0.945），但**在证据应用上一致性更低**。
6. 结论：**指南回忆不等于患者特异的证据使用**；作者明确声明这些结果**不建立临床有效性**。

## 摘要（原文）
Benchmark scores for medical large language models (LLMs) are difficult to interpret when scoring
criteria cannot be traced to clinical evidence. Here we developed Med Evidence Bench (MEB;
https://www.medevidencebench.org.cn/), comprising 1,536 physician-constructed items across three
levels: guideline knowledge, standardized clinical scenarios and real-world laboratory scenarios.
Each criterion stating a medical claim is linked to a versioned source-text span. Across ten LLMs,
the Evidence Trace Score (ETS) was 0.975-0.997 for guideline knowledge, 0.292-0.658 for standardized
scenarios and 0.182-0.447 for real-world scenarios, and rankings varied across levels. For real-
world scenarios, scores for identifying clinical information (mean, 0.770) exceeded those for
matching evidence (0.165) or formulating recommendations (0.160). On 1,296 criterion-level
judgments, automated grading agreed with the majority vote of three physicians (macro-F1, 0.930; 95%
confidence interval, 0.915-0.945), although agreement was lower for evidence application. These
findings from Chinese-language materials indicate that guideline recall does not ensure patient-
specific evidence use and do not establish clinical effectiveness.
