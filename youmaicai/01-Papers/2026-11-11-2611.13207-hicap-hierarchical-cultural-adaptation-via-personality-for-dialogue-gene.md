## 摘要（原文）
Cultural adaptation in dialogue systems refers to the ability to generate responses that conform to
linguistic norms and social expectations across different cultures. However, existing dialogue
generation approaches pay limited attention to the role of personality in shaping cultural
expression and communication style. We therefore propose HiCAP, a novel framework that integrates
personality traits into culturally adaptive dialogue generation. HiCAP employs a personality-
conditioned control mechanism that hierarchically modulates response style by first aligning it with
the cultural tone and then refining linguistic expression and content according to individual
personality attributes. To embed these factors, the model combines supervised fine-tuning and
instruction-based tuning, supported by dynamic instruction templates and multi-agent demonstrations
to capture diverse combinations of culture and personality. Experiments on CulturalPersonas and CPED
show that HiCAP achieves the strongest overall performance across various dialogue generation
metrics, demonstrating effective cultural adaptability and personality modelling.
