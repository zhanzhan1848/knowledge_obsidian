## 技术方案

### 核心思想

Radiological risk stratification and management planning are essential downstream tasks in medical
imaging. Automating these tasks is challenging because clinical guideline criteria are distributed
across prose, tables, and cross-referenced definitions. We present Guideline-derived Decision Graph
(GDGraph), a framework that decouples guideline compilation from case evaluation. During
compilation, a large language model partitions guidelines into section-based chunks, extracts local
decision subgraphs with linked definitions, and aggregates related branches into an executable
decision graph. During inference, a fast System One model answers localized clinical questions while
deterministic traversal resolves Boolean logic, turning opaque single-pass predictions into a
traceable decision path without task-specific training. Across 347 cases spanning renal cyst,
adnexal mass, and thyroid nodule guidelines, GDGraph improves System One accuracy from 57.3% to
90.8%, outperforming retrieval-augmented GPT-4o (80.1%) and graph-guided GPT-4o with chain-of-
thought (82.7%) at substantially lower latency and cost. The source code will be available.
