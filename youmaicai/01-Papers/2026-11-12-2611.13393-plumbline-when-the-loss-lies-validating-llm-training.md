## 核心要点
1. 提出 **Plumbline**——部署于 Amazon 生产环境的 **LLM 训练正确性验证系统**。它**逐环节检查训练生命周期**：从训练所用数据、到最终服务的模型、再到每个新模型引入的**新层**、一直到**整轮训练运行**；其**诊断能力复用同一套检查**把失败追溯到根因，两者都建立在**可跨模型与跨框架迁移的系统化正确性方法论**之上。
2. 覆盖**静默失败**：故障可能潜伏在运行的任何环节，表现形式随框架及其依赖库而异——有些报错响亮、有些悄然无声，但两者都耗时才能发现与定位；单次故障即可浪费一整轮长训练（晚崩溃，或仅在最终评测时分数变低）。
3. 效果与用例：支持两类**智能体化用例**——**Day-0 上线校验**（新模型发布后、团队依赖前先验证其训练正确性）与**训练失败根因追溯**。跨 **3 个框架、5 个模型**共发现 **61 个问题，其中 32 个是静默的**；**42 个已修复或已向上游报告，其中 8 个由本文首次提出**；并进一步把 61 个问题归纳为**七种失败模式**，指出训练在哪里容易出问题及其跨框架/模型的复现规律。

## 摘要（原文）
Correctness is a prerequisite for training large language models (LLMs) at scale, yet it is hard to
ensure: a fault can sit in any part of a run, and how it shows differs across frameworks and the
libraries they depend on. Some failures are loud and some are silent, and both take time to detect and
trace. A single fault can waste a long run: the run crashes late, or the model scores lower only at
the final evaluation. We present Plumbline, a correctness validation system for LLM training, used in
production at Amazon. Plumbline checks each part of the training lifecycle, from the data a run trains
on to the model it serves, down to the new layers each model brings and up to whole runs. Its diagnosis
reuses these checks to trace failures to their root causes. Both rest on a systematic correctness
methodology designed to carry across models and frameworks. Plumbline supports two agentic use cases:
Day-0 onboarding, which validates the training of a newly released model before teams rely on it, and
root-cause tracing of training failures. Across 3 frameworks and 5 models, it found 61 problems, 32 of
them silent. Of these, 42 are fixed or reported upstream, 8 of them first raised by us. We further group
the 61 into seven failure patterns that show where training breaks, and report how problems recur across
frameworks and models.
