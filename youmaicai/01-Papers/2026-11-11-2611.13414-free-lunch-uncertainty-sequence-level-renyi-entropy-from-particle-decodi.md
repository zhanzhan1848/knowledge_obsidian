## 核心要点
1. **SMC 采样的 power sampling 已经估计了 power 分布的归一化常数**，由此**以零边际成本**得到模型补全分布的**序列级 Rényi 熵（SRE）**，且**在每个粒子预算下都有保证**。
2. Rényi 链式法则将该读数扩展到**任意阶的逐步熵**。
3. 证明：**高于 1 的阶与每步最可能的 token 绑定**（这解释了它们为何追踪 log 概率），而 **order-1/2 成员累积每步搜索开销的对数**。
4. 该成员在 **13 个设定中的 10 个**是最佳零成本选择性预测信号。
5. **MATH500** 上其 **AUROC 高出 log 概率 0.022–0.044**（全部四个模型、且在长度四分位内成立），留出各设定时仍选中同阶。
6. **8 粒子探针**识别出受益于更多粒子的问题；**两遍策略以少 34.2% 的粒子匹配 64 粒子的 MATH500 准确率**（三个种子）。

## 摘要（原文）
Quantifying uncertainty over an entire LLM completion is critical for selective prediction and
adaptive compute. Zero-cost signals such as mean token entropy and log-probability are local to
tokens or to one completion, and self-consistency needs many extra completions. We show that
Sequential Monte Carlo (SMC) decoding for power sampling already estimates the normalizing constant
of the power distribution, which gives the sequence-level R'enyi entropy (SRE) of the model's
completion distribution at zero marginal cost, with a guarantee at every particle budget. A R'enyi
chain rule extends this readout to a family of stepwise entropies of any order. We prove that orders
above one are tied to the most likely token of each step, which explains why they track log-
probability, while the order-1/2 member accumulates the log of per-step search effort. This member
is the best zero-cost selective-prediction signal in 10 of 13 settings. On MATH500 its AUROC is
0.022 to 0.044 above log-probability with all four models and within length quartiles, and the same
order is selected when each setting is held out. An 8-particle probe identifies the problems that
benefit from more particles, and a two-pass policy matches 64-particle accuracy on MATH500 with
34.2% fewer particles over three seeds.
