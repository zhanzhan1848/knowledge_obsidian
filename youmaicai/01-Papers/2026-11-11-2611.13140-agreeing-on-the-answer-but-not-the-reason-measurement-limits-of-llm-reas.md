## 核心要点
1. 定义 Compositional Collapse（模型注意到问题的一个必需子部分，随后丢弃或误用它），在 **12 个模型 × 11 种语言**的多语言数学基准（**2,316 条**评分记录）上测量。
2. **50.1% 的失败同时携带多个类别证据**——「众数占比」取决于分类器测试各别的**顺序**：颠倒两个测试，**12 个模型中 11 个**判定改变；遍历 6 种顺序，Collapse 占比跨度 **26.4% → 76.4%**。
3. 人类一致性对**任务**未崩塌、对**类别**崩塌：母语者判定 179 条转录，对答案正确性 κ=**0.932**，对 Collapse 仅 κ=**0.093**。
4. 机制性证据：两位母语者彼此 κ=**0.659**，各自与检查器 κ≤**0.094**；**逐字相同的 prompt 重跑检查器模型**后 κ 从 0.147 升至 **0.636**——**差距主要来自 prompt，不是模型**。
5. 检查器标注频率是人类母语者的 **1.81 倍**；剔除一个缺陷基准项（孟加拉语/中文翻译被删从句）后降至 **1.17 倍**。
6. 作者给出四条廉价审计：**报告标签共现、置换打破平局、共享独立样本验证、审计系统性分歧**。

## 摘要（原文）
Failure taxonomies for LLM reasoning are increasingly reported as measurements: papers state what
fraction of a model's errors fall into named categories, then compare those fractions across models.
We show that for one such category the reported fraction is largely a property of the measurement
procedure. We defined Compositional Collapse (a model notices a required sub-part of a problem, then
drops or misapplies it) and measured it across twelve models and eleven languages on a public
multilingual math benchmark (2,316 graded records). First, 50.1% of failures carry evidence for more
than one category, so the reported plurality depends on the order in which the classifier tests
them: reversing two tests moves it in eleven of twelve models, and across all six orderings
Collapse's share spans 26.4% to 76.4%. Second, agreement collapses for the category but not for the
task. Native speakers of all eleven languages judged 179 transcripts, agreeing with our inspector at
kappa = 0.932 on answer correctness but only kappa = 0.093 on Collapse. A second native speaker per
language re-rated 142 items; on 121 determinate pairs the two humans agree with each other at kappa
= 0.659, while each agrees with the inspector at kappa <= 0.094. Re-running the same inspector model
with the human instructions verbatim raises kappa against Human 2 from 0.147 to 0.636, so the gap is
largely attributable to the prompt, not the model. Third, the inspector labels Collapse 1.81 times
as often as native speakers, but only 1.17 times once a defective benchmark item (a deleted clause
in the Bengali and Chinese translations) is excluded. We recommend four cheap checks before
comparing failure-mode rates across models: report label co-occurrence, permute the tie-break,
validate on a shared independent sample, and audit systematic disagreements.
