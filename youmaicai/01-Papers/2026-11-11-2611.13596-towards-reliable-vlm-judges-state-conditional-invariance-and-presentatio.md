## 核心要点
1. 指出聚合准确率或一致性**不揭示 judge 是否因正确理由改变裁决**。
2. **State-Conditional Invariance (SCI)**：judge 应在**保语义不变的呈现变化**下保持稳定，同时对**改变任务结果的因果干预**作出响应。
3. **GridWM-Judge**：基于确定性 MiniGrid 轨迹构建，含对齐的 Full、证据移除与反事实变体，加呈现与表示探针。
4. 五个诊断任务检验局部感知、动作条件转移、呈现稳定性、表示可交换性与 Full-CF 因果判别。
5. 发现**异质失效模式**：对证据呈现与编码敏感、成功轨迹上激活低、成对反事实判别弱。
6. 感知受控分析表明**视觉落地与目标恢复是显著的、模型依赖的瓶颈**。主张**联合报告正确性、呈现稳定性与因果敏感性，而非单一分数**。

## 摘要（原文）
Vision-language models (VLMs) are increasingly used as automated judges for multimodal and embodied
tasks, yet aggregate accuracy or consistency alone does not reveal whether a judge changes its
verdict for the right reason. We propose State-Conditional Invariance (SCI), a reliability principle
requiring a judge to remain stable under semantics-preserving changes in presentation while
responding to causal interventions that change the task outcome. We instantiate SCI in GridWM-Judge,
a simulator-grounded diagnostic benchmark built from deterministic MiniGrid trajectories with
aligned Full, evidence-removal, and counterfactual variants, plus presentation and representation
probes. Across five diagnostic tasks, GridWM-Judge tests local perception, action-conditioned
transitions, presentation stability, representation exchangeability, and Full-CF causal
discrimination. Our diagnostic evaluations reveal heterogeneous failure modes, including sensitivity
to evidence presentation and encoding, low activation on successful trajectories, and weak paired
counterfactual discrimination. Perception-controlled analyses further indicate that visual grounding
and objective recovery are substantial, model-dependent bottlenecks. These results motivate jointly
reporting correctness, presentation stability, and causal sensitivity instead of relying on a single
score.
