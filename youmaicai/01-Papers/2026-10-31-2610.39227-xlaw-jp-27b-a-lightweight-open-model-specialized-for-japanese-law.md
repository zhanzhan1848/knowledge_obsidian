## 技术方案

### 核心思想

We present XLaw-JP-27B, an open-weight model specialized for Japanese law that runs on a single
48\,GB GPU under the Apache~2.0 licence. It adapts Qwen3.8-27B with hybrid retrieval over all
current statutes and 31k case summaries, a light QLoRA fine-tune on its own verified answers to past
bar-exam questions (self-distillation), a contract-specific LoRA adapter, and four-sample voting. We
evaluate it on eight Japanese legal tasks---the multiple-choice section of the bar examination in
2024, 2025 and 2026, statement-level bar questions (JBE-QA), statute questions (LawQA-JP), the
patent attorney exam, and contract review and drafting built from government model contracts---
against open models of 12--33B under identical prompts and retrieval. XLaw-JP-27B ranks first on six
tasks, ties on one and is third on one (mean rank 1.3 against 2.8--3.1 for the baselines). It is the
only system that passes the bar exam in all three years (129, 120 and 130 of 175, meeting every
subject minimum; no test-year material was used in training), while open Japanese 32--33B models
fail at least one year. A controlled study of each component shows that retrieval, voting and self-
distillation help, and that thinking mode, retrieval-grounded synthetic tuning, self-verification,
per-statement decomposition and more self-distillation do not. The largest remaining gaps to
frontier models are in contract review and patent law. The model is available as a free web service
at https://x-law.jp/ with the weights available at https://huggingface.co/X-LawJapan/xlaw-jp-27b
under Apache~2.0.
