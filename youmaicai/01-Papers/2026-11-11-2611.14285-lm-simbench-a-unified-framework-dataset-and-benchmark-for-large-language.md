## 核心要点
1. **LM-SimBench**：评估 LLM 训练模拟器的统一框架、剖析数据集与基准。
2. 动机：评估候选并行化策略**需真实分布式训练运行，在大规模下成本过高**；性能模拟是低成本替代，但既有模拟器在**建模的训练阶段**与**所评估的工作负载**上不同，控制性比较困难。
3. 框架把模拟分解为从**模型编译到 schedule 级聚合**的功能阶段，把执行表示与成本估计器连接到可测的评估目标。
4. 在 **Ascend NPU 集群**上剖析 **10 个稠密与 MoE LLM 家族的 23 个模型设置**。
5. 数据集含 **348 个可行配置**，联合变化**五个并行化维度**与激活重计算，提供**六个视图**（配置/rank/算子/事件/kernel 粒度）。
6. **匹配的 GPU-NPU 实验**验证了收集与评估协议的跨平台适用性，并展示 NPU 引导的 GPU 配置选择。

## 摘要（原文）
Evaluating candidate parallelization strategies through distributed training runs makes strategy
selection prohibitively expensive at scale. Performance simulation offers a lower cost alternative,
yet controlled comparisons among existing simulators remain difficult because they differ in the
training stages they model and the workloads on which they are evaluated. We introduce LM-SimBench,
a unified framework, profiling dataset, and benchmark for evaluating LLM training simulators. The
framework decomposes simulation into functional stages from model compilation to schedule level
aggregation, connecting execution representations and cost estimators to measurable evaluation
targets. Following this framework, we profile 23 model settings from 10 dense and Mixture-of-Experts
(MoE) LLM families on an Ascend Neural Processing Unit (NPU) cluster. The resulting dataset contains
348 feasible configurations that jointly vary five parallelism dimensions and activation
recomputation, with six views spanning configuration, rank, operator, event, and kernel granularity.
Evaluations of representative training simulators demonstrate how LM-SimBench supports comparisons
of prediction accuracy, assessment of configuration selection, and diagnosis of prediction errors.
Matched GPU-NPU experiments validate the cross-platform applicability of the collection and
evaluation protocol and demonstrate NPU-guided GPU configuration selection.
