## 技术方案

### 核心思想

Large language model serving stacks, commercial inference APIs, vector databases, and agent
orchestration gateways are now ex- posed on the public internet and indexed by commodity scanners
within hours of deployment. The threat characteristics of this sur- face are only beginning to be
measured, and the honeypot frame- works that could measure it were designed for conventional net-
work services rather than for AI infrastructure. In fact, emulating an AI service is a distinct
deception problem: the observable behavior of the service, including model inventories, token
formats, tool-call protocols, and authorization flows, must be reproduced faithfully, while the
underlying computation, model inference and tool ex- ecution, must never occur. We present InferD, a
low-interaction honeypot framework organized around a single principle we call deception without
inference: every request an adversary can observe is answered from static response pools, and
nothing an adversary submits is ever executed. InferD decomposes the emulation into four deception
planes, transport, service protocol, vulnerability surface, and agentic session, adds a provenance
plane that links a planted credential to evidence of downstream model-mediated processing.
