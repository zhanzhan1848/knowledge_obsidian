## 核心要点
1. **MRoPE** 扩展 RoPE 引入时间/高/宽三轴编码视觉 token 的空间结构，但**给没有内在空间结构的文本 token 也分配了高/宽坐标**，把空间位置效应引入文本-视觉注意力。
2. 既有 MRoPE 变体探索不同频率分配与坐标布局，其设计隐含两种相反直觉：跨模态效应**为语言与视觉区域关联提供有用空间参照**，或**引入不必要的位置偏好妨碍文本-视觉交互**。
3. 作者**识别出共享坐标布局的一个约束**——它使得「消除跨模态空间效应」与「保留视觉内空间编码」无法同时满足，并设计受控实验分离二者贡献。
4. 跨多个模型规模、MRoPE 变体与基准的结论：**跨模态空间效应作为空间参照带来的额外收益很小，甚至损害性能**；而**视觉内空间编码对细粒度 grounding 仍然有用**。

## 摘要（原文）
Vision-language models widely adopt Multimodal Rotary Position Embedding (MRoPE), an extension of
Rotary Position Embedding (RoPE) that incorporates temporal, height, and width axes to encode the
spatial structure of visual tokens. However, MRoPE also assigns height and width coordinates to text
tokens that lack inherent spatial structure, introducing spatial positional effects into text-vision
attention. Existing MRoPE variants explore different frequency allocations and coordinate layouts,
with their designs suggesting different intuitions about these cross-modal effects: they may provide
useful spatial references for associating language with visual regions, or introduce unnecessary
positional preferences that hinder text-vision interactions. In this work, we disentangle intra-
visual spatial structure from cross-modal spatial effects to investigate this question. We identify
a constraint of shared coordinate layouts that prevents eliminating cross-modal spatial effects
while preserving intra-visual spatial encoding, and develop controlled experiments to isolate their
respective contributions. Our results across multiple model scales, MRoPE variants, and benchmarks
show that cross-modal spatial effects provide little additional benefit as spatial references and
can even impair model performance, while intra-visual spatial encoding remains useful for fine-
grained grounding.
