## 核心要点
1. 指出既有偏差探测方法的两个局限：**依赖固定格式任务**（代词消解、职业关联）而对齐调优的模型容易处理；**比较原始的组级 rubric 分数**，忽略逐维度方差并掩盖哪些偏差维度真正区分性别组。
2. **GRAP**：用**轻量 logistic 探针**从 **10 个 rubric 维度**打分的响应中识别最具区分度的偏差维度。
3. **任务无关地**作用于开放式指令跟随。

## 摘要（原文）
As LLMs increasingly mediate everyday interactions, subtle gender biases in open-ended responses
pose growing societal risks. Existing probing methods have two key limitations: they rely on fixed-
format tasks, such as pronoun resolution or occupation association, that alignment-tuned models can
easily handle; and they compare raw group-level rubric scores, ignoring per-dimension variance and
obscuring which bias dimensions reliably distinguish gender groups. We introduce GRAP (Gender
Rubric-Aware Probing), a framework that uses a lightweight logistic probe to identify the most
discriminative bias dimensions from responses scored across 10 rubric dimensions, operating task-
agnostically on open-ended instruction following.
