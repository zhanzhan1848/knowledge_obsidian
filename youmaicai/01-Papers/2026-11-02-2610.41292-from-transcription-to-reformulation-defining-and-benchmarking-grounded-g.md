| **摘要** | Speech language models have expanded automatic speech recognition (ASR) from transcription to general speech-to-text generation. Yet existing benchmarks still assume a single ground-truth transcript per utterance, an assumption that struggles when models produce readable, formatted, or structured outputs. In this work, we introduce Grounded Generative Speech Recognition (GSR), reformulating speech recognition as generating semantically grounded text instead of a single transcript. We define a grounded reformulation space that characterizes all valid textual realizations supported by the input speech, and establish a four-level hierarchy of reformulation capabilities, including literal transcription, canonical reformulation, semantic reformulation and speech-grounded structuring. Based on this formulation, we present GSR-Bench, the first comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate generative speech recognition systems beyond exact transcript matching. GSR-Bench covers diverse scenarios including refined meeting transcription, cross-dialect translation, context-aware speech understanding, and robustness under silent or noisy acoustic conditions. To address the limitations of word-level evaluation metrics, we develop a multi-dimensional evaluation framework combining speech grounding assessment and LLM-based semantic judgment, whose reliability is validated through human preference studies. Extensive experiments across representative architectures, ranging from conventional ASR systems to end-to-end Speech-LLMs, reveal fundamental trade-offs between transcription fidelity and generative reformulation ability. Our analysis further identifies critical failure modes, including acoustic hallucination, unsupported generation, and instruction drift, providing insights into the design and evaluation of future grounded generative speech systems. |

## 核心贡献

1. **问题**：语音语言模型把 ASR 从**转写**扩展到**通用语音到文本生成**。但现有基准仍假设**每个话语有单一 ground truth 转写** —— 这个假设在模型产出**可读、格式化或结构化**输出时就不成立了。
2. 提出 **GSR（Grounded Generative Speech Recognition）**：把语音识别**重新表述为生成语义接地的文本**，而非单一转写。
3. **核心概念 —— 接地的重述空间（grounded reformulation space）**：刻画输入语音所支持的**所有有效文本实现**。
4. **四级重述能力层级**：
   1. **字面转写**（literal transcription）
   2. **规范化重述**（canonical reformulation）
   3. **语义重述**（semantic reformulation）
   4. **语音接地的结构化**（speech-grounded structuring）
5. **GSR-Bench**：首个全面评估生成式语音识别（超越精确转写匹配）的基准，覆盖**精炼会议转写、跨方言翻译、上下文感知语音理解、静音或噪声声学条件下的鲁棒性**。
6. **评测框架**：多维度评估 = **语音接地评估 + LLM 语义判定**，可靠性经**人类偏好研究**验证。
7. **实验发现**：跨从传统 ASR 到端到端 Speech-LLM 的代表性架构，揭示了**转写保真度与其他能力之间的根本权衡**。

### 关键发现

- **"单一 ground truth 转写"是一个正在过时的假设** —— 当模型输出"会议纪要 + 行动项 + 说话人标签"时，"正确转写"是什么本身就成问题了。这篇的核心贡献是**重新定义了问题**，而不是造了个更长的 benchmark。
- **四级层级** 划得很清楚：字面 → 规范 → 语义 → 结构化，这是一条**从易到难的梯度**，正好对应模型能力的成熟路径。
- **"LLM 语义判定 + 人类偏好验证"** 是评测框架的自我校准机制 —— 承认 LLM judge 不可靠（与 2026-11-01 那批"评委不可靠"主线一致）并给出验证途径。
- **"转写保真度与其他能力之间的根本权衡"** 是对语音 LLM 设计的关键洞察 —— 你要精确转写还是要理解与结构化，**这两者会互相拉扯**。
- 与 [[2026-11-02-2610.40738-apriel-voice-27b-preview-an-open-model-for-speech-reasoning-and-tool-use]]（Apriel Voice）、[[2026-11-02-2610.40883-incongruence-aware-evaluation-of-multimodal-speech-emotion-recognition-i]]（语音情绪不一致度）构成今日**语音三连**：能力、评测、诊断。

## 局限性

- 基准的有效性依赖 LLM judge 的质量，"经人类偏好验证"缓解但未消除该依赖
- 四级层级的划分有主观性，边界案例的判定标准未完全形式化
- 摘要未给出各架构的具体分数
- 跨方言翻译受限于测试语料的方言覆盖度
