## 核心要点
1. **Mobile AI Factory** 是 agentic 系统，为给定任务与手机构建**微调 + 量化的 SLM**。
2. 编排 LLM 决定是调超参、挖掘真实数据还是生成合成样例；**固定规则回滚损害精度的变更**；工厂**从小到大模型跨基座与量化搜索**，**在搜索中自适应调整精度目标**。
3. 关键：**使用目标手机的真实测量**（跨推理引擎与后端）。
4. 十个任务、四台 Android 手机上，未见测试集上其端侧模型在五个任务上**超过两个五样本前沿模型（最多 +0.34）**，另三个任务持平。
5. 搜索结果距**穷举训练全部 27 个模型变体仅差 0.03**，而**GPU 时间少 19 倍**。
6. **手机测量确实重要**：最佳配置跨手机变化；**没有手机测量时，即使用 Claude Opus 5.5 并给出全部硬件与模型规格，仍在 25% 的简单/中等案例与 60% 的困难案例上不满足应用要求**。

## 摘要（原文）
Small language models (SLMs) running on mobile devices can keep user data private and work without
connectivity. But they perform well only when fine-tuned for the target task and sized for the
target device, a process that today requires repeated expert tuning. We present {\it Mobile AI
Factory}, an agentic system that builds a fine-tuned, quantized SLM for a given task and phone.
Given a task specification and a target phone, an orchestrator LLM chooses whether to tune
hyperparameters, mine real data, or generate synthetic examples, while fixed rules roll back changes
that hurt accuracy. The factory searches from small to large models across base models and
quantizations, adapts its accuracy goal as it searches, and uses real measurements of the target
phone across inference engines and backends. We evaluate Mobile AI Factory on ten tasks and four
Android phones. On an unseen testset, its on-device models outperform two five-shot frontier models
on five tasks, by up to 0.34, and match them on three more. Its search comes within 0.03 of
exhaustively training all 27 model variants on an entity extraction task, while using 19$\times$
less GPU time. Phone measurements also matter: the best configuration changes across phones. Without
phone measurements, Claude Opus 5.5, given the full hardware and model specifications, misses the
app's requirements in 25\% of easy and moderate cases and in 60\% of hard ones. Code is available
at: https://github.com/evanly-gh/SLM_Factory.
