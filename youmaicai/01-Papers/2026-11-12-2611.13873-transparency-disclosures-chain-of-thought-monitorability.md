## 核心要点
1. **主张：前沿 AI 开发者应披露具体指标，使 CoT 监控所面临的威胁可被追踪**，以更好地协调维护 **CoT monitorability（思维链可监控性）**。作者识别出**五个同时高度相关且合理可行披露的指标**，对应 CoT 监控所依赖的**五步链条**中的每一步。
2. **五个指标的具体构成**：**第三方 CoT 访问与基础架构规格**（同时支撑对其余指标的外部验证）；**CoT 质量**方面的**自然度（naturalness）**与**忠实性（faithfulness）**；**监控有效性**方面的**检测能力（detection）**与**提升度（uplift）**。
3. **对 Anthropic、OpenAI、Google DeepMind 现有披露的审计结果**：**没有任何一家开发者覆盖全部五项**，但**每一项指标至少已被某家前沿开发者报告过**。由于现有披露主要依赖**开发者自评**，作者建议以**比公开披露更深度的访问**重复一次**跨开发者审计**——由**嵌入开发者内部的评估者**执行，或交由**独立验证组织（Independent Verification Organization）**承担。

## 摘要（原文）
Chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring is one of the most valuable tools for monitoring advanced AI systems, but
it is under threat. We argue that frontier AI developers should report specific indicators to make these
threats trackable and to better facilitate coordination to preserve CoT monitorability. We identify five
indicators that are both highly relevant and reasonably feasible to disclose, one for each link in a five-step
chain on which CoT monitoring depends. The first, third-party CoT access and base architecture
specifications, also underpins external verification of the rest. The others span CoT quality (naturalness
and faithfulness) and monitoring effectiveness (detection and uplift). An audit of current disclosures by
Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind shows that no developer covers the full set, but that every indicator
has been reported by at least one frontier developer. Because current disclosures rely largely on developers'
own evaluations, we also recommend that a cross-developer audit be repeated with deeper access than public
disclosures allow, either by embedded evaluators working within developers or by an Independent Verification
Organization.
