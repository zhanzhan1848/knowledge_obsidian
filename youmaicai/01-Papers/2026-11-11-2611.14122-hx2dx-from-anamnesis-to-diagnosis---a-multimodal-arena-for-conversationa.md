## 核心要点
1. 批评：**LLM 在静态评估中达到专家级诊断准确率**，但静态评估给的是**已浓缩了诊断相关发现的病例摘要**——测量的是**在已选证据上的推断**，**不计及获取这些证据所用的问询**。
2. **Hx2Dx ARENA**：来自 **NEJM 的 1,000 个临床案例**的交互式基准，每个案例是模拟接诊，从初诊表现经病史采集（Hx）、体格检查到诊断检查（Dx）。
3. **所有模型相对静态设定都损失了诊断准确率。**
4. 关键归因：**「问对问题的能力」解释了模型间差距与准确率损失的大部分**——随着问题更有针对性、构建出更完整的临床图景，诊断随之改善。
5. 结论：**LLM 在相关数据到手后推理得很好，但在假设驱动的信息收集上受限——在「为了知道而问什么」上受限。**

## 摘要（原文）
Large language models (LLMs) achieve expert-level diagnostic accuracy in static evaluations, where
the model receives a curated vignette or case report that already condenses the diagnostically
relevant findings. Such evaluations measure inference over already selected evidence; the
evaluations do not account for the inquiry used to obtain it. In clinical practice, however,
diagnosis is an iterative process of inquiry. Clinicians take the patient's history, or anamnesis
(Hx), through targeted questioning, examine the patient, and request and interpret tests,
progressively narrowing the differential until they reach a final diagnosis (Dx). We introduce Hx2Dx
ARENA, an interactive benchmark of 1'000 clinical cases from the New England Journal of Medicine
(NEJM). Each case is a simulated encounter that proceeds from initial presentation through history
taking and physical examination to diagnostic workup. We evaluate open- and closed-source LLMs under
multiple configurations. Performance varies considerably across models in the interactive setting,
and every model loses diagnostic accuracy relative to the static setting, where the full case is
presented upfront. The ability to ask the right questions accounts for much of both the gap between
models and the loss of accuracy: diagnosis improves as questions become more targeted and build a
more complete clinical picture. LLMs thus reason well once the relevant data are available but
remain limited in hypothesis-driven information gathering, in knowing what to ask in order to know.
Closing this gap is a prerequisite for their safe integration into real-world diagnostic workflows.
