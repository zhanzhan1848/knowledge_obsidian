## 核心要点
1. 提出 **Ask2Decide 基准**：43 个规划任务，评估智能体是否会**主动追问缺失但对决策关键的信息**。评测设计中由"操作员"持有智能体请求与记录中缺失的事实，用**优化求解器判定哪些事实会改变最优计划**，并**刻意加入措辞相近但不影响最优计划**的事实，以考察智能体能否区分有用信息与无关细节。
2. 关键实证：**人类回复远比未校准的模拟用户更容易漏掉纠正性事实——43% vs 7%**。基于 19 名参与者、79 段真实交互会话，作者校准出一个更贴近人类回复模式的**模拟操作员**（此前用未校准 LLM 模拟用户会严重高估智能体的表现）。
3. 评测四个前沿 LLM：当信息缺失时，其**初始计划有 58–98% 的任务不可行**；而信息齐备时仅 5–28% 不可行。且智能体挖出**会改变最优计划**的事实的概率（39–52%）仅略高于**不改变最优计划**的事实（32–41%）——说明通过交互获取决策相关信息仍是 LLM 规划的主要瓶颈。

## 摘要（原文）
LLM agents are increasingly asked to make plans for people, such as scheduling drivers or assigning
nurses. However, people's requests are often incomplete, and the supporting information for
decision-making may be outdated, requiring agents to ask for clarification. Existing benchmarks
typically provide fully specified problems or rely on uncalibrated LLM-simulated users, leaving this
ability insufficiently tested. We introduce Ask2Decide, a benchmark of 43 planning tasks that evaluates
whether agents ask for missing information that matters for their decisions. An operator holds facts
absent from the agent's request and records, and optimization solvers determine which facts change the
optimal plan. We also include similarly worded facts that leave the optimal plan unchanged to test
whether agents distinguish useful information from irrelevant details. We collect 79 interaction
sessions from 19 participants and find that, after an incorrect plan, human replies omit the correcting
fact far more often than replies from an uncalibrated simulated user (43% versus 7%). Using these
interactions, we calibrate a simulated operator that more closely matches human reply patterns.
Evaluating four frontier LLMs, we find that their initial plans are infeasible on 58-98% of tasks when
information is missing, compared with 5-28% when all facts are provided upfront. Agents uncover facts
that change the optimal plan only slightly more often than facts that leave it unchanged (39-52% versus
32-41%). These results show that obtaining decision-relevant information through interaction remains
a major bottleneck for LLM planning. This is ongoing work and will be updated. The date of this version
is shown on the first page.
