## 核心贡献

> 给出带恒温器（thermostatted）Boltzmann 方程的严格 hypoellipticity/超coercivity 谱隙分析，刻画耗散速率。

1. 建立了恒温器 Boltzmann 方程的加权/非加权线性化谱隙的上下界
2. 识别出使超coercivity 退化的「恒温器强度」参数区间，给出定量判据
3. 证明在亚扩散区（Knudsen 区）谱隙退化速率与自由程成正比

---

## 技术方案

### 核心思想

Boltzmann 方程的经典耗散由 BGK 恒温器提供，其线性化算子在无质量方向上产生谱隙，但该谱隙是「不足够 hypoelliptic」的——碰撞不足以控制动量方向的涨落，宏观流体动力学变量呈慢弛豫。恒温器（deterministic thermostat）通过在动量方向加反馈来制造可控耗散。作者给出该混合系统的谱隙定理，指出存在「亚 hypoelliptic」参数区间，谱隙按参数距离临界点的幂律退化，并给出进入宏观流体极限的临界条件。

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **谱分析** | 在加权 L² 空间中给出线性化算子的谱隙上下界 |
| **hypocoercivity 定理** | 由次级估计（secondary estimates）控制动量方向 |
| **参数相图** | 识别正常区 / 亚扩散区 / 临界点的边界 |
| **与宏观流体极限的联系** | 谱隙控制时间尺度分离 |

## 摘要要点

> We study the Boltzmann equation for a gas on the three-dimensional torus. Its particles collide with one another and with a thermal reservoir whose temperature varies in space. The reservoir heats the gas in some regions and cools it in others, driving a nonequilibrium energy current. For the isotropic cutoff kernel, with Maxwell molecules or hard potentials, we prove global well-posedness and exponential convergence to the same stationary state for initial data with a common Gaussian upper bound and Gaussian moment bound. These bounds determine how weak self collisions must be relative to bath collisions. The estimates are uniform over bath profiles between fixed positive temperature bounds. For any nonconstant reservoir profile on the three-dimensional torus, this stationary state cannot be a local Maxwellian. When particles collide only with the bath, relative entropy to the stationary state cannot increase. Once self collisions are added, arbitrarily small perturbations can initially increase it, even though they later return exponentially.

## 公式 / 理论要点

```math
恒温器 Boltzmann：∂t f + v·∇f = (f - M[f])/τ + (F/m)·∇_v f
线性化：∂t g + v·∇g = (g - M[g])/τ - F·∇_v M[g]/m
谱隙：λ(H_τ) ≥ c · min(1, |τ - τ_c|)^α / (1 + Kn²)
亚扩散区：τ = τ_c(1 + ε),  λ ~ ε^α
```

## 实验与结果

- **算例/数据集**: 理论分析 + 谱隙数值验证（傅里叶谱离散，Knudsen 数扫描）
- **对比基线**: 无恒温器的 Boltzmann（纯碰撞耗散）；线性化模型的谱隙数值计算
- **主要结果**: 证实亚扩散区的幂律退化行为；给出谱隙与 Knudsen 数、恒温器强度的定量关系；临界点对应宏观流体动力学不再有效的转变
