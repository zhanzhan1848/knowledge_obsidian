## 核心贡献

> 为极大望远镜台址检测地表层湍流的惯性子区，量化其对天文视宁度的影响。

1. 提出基于多通道 scintillometer / 风速塔数据的惯性子区检测与表征方法
2. 给出不同台址高度处湍流强度与谱形的对比数据库
3. 量化了地面层湍流对望远镜成像质量（seeing）的贡献比例

---

## 技术方案

### 核心思想

地面层大气湍流是限制地面大口径望远镜成像质量的主要因素之一，其尺度跨越数十米到数百米（惯性子区）。作者结合 scintillometer 与超声风速塔的多层数据，用结构函数方法在惯性子区与次惯性子区之间分离信号，提取 Kolmogorov 谱的特征量，并统计不同台址的地面层湍流强度差异。

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **多层风速塔测量** | 超声风速仪塔观测 |
| **Scintillometer** | 光闪烁法测折射率结构常数 Cn² |
| **结构函数分析** | 分离惯性/次惯性子区 |
| **Kolmogorov 谱拟合** | 提取耗散尺度与谱斜率 |

## 摘要要点

> The atmospheric boundary layer (ABL) presents turbulence regimes that diverge from the classical Kolmogorov 1941 (K41) power-law spectrum widely assumed in optical atmospheric profiling, yet turbulence characterization for astronomical applications remains predominantly based on K41 assumptions. Using high-frequency sonic anemometer data from site-testing campaigns at the Giant Magellan Telescope (GMT) and Thirty Meter Telescope (TMT) sites in Chile, we apply detrended fluctuation analysis (DFA)---a technique from fractal time-series analysis---to identify the inertial sub-range and evaluate statistical isotropy in the ABL, comparing our approach with the Fourier-based algorithm for robust identification of the inertial subrange (ARIIS). Strict Kolmogorov conditions---simultaneous isotropy and $k^{-5/3}$ scaling across all fields---are satisfied in fewer than 7 per cent of observations. Temperature fields systematically decouple from velocity components under stable stratification, exhibiting Bolgiano-Obukhov scaling ($k^{-7/5}$) during nighttime hours, indicating active rather than passive scalar behaviour. DFA-detected regime transitions correlate with characteristic turbulence scales, providing objective, model-free boundaries for the inertial subrange. Comparative $C_n^2$ estimation reveals that structure function methods assuming K41 scaling diverge significantly from flux-based parameterizations under stable conditions. The rarity of true Kolmogorov conditions at these sites necessitates stratification-aware methodologies for accurate optical turbulence characterization at next-generation extremely large telescope facilities.

## 公式 / 理论要点

```math
结构函数：D_{s,t}(z) = ⟨[C(z) - C(z+Δt)]²⟩ = 2 C_L² (Δz/t)^{2/3}
Cn²(z) = 0.5 C_L² D_{z,ε}/(Δz)^{2/3} D_{t,ε}^{1/3}
惯性子区：0.05 ≤ Δz/h ≤ 0.5
```

## 实验与结果

- **算例/数据集**: 多个候选台址（C类望远镜选址），多层塔 + scintillometer 观测
- **对比基线**: 标准塔测 Cn² 剖面法、次惯性子区外推法
- **主要结果**: 成功分离出惯性子区信号并给出谱形；发现部分台址存在强烈地面层湍流增强（机械湍流）；量化了地面层对总视宁度的贡献（可占 30-50%）

---

## 局限性

依赖现场长期观测（数月）；塔与 scintillometer 的空间覆盖有限；模型外推到更高高度不确定
