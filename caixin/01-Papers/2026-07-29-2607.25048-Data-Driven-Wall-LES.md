## 核心创新点

### 1. 数据驱动叶轮模型
- 使用PIV和扭矩测量实验数据
- B样条参数化叶轮区域速度
- 通过动量强迫耦合到LES

### 2. ODIL + CMA-ES优化框架
- 初始B样条系数使用ODIL（Optimizing a DIscrete Loss）框架推断
- 约束条件：RANS方程、PIV测量、扭矩测量
- CMA-ES（Covariance Matrix Adaptation Evolution Strategy）细化系数

### 3. 元稳定状态切换动力学
- 模拟再现体流动平均场
- 显示元稳定状态切换动力学
- 四细胞流动模式缓慢旋转

## 摘要原文

> The von Kármán turbulent swirling flow exhibits intriguing large-scale metastable dynamics, including low-frequency state switching. The study of state switching demands long-duration high-fidelity simulations at high Reynolds numbers that capture the flow generated by the impellers. Blade-resolved Large Eddy Simulations (LES) are computationally prohibitive, limiting access to these slow dynamics.
