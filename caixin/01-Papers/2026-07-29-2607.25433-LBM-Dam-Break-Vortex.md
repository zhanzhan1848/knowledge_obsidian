## 摘要原文

> In this study a two-phase numerical model, based on the Lattice Boltzmann Method coupled to a conservative Allen-Cahn interface-capturing equation is employed to perform direct numerical simulations of dam-break waves propagating over a dry bed and impacting on vertical walls. Access to high-resolution simulations enables a detailed assessment of how accurately resolving both air-water and solid-water interfaces affects local and overall dynamics.
