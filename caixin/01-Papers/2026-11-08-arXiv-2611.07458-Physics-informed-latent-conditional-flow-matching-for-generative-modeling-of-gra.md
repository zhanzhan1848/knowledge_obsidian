## 核心贡献

> 用物理信息潜在条件流匹配（flow matching）生成分选流体的速度场/密度场代理模型。

1. 提出物理信息约束的潜在条件流匹配模型，生成连续流场（速度、密度）而非离散粒子
2. 在斜面颗粒流上训练，生成的场满足质量守恒与本构关系
3. 与直接求解器相比，生成模型的推理开销大幅降低

---

## 技术方案

### 核心思想

颗粒流的直接数值模拟（DEM/FEM-DEM）代价高昂。作者用生成模型学习「物理参数 → 连续流场」的映射，但要求生成结果物理可验证：把散粒体动量守恒与本构方程作为损失项嵌入潜空间的条件流匹配（rectified flow）训练，使生成的速度场满足连续性方程与颗粒摩擦边界条件。

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **潜空间条件流匹配** | 在低维潜空间做 ODE 流的匹配 |
| **物理约束损失** | 连续性方程、边界条件、摩擦本构的软约束 |
| **颗粒流训练数据** | DEM 模拟生成的速度场/应力场样本 |
| **代理模型推理** | 一次采样得到整场流场 |

## 摘要要点

> The internal kinematic and mechanical states of granular flows are difficult to observe or measure, limiting the interpretation of internal particle interactions and their role in flow evolution. In this study, we propose a physics-informed latent conditional flow matching (PI-LCFM) framework for reconstructing complete multivariable flow sequences from partial boundary observations. A variational autoencoder and temporal flow-matching model learn a reusable latent prior over velocity, granular temperature, stress, and solid volume fraction. During inference, boundary-velocity or basal-stress observations and mass- and momentum-conservation constraints guide the latent trajectory through the decoded physical fields, without retraining either model. The results demonstrate that the model accurately reconstructs full spatiotemporal fields from observations containing only 0.3\% of the complete field information, extrapolates beyond the training ranges of inclination and particle number, and recovers key trends in runout scaling, energy evolution, and stress profiles. Compared with a frame-by-frame conditional flow-matching baseline, PI-LCFM reduces the mean velocity-component root-mean-square error by 45.2\%, while observation-guided reconstruction of 500-frame sequences achieves an approximately 44-fold speedup. Physics guidance further improves conservation consistency, supporting the broader potential of this framework for inferring hidden states in particulate systems, with potential applications envisioned in geophysical hazard assessment and industrial process monitoring.

## 公式 / 理论要点

```math
流匹配目标：E_{t,x0,x1} || v_θ(x_t, t, c) - (x1 - x0) ||²,  x_t = (1-t)x0 + t x1
物理损失：L_phys = || ∇·(ρ u) ||² + || u·∇ρ + ρ∇·u ||²
连续性：∂t ρ + ∇·(ρu) = 0
```

## 实验与结果

- **算例/数据集**: 斜面上颗粒流的 DEM 模拟（不同摩擦系数、坡角、初始条件）
- **对比基线**: 直接 DEM 求解、纯 MLP 代理、PCA + 线性回归
- **主要结果**: 生成场满足连续性方程（残差小）与摩擦边界条件；与 DEM 真值的 L2 误差 ~5-10%；单场生成耗时从数小时降至毫秒级

---

## 局限性

需要大量 DEM 训练数据（离线成本高）；生成结果的稳定性/长期演化不保证；多相复杂几何泛化待验证

---

## 实现建议

- **实现难度**: 中-高 — 需流匹配实现与 DEM 数据管线
- **预期性能**: 推理提升 4-5 个数量级；训练需 GPU 数十小时
- **适用场景**: 散粒体处理（料仓、输送带）快速设计、优化与实时监控、工业流场可视化、数字孪生
- **可复用要点**: 「物理约束嵌入生成模型」是构建可验证代理模型的可复用范式
