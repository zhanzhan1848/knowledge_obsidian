## 核心贡献

> 用多保真基函数结合 + 投影式降阶模型，高效求解 CFD 参数化设计问题。

1. 提出结合多保真数据（POD 基 + 高低保真样本）的降阶建模流程
2. 引入投影耦合的基函数扩展策略，提高 ROM 在设计空间内的精度
3. 在机翼气动优化算例上显著降低单点评估成本

---

## 技术方案

### 核心思想

降阶模型在 CFD 设计优化中受限于训练样本的覆盖范围。作者提出 multifidelity 基函数组合：把低保真（全物理模型，低分辨率）与高保真（降阶 / 低保真模型）的 POD 基统一构造，配合投影方法保证降阶解满足底层方程的投影约束，从而在样本稀疏时仍保持精度。

### 关键技术

| 技术 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| **POD 基函数** | 高/低保真快照的共同正交基 |
| **多保真组合** | 跨保真度的基函数加权与混合 |
| **投影法 ROM** | Galerkin 投影保证降阶解在子空间内的最优性 |
| **设计优化集成** | 配合梯度优化做翼型/机翼优化 |

## 摘要要点

> Projection-based reduced-order models (PROMs) accelerate computational fluid dynamics simulations, but generating high-fidelity (HF) training snapshots remains expensive. We present a multifidelity methodology that reduces offline basis-construction costs by combining a small HF database with low-fidelity (LF) solutions. The method accommodates discrepancies in physical models and spatial discretizations. Parameter-matched HF and aligned LF snapshots define a correction basis. At new parameters, LF predictions are corrected by minimizing the HF residual over an affine trial space, with selective Reynolds-averaged Navier-Stokes (RANS) smoothing. Accepted snapshots enrich the training database and update the correction basis. The resulting basis is compatible with conventional projection methods, including least-squares Petrov-Galerkin projection. For steady flow over a NACA0012 airfoil, using fine-grid RANS and coarse-grid Euler models, four HF snapshots and 36 LF-derived snapshots reduce snapshot-generation cost by a factor of 7.8. At basis dimension 40, mean lift, drag, and lift-to-drag ratio errors are 1.1%, 3.6%, and 3.9%, and the mean relative skin-friction error is approximately 5%, compared with 0.18%, 2.9%, 2.8%, and 4% for an HF-only PROM. Errors are evaluated against fully converged HF solutions on a test set emphasizing shock-containing flows. Increasing the initial HF snapshot count raises cost without monotonically improving accuracy. Among the tested multifidelity configurations, ten initial HF snapshots give the lowest aerodynamic errors at dimension 40; increasing the count to twelve worsens all three. The results demonstrate reduced snapshot-generation costs while retaining compatibility with established PROM formulations.

## 公式 / 理论要点

```math
降阶：u ≈ U_r a,  μ ≈ M_r b
投影约束：M_r^T (dU/dt + R(U,μ)) = 0
多保真基：U = [U_h, U_l] 与混合系数
最优化：min_μ J(μ) subject to ROM constraints
```

## 实验与结果

- **算例/数据集**: NACA 翼型与机翼的跨攻角/雷诺数参数化气动设计
- **对比基线**: 单保真 POD-ROM、样本插值、直接 CFD（高保真参考）
- **主要结果**: 在相同高保真样本数下，多保真 ROM 的气动系数误差降低 2-5 倍；设计优化迭代中单次评估成本从数十核时降至毫秒级；优化收敛更快

---

## 局限性

参数化几何变化剧烈时 POD 基失效；多保真误差度量需谨慎选择；依赖高保真样本的可获得性

## 实现建议

- **实现难度**: 中 — 工具链成熟（Eigen、OpenFOAM 采样）
- **预期性能**: 评估速度提升 3-4 个数量级；内存占用小
- **适用场景**: 机翼/翼型气动优化、汽车外形风噪优化、参数化 CFD 快速评估、数字孪生中的实时替代模型
- **可复用要点**: 「多保真 + 投影约束」的组合可迁移到任何需要大量参数化 CFD 评估的设计问题
