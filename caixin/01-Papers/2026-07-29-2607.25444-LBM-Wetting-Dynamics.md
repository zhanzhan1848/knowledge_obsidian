## 核心创新点

### 1. 局部几何润湿边界条件
- 针对保守Allen-Cahn格子Boltzmann框架
- 通过局部重建壁面法向量和供体流体外推构造幽灵相场值
- 幽灵节点润湿更新是局部的、几何一致的

### 2. 质量守恒
- 通过单独的全局体积校正控制相场质量
- 线程安全，适合大规模并行实现

### 3. 验证案例
- 静态接触角测试
- 短时液滴铺展
- 疏水表面撞击
- 重力驱动通过尖锐孔口的运动

## 摘要原文

> In this work we propose a local geometric wetting boundary condition for a conservative Allen-Cahn based lattice Boltzmann framework. The prescribed contact angle is imposed through ghost phase-field values constructed from a locally reconstructed wall normal and a donor-fluid extrapolation. The ghost-node wetting update is local, geometrically consistent, and compatible with thread-safe large-scale implementations.
